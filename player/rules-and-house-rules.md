# House Rules

> These are the table rules for this campaign. They contain **no story secrets**. The DM applies them every session. Where a rule here differs from the 2024 *Player's Handbook* (PHB) or *Dungeon Master's Guide* (DMG), **this file wins.**

---

## 1. The core rules

- **Edition:** D&D 2024 (the 2024 PHB, DMG and Monster Manual), with the changes below.
- **Leveling:** milestone. You level when a big story event happens, never by experience points or session count.
- **Pacing target:** about 35–40 sessions from level 1 to level 20. Levels 1–4 take about one session each. Levels 5–16 take about two sessions each. Levels 17–20 come with the biggest story moments. There are no catch-up rushes. If a chapter runs long, the next one doesn't get squeezed.

## 2. Rule changes (carried over from the last campaign)

| Rule | How it works here |
|---|---|
| **Counterspell** | Uses the **2014** version. Reaction, when you see a creature within 60 feet casting a spell. A spell of 3rd level or lower simply fails. For a higher-level spell, make an ability check with your spellcasting ability. The DC is 10 + the spell's level. If you cast Counterspell with a higher slot, any spell of that slot's level or lower fails automatically. |
| **Mirror Image** | Uses the **2014** version. Three illusory duplicates. When a creature targets you with an attack, roll a d20. With 3 duplicates left, 6 or higher hits a duplicate instead of you; with 2, 8 or higher; with 1, 11 or higher. A duplicate has AC 10 + your Dexterity modifier and is destroyed when hit. The spell doesn't work against creatures that can't see or that see through illusions (blindsight, truesight). |
| **Long rests** | There's **no required gap** between long rests. If you can find 8 safe hours, you can take one, even if you took one earlier that day. (The DM may rule that a place isn't safe.) |
| **Potions** | **Drinking a potion is a bonus action** (yours or a willing ally's within reach). Feeding a potion to someone else is also a bonus action. |
| **Money** | **All coin counts as gold.** Everything is priced and tracked in gold pieces (gp). Small purchases under 1 gp are bundled and rounded to the nearest gold, or ignored when trivial. |
| **Prices** | Follow the **2024 DMG**. Magic items by rarity: **Common 100 gp · Uncommon 400 gp · Rare 4,000 gp · Very Rare 40,000 gp · Legendary 200,000 gp.** Consumables (potions, scrolls) cost **half**. Magic weapons and armor add the cost of the base item. Gear follows the 2024 PHB. Shops can have personalities and haggle, but the starting price is the book price. |

## 3. Ash's subclass: Thief-Swashbuckler (house rule)

> Rogue subclass, chosen at **level 3**. It mixes the 2024 Thief with the 2014 Swashbuckler. It **does not** include Rakish Audacity or Supreme Sneak.

### Level 3: Fast Hands
When you take the **Cunning Action** bonus action, you can choose one of these extra options:
- **Sleight of Hand.** Make a Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check to pick a lock or disarm a trap with Thieves' Tools, or to pick a pocket.
- **Use an Object.** Take the Utilize action, or take the Magic action to use a magic item that needs that action.

### Level 3: Second-Story Work
You've spent your life on rooftops.
- **Climber.** You have a **Climb Speed equal to your Speed** (30 feet for Ash). You don't need a check to climb ordinary walls, rooftops or ropes.
- **Jumper.** You use your **Dexterity** instead of your Strength to work out how far you can jump. (For Ash: long jump up to 18 feet with a 10-foot run-up, high jump up to 7 feet. Halve both from a standing start.)

### Level 3: Fancy Footwork
During your turn, if you make a melee attack against a creature, **that creature can't make opportunity attacks against you** for the rest of your turn.

### Level 9: Panache (replaces Supreme Sneak)
As an action, make a **Charisma (Persuasion)** check contested by a creature's **Wisdom (Insight)** check. The creature must be able to hear you, and you must share a language.
- **If the creature is hostile:** on a success, it has **disadvantage on attack rolls against anyone but you**, and **can't make opportunity attacks against anyone but you**. This lasts 1 minute, or until one of your companions attacks it or hits it with a spell, or until you're more than 60 feet apart.
- **If the creature isn't hostile:** on a success, it's **charmed** by you for 1 minute and treats you as a friendly acquaintance. The charm ends at once if you or your companions harm it.

### Level 13: Use Magic Device
You can use magic items others can't.
- **Attunement.** You can attune to up to **four** magic items at once.
- **Requirements ignored.** You ignore class, species and level requirements on magic items.
- **Charges.** Whenever you use a property of a magic item that spends charges, roll a d6. On a 6, you use it without spending any charges.
- **Scrolls.** You can use any Spell Scroll, using **Intelligence** as your spellcasting ability. Cantrips and 1st-level spells always work. For a higher-level spell, you first make an Intelligence (Arcana) check (DC 10 + the spell's level). On a failure, the scroll crumbles to dust.

### Level 17: Thief's Reflexes
You get **two turns during the first round of any combat.** Your first turn is at your normal initiative. Your second is at your initiative minus 10. You can't use this if you're surprised.

## 4. Ash's familiar: Bartholomew ("Bart") (house rule)

Ash learned **Find Familiar** through his **Magic Initiate (Wizard)** feat. He can cast it once per long rest without a spell slot. Bart was already summoned before the campaign began.

**The standard rules (2024 Find Familiar), used as written:**
- Bart is a spirit in animal form. His default form is an **owl**.
- He **speaks only telepathically, to Ash alone**, within 100 feet. Nobody else hears him. Ash passes on Bart's comments if he wants to ("He says you're an idiot").
- He **rolls his own initiative and acts on his own turn.** He **can't attack**, but he can take other actions, most often **Help** to give Ash **advantage** on his next attack against an enemy next to Bart.
- As an owl he has **Flyby**: he doesn't provoke opportunity attacks when he flies out of an enemy's reach.
- As a bonus action, Ash can see through Bart's eyes and hear what he hears until the start of Ash's next turn.
- Ash can deliver a touch spell through Bart.
- If Bart drops to 0 hit points, he vanishes. Recasting the spell brings him back (1 hour as a ritual, or the free cast). The spell consumes 10 gp of incense each time.
- Recasting Find Familiar while Bart is around changes his form for the day to another allowed familiar form. That uses the free cast, or a ritual, and the incense.

**House rule: Bart's free shape-swap.**
- **Once per day** (it resets on a long rest), **at no cost**, Bart can blink into his pocket dimension and come back as a **different familiar form** for **up to 1 hour**. Then he blinks away and returns in his form for the day (normally the owl).
- **Not during combat.**
- **Bart chooses the form.** Ash tells him the goal ("get under that door," "listen in on the meeting"), and Bart picks the best shape for the job. He's likeliest to pick a **spider** or a **rat**.
- Uses the stat block for the chosen familiar form.

**Personality (played by the DM):** Bart is snarky. He makes fun of Ash's brothers, and he's lippy with Ash too. His jokes are funny unless you're the target. Smart enemies will notice the owl making Ash dangerous and may go after him. He has 1 hit point.

## 5. Feats and features worth remembering

- **Lucky (Ash):** Ash has **Luck Points equal to his proficiency bonus** (2 at level 1) and regains them on a long rest. He spends one to give himself advantage on a d20 test, or to give an attacker disadvantage when it attacks him. He decides **after seeing the roll** for his own tests.
- **Alert (Gideon):** proficiency bonus added to initiative, plus **Initiative Swap**. Right after initiative is rolled, Gideon can trade his initiative with a willing ally. In solo play, that's a big lever.
- **Weapon Mastery:** each character's mastery properties are on their sheet. Use them every attack.
- **Cunning Strike (Ash, level 5+):** when Ash deals Sneak Attack damage, the DM lists his Cunning Strike options before he rolls the Sneak Attack dice.

## 6. Dice at the table (summary; the play kit has the full rules)

- **You roll every d20 for Ash:** checks, saves and attacks. The DM tells you what to roll and the modifier, then stops.
- **The DM rolls everything else** in code, including all of Gideon's, Zeke's, Moss's, Bart's and every enemy's dice, and never states a number that wasn't rolled.
- If you say you're driving or don't have dice, the DM rolls everything until you say otherwise.
- **Take 10** whenever Ash isn't under pressure.
