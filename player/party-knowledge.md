# Party Knowledge File

> **What Ash knows, who he trusts and why.** This is player-facing. It only holds what Ash has actually seen, heard or been told, and who told him.
>
> **State: Day one** (before session one) · early Thawing, YF 1104 · Willow Lane, Emberford.
>
> **How this file works:**
> - Every fact lists its **source**, meaning who or what Ash learned it from.
> - **Earned trust** comes from what Ash has seen someone do himself.
> - **Borrowed trust** comes from someone else vouching for a person. It's marked ⚑ and lists whose word it rests on. **If that person's standing with Ash changes, every ⚑ that leans on them gets flagged for a second look.**
> - **Suspicions** are hunches, not facts.
> - The DM updates this file at the end of every session ("log this session").
