# Contests and Fight Rings (player rules)

Every city in Eyrland has ways to win a purse besides robbing someone. This page is the rules you need at the table. The DM has the courses, opponents and rivals.

---

## Contests: the basics

- **No magic.** No spells, no magic items, no familiars. Bart watches and heckles.
- **Class features that aren't magic count.** That's where Ash's training shows (see the table below).
- **Luck Points** work in contests. They come back on a long rest, so spending them is a real choice.
- **No taking 10.** It's a contest. The pressure's the point.
- **Harder towns, harder contests.** Big cities and later in the story mean higher DCs and bigger purses.

## How the pot works (most contests)

1. **Pay the entry fee** (about **2 gp** in Emberford now; much more in rich cities and later on).
2. **Everyone who passes splits the pot**: all the fees, minus the organizer's cut (usually 10–20%).
3. **If you're the only one who passes, you take it all.** If nobody passes, the pot carries over to next time, and the crowd doubles.
4. The passer with the cleanest run usually gets a small bonus.
5. **You can bet on yourself** with the bookies (almost everywhere).
6. You can't enter the same course twice in a month. Move on to the next course, or the next town.

## Obstacle courses

A course is **5 to 8 stations**, one quick roll each.
- **Drop stations** (water, mud, a net): fail and you're out.
- **Clock stations:** fail and you lose time. **Three lost-time marks and you're timed out.**

| Kind of station | Usual roll | Where Ash's training helps |
|---|---|---|
| Barrels, logs, swinging things | Acrobatics | Acrobatics +6 |
| Rope, net, pole, wall | Athletics **or** Acrobatics | **Second-Story Work (level 3):** climbing is automatic or easy |
| Gap jumps | Athletics, or just distance | **Second-Story Work:** he jumps with Dexterity (18 ft with a run-up) |
| Tilting plank, slick stone | Dexterity save | Rogue Dex save +6 |
| Sprints | Athletics or Constitution save | **Cunning Action (level 2):** Dash clears one sprint |
| Crawls under nets | Stealth or Acrobatics | **Stealth +8** |
| Snatch the ribbon | Sleight of Hand | Sleight of Hand +6; **Fast Hands (level 3)** helps |
| Spot the trick | Perception | **Perception +6** |
| Long hauls, heat, endurance | Constitution save | Ash's weak spot (+2) |

## Other contests you'll see

- **Knife-throwing:** five daggers at five targets, each harder than the last. Moving targets and trick throws in the finals. **Steady Aim (level 3)** helps when you don't move.
- **Archery:** short-range, long-range and moving-target rounds, then trick shots. A **shortbow** shoots badly past 80 feet. A **longbow** reaches farther, but Ash isn't trained with one.
- **Lock-picking:** five locks against a sand-glass, each harder than the last. The last one may be trapped. Finals can be head-to-head or blindfolded. Ash's **Thieves' Tools +6**.
- **Rooftop races, climbing races, mast climbs:** Ash's best events once he has a climb speed.
- **The Ribbon Pull:** pickpocketing ribbons off marks in bell-sewn coats. Ring a bell and you're out.
- **Shell-game tournaments:** hustlers run the cups for each other. Every sharp cheats. Catching one is a skill too.
- **Escape-artist contests:** manacles, a sack and a crate. Fastest out wins.
- **Show contests:** juggling, knife-catching, tumbling, judged by the crowd.

## Fight rings: the formats

**Rules in every ring:**
- **No magic of any kind.** No spells (a paladin's **smite** is a spell), no holy auras, no animal shapes, no magic items and no familiars.
- **Training that isn't magic is allowed:** Cunning Action, Sneak Attack (with practice blades), Cunning Strikes, Uncanny Dodge, Fancy Footwork, Lucky, feats and weapon masteries.
- **No real armor.** Fighters wear padded jackets, so **armor class is 10 + Dexterity.** Quick fighters are hard to hit.
- **Non-lethal.** Drop to 0 and you're out cold, not dead.
- **You pick the format** from what's on that night's card.

| Format | How you win | Who it favors |
|---|---|---|
| **Bare-Knuckle** | Knock him down, or win the most rounds of three | Strong men. Fighters trained in Acrobatics may punch with Dexterity instead (1 + Dex damage). |
| **Wasters (Touches)** | **First to 3 touches** (or most after five rounds). Any hit is a touch. A hit with **Sneak Attack** or a **critical** counts as **2**. Knocking him prone with the hit adds 1. No more than 2 touches in one turn. | **Quick, accurate fighters.** This is Ash's format. Practice blades keep his weapon masteries and Sneak Attack. |
| **The Plank (Ring-Out)** | Push him off the plank or beam. Take a hit and you may lose your balance. | Whoever keeps his feet. Acrobatics, Dex saves and footwork matter as much as strength. |
| **Last-Standing** | Last fighter on his feet in a pit of 4–8 takes the pot | Clever fighters. Let the big men knock each other down, but don't hang back too long. |

**Purses** run about 20–100 gp in Emberford rings now, more later and in bigger cities. Entry is about 5 gp. **The ring healer** patches you up between bouts for a few gold.
