# Bramblecog: Side-Activity Menu (SEALED — DM ONLY)

> **Bramblecog** (aka Bramble Cog). A gnome town of about 1,800 in the Hobbland hills on the Hobb River. The best clockwork in Eyrland.
> **Government:** a **gnome lottery**. Every year at **the Drawing** (1 Leaffall), the five-member town council is chosen by lot from every adult resident, using a giant clockwork drum called **the Fairest Machine**. You cannot refuse. **This year's Lot-Mayor: Dabble Quickwhistle** (NPC-073), a nervous cheesemaker who did *not* want this. **Law:** **Constable Fenna Hobbs** (NPC-074) and a flock of clockwork alarm geese.
> **When visited:** optional. The likeliest windows are the Ch8–9 minor-gate raids (EVT-14: the millpond is hit the same week as the Drift; Coble Tull brings the news) or a Zeke-driven detour for the Contraption Fair. **Tier: T2 (T3 notes).**
> **DM notes (world/03):** the millpond is a minor Wellgate. A badger-folk refugee family is hiding in the Burrow Rows, and the gnomes are keeping them secret.

---

## 1. The town at a glance

- **Look:** green hills with round doors dug into them (the Burrow Rows); a river lined with waterwheels of every size, all turning something; brass weathervanes shaped like animals that point the wrong way on purpose; sheep everywhere; a village green with a bandstand and the Fairest Machine under a little copper dome.
- **Sound:** gears, sheep, puns, and a goose honk that's slightly too regular (clockwork).
- **Feel:** whimsy with craft. Gnomes are brilliant and know it. Not silly-stupid: everything is a joke and everything works.
- **Districts:** **the Millwheels** (water-wheel workshops), **Cog Market**, **the Drawing Green**, **the Burrow Rows**.
- **Custom:** every gnome greets a stranger with a riddle. Answering earns a smile; a better riddle back earns a cup of cider. Failing earns nothing worse than a pat on the arm.

## 3. Festivals and contests

### 3.1 The Cheese Roll (1 Blossom)
A wheel of Hobbland Blue is rolled down Pudding Hill (steep, grassy, with a hedge halfway). Everyone chases it. First to the bottom with the cheese wins the cheese and the year's bragging rights.
**The chain:** **Dexterity (Acrobatics) DC 13** (the start) · **Strength (Athletics) DC 14** (the hedge) · **Dexterity (Acrobatics) DC 15** (the last slope, where everyone falls) · contested **Dexterity** at the bottom against the leader (+5). A fall on any link: 1d4 bludgeoning and a roll to the bottom (you're out, and filthy).
**Prize:** the cheese (2 gp, priceless), a garland, and the town's love.

### 3.2 The Contraption Fair (the week before the Drawing, late Harvest)
**Zeke's dream.** An invention contest: build something in a day and show it to three gnome judges.
**The chain (Zeke, or anyone with tools):** **Design** (Intelligence (Arcana or Investigation) DC 15) · **Build** (Tinker's Tools or Smith's Tools DC 15; without proficiency, Dex DC 17) · **Sabotage check** (the rival, §8: **Perception DC 15** to catch it the night before; failure means link 4 is at disadvantage) · **Demonstration** (Charisma (Performance) DC 14; Ash can do it instead of Zeke with **advantage**, because Ash can sell anything).
**3–4 successes:** first prize, **150 gp** (T2) or **500 gp** (T3), a gnome-made **Clockwork Amulet** or **Immovable Rod** (judges' choice), and Pernella offers Zeke an apprenticeship ("any summer"). **2:** a ribbon and 25 gp. **0–1:** something explodes; everyone cheers.
**Zeke discovery moment:** if the party's here, this is a natural place for one of Zeke's "I didn't know I could do this" moments (`campaign/05` §6): whatever he builds *hums in tune* with Ash's tune. The gnomes hear it. Nobody can explain it.

### 3.3 The Drawing (1 Leaffall)
The Fairest Machine draws the new council. Betting on it is illegal and universal (§6). A comedy set piece: if a Fenwick (or Moss, who's technically resident nowhere) is in town, the Machine's chute spits out a slip with his name on it (the Machine draws visitors' names sometimes as a joke built into it by its maker; the gnomes laugh until they cry). A visitor can't serve. It's still in the town ledger forever.
**Thing worth protecting (world/03):** someone is trying to steal the Machine's key to rig the lottery. Stopping them is the job (§7).

### Skill contests (`00-index.md` §11; CHG-21)
- **The Clockwork Gauntlet** (Gauntlet, Hard, T2; every market day): the gnomes' moving course. Every station's DC is **+1** (the obstacles move), and every course has a **Perception ⏱** station (spot the gear-gap before it closes, DC 14) and a **timing ⬇** station (Dex save DC 15: the pendulum). **Entry 10 gp**; the Fairest Machine draws the station order by lottery each run, which delights everyone. Rival: a Gearwright cousin's apprentice racer (+7, cheats with a wind-up boot that jams 1 time in 3).
- **Blindfold Lock** (lock-picking, T2): gnome locks by feel, DCs **15 / 16 / 17 / 18 / 20**, the last one a puzzle lock that needs **Intelligence (Investigation) DC 15** before it can be picked. **Entry 10 gp, prize 120 gp** and a gnome-made pick that hums near the right pin (+1, non-magical; it's a tuning fork). Zeke loves this contest and can't pick a lock to save his life.

## 4. Clockwork bouts (Bramblecog's fight ring)

**Ring formats (`00-index.md` §5.1, CHG-19):** the gnomes' clockwork bouts are fought in **Touches** (the automatons are Dex fighters: ring AC 14–15) or **Ring-Out on a rotating gear-wheel** (each round the wheel turns: Dex save DC 12 or prone). No magic in any format: no smites, no spells, no Wild Shape, no familiars. Dexterity fighters can win here.

**Bring a fighting automaton.** Bouts on a chalk circle on the Drawing Green; the owner stands outside the circle and "drives" with a whistle. Three rounds; knock the other automaton out of the circle or down to 0.
- **Entering:** buy a bout-automaton from Pernella (**50 gp**, T2) or build one (Contraption Fair link 2, DC 15; one day).
- **Driving:** the owner rolls the automaton's attacks and checks using **the owner's Intelligence** modifier instead of the automaton's own (gnome rules). Zeke (+4 Int at L1, +5 from L4) is the natural driver. Ash can drive with Cha instead, by "talking to it" (house tradition: one entrant a year may; the crowd adores it).
- **Purses:** 20 gp (T2 undercard), 80 gp (main), 300 gp (the Fair's championship).

### BOUT-AUTOMATON (a basic one)
*Small Construct, Unaligned*
**AC** 15 · **Initiative** (owner's Int) · **HP** 33 (6d6 + 12)
**Speed** 25 ft.
STR 14 (+2) · DEX 12 (+1) · CON 14 (+2) · INT 3 · WIS 10 · CHA 1
**Immunities** poison, psychic; charmed, frightened, poisoned · **CR** 1
*Tuned for a Bramblecog bout (T2).*
- ***Driven.*** Uses its owner's Int modifier for attacks and checks; follows whistled orders (no action for the owner).
- ***Round-2 Turn: "Wind It Up."*** At the start of round 2, the owner may spend their action winding it: the automaton gains **+10 ft speed and an extra Slam** this round.
- ***Slam.*** *Melee:* +owner's Int + 2, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 1d8 + 2 bludgeoning.
- ***Shove-Spring (Recharge 5–6).*** One automaton within 5 ft: **DC 12 Str save** or pushed 15 ft (out of the circle, probably).

### "OLD THUMPER" (the reigning champion automaton)
*Medium Construct, Unaligned* · **AC** 17 · **HP** 70 · **Speed** 30 ft. · **CR** 4 · *Tuned for a T2 championship bout.*
Driver: **Granny Wix Thimbleton** (no ID; Tier 3), a 200-year-old gnome who has driven Old Thumper for sixty years.
- ***Multiattack.*** Two Slams (+7, 1d10 + 3).
- ***Steam Vent (Recharge 5–6).*** 10-ft cone, **DC 14 Con save**, 3d6 fire (half on success); blinds the target's driver's view (the driver has disadvantage on checks for the automaton) until the end of the next round.
- ***Round-3 Turn: "Granny Whistles."*** If losing, Granny whistles a tune only Old Thumper knows: it gets one extra turn immediately. (If Ash whistles it back, Old Thumper hesitates — **Performance DC 15**: it loses that turn. Granny laughs so hard she has to sit down.)

## 5. Street fights

Gnomes don't. If a stranger starts one, the alarm geese arrive in round 1 (a Swarm of geese: **DC 12 Dex save** each round or knocked prone) and Constable Hobbs in round 2, who confiscates everyone's boots overnight. That's the punishment. It works.

---

## 6. Betting

**On the Drawing:** illegal and universal. Odds on each resident are posted in chalk inside the Merry Wheel's privy door. **On the Cheese Roll, the Contraption Fair, clockwork bouts:** legal. Odds per `00-index.md` §4. Gnome bookies pay in cheese if they run short. A cheese is worth what the gnome says it's worth.

## 8. Rivals

- **Fizzwick Brassbottom** (NPC-332; aka Fizwick, Brassbotom): a rival gnome inventor, last year's unwilling council member, who sabotages Zeke at the Contraption Fair (link 3) because Zeke is "a big-folk amateur." If Zeke beats him fairly and then asks how his own machine worked, Fizzwick melts and becomes Zeke's pen-friend for life (letters arrive by clockwork bird; Ch17 callback: he sends a crate of gadgets to the alliance).
- **Old Thumper** (§4): not a rival, a legend.
