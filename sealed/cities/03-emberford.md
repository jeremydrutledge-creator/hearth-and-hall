# Emberford: Side-Activity Menu (SEALED — DM ONLY)

> Everything Ash can do in his own city when the story gives him a free hour: festivals, contests, the Bucket, street fights, betting, the odd-jobs board, things worth stealing and rivals. Shops are in `01-home-city-shops.md`; the Row is in `02-enchanters-row.md`; the shared rules (haggling, the Street Hustle, betting odds, ring math, heists, fences) are in `00-index.md`.
> **The chapter wins.** Where a chapter file writes its own version of something here (a named Bucket opponent, a Bargerace, the Lamp-Climb), run the chapter's version for that scene. This file supplies everything around it, and every return visit.
> **Tiers:** T1 L1–4 · T2 L5–10 · T3 L11–16 · T4 L17–20. Emberford is the one place with all four tiers.

## 2. Contests (each written as a quick chain of checks)

### 2.1 The Bargerace (Highwater Fair, 16 Highwater)
**Ch4 §4 is the full version.** For a return in YF 1105 (if the campaign is in Emberford in Highwater of the second year, which it may not be), use this:
- Team of four poling a barge through the Seven Bridges. Entry 5 gp a team. Prize **100 gp and a guild ribbon** (T1) / **300 gp** (T2+, the Bargemen's Guild raises it in an election year).
- **The chain (six links, each a team check: one roll by the best member, others may Help):** the Start (Strength (Athletics) DC 13) · First Bridge (Wisdom (Vehicles (water)) or Survival DC 13) · the Narrows (Dexterity (Acrobatics) DC 14, a rival barge rams) · Market Bridge (Charisma (Intimidation) DC 13 to make a rival give way, or Athletics DC 15 to push through) · the Ferry Gap (Vehicles (water) DC 15) · the Sprint (Athletics DC 14).
- **Score:** 5–6 successes: win. 4: second (25 gp). 3 or fewer: also-rans, and the crowd cheers anyway.
- **Rivals on the water:** Rook's Magpies' barge (+4 to their checks; they cheat at the Narrows), the Bargemen apprentices (+5), Swift and Sons (+3; they sabotage the night before: **Perception DC 14** the morning of to notice a cracked pole).

### 2.2 The Lamp-Climb (Ford Festival)
**Ch11 SC 11-B is the full version.** Practice pole (30 ft, lightly greased) any day on Guildhall Isle: **Athletics or Acrobatics DC 15**, advantage with Ash's climb speed; tap the practice lamp for 5 gp from a bored Lamplighters' clerk. Festival pole (60 ft, heavily greased): three links, **DC 15 / 17 / 19**, advantage with climb speed; one fall resets one link; two falls and you're out. **Prize 50 gp and a free drink in every tavern for a year** (Peg honors it; nobody else does).

### 2.3 Knife Wall (Market Steps, any day)
Five throws at painted targets, **AC 12, 14, 16, 18, 20** (Ch1); 1 sp a try (ten tries for 1 gp). Weekly champion (4+ hits) **10 gp** (20 gp during the Fair).
- **Weekly board names to beat** (roll a d4 for this week's best): 1, Joss Kettering (a sailor, 3 hits); 2, Rook (3 hits, and he'll be watching); 3, a Lyceum student called Oswin Fitch (2 hits, keeps trying); 4, nobody (an easy week, and it bothers Ash).
- **T3+:** the board brings in a "champion's wall" with AC 18–26 targets for 50 gp (the Lamplighters' Guild sponsors it, glumly).

### 2.4 The Riddle Table and Liars' Night (monthly)
- **The Riddle Table** (the Lyceum, old refectory): five riddles, **Intelligence DC 12 / 13 / 14 / 15 / 17** (+2 each in Act II, +4 in Act III). Zeke has advantage on the two about magic. Prize: a Common magic item (T1: Clockwork Amulet; T2: a 2nd-level scroll; T3: an Uncommon item from the Patient Owl's shelf, chosen by the judge). **Use real riddles at the table when the player wants to solve them himself.**
- **Liars' Night** (the Willow): best tall tale wins the tab. **Charisma (Deception or Performance)** contested by the room's best liar (Walt Finch +4; Peg +5 if Walt's Hushed; Brynja's true story DC 18 in Ch11). A real, good tale gets advantage. **Prize:** the tab (~3 gp T1, ~15 gp T2) and Peg's battered pewter **Liar's Cup** for a month.

### 2.5 Guild Games (last week of Harvest)
Outsiders enter as "the Fringe." Five events; any party member can take any event. **Prize:** 25 gp per event won; the team that wins most gets **100 gp** and the right to march first at the Ford Festival.
| Event | Check chain | Opponent best |
|---|---|---|
| Stone-lift | Athletics DC 13 / 16 / 19 | Big Tam Hollin's smiths (+7) |
| Archery | three ranged attacks vs AC 14 / 16 / 18 | the Watch (+6) |
| Wrestling | best of three contested Athletics | the Bargemen (+6) |
| Tug-of-war | team Athletics, best of three, DC rises 13 / 15 / 17 | the Masons (+7; they cheat with a buried stake: Perception DC 15) |
| The Clerk's Race | sums: Intelligence DC 13 / 15 / 17 | the Merchants (+5). Zeke's event. |

### 2.6 The Midsummer Swim
Across the Fordwater and back. Three **Athletics DC 15** checks, best of three wins; **10 gp and a garland**. (Ch7: Lark wants to try. Don't let her near still water. She knows.)

### 2.7 Turn of Light (Ash's birthday)
No contest. At sunset, floating lamps. Each person makes a wish, and it's bad luck to say it aloud. Ask the player what Ash wishes for, and don't comment. Write it in the Campaign Bible. **(Ch20 uses it.)** The Lane throws Ash a small party at the Willow if Peg can manage it; the Lane has done this every year since he was three.

## 4. The Bucket (Ma Garrow's fight ring)

### 4.1 The place and the rules
A drained cistern under a Wharves warehouse: a round brick pit with an iron rail, three tiers of crates for seats, lanterns on chains, the smell of river and sweat. **Ma Garrow** (NPC-024) sits on an upturned barrel by the ring cracking walnuts one-handed. Odds chalked on a slate by her nephew, **Little Ned Garrow** (NPC-310; huge, gentle, the chalk looks tiny in his hand).
- **Card nights:** Tenth-day (main card), Restday (undercard and, in Act I, beast bouts until Ma's code shuts them, Ch1/Ch7).
- **House rules:** no weapons that cut (cudgels and wasters allowed; `00-index.md` §5.1), no magic, no familiars in the pit (Bart heckles from the chains). "No killing on a Tenth-day" (Ma applies it every day for the undercard). Nonlethal.
- **Entry:** 5 gp. **Purses:** `00-index.md` §5.3 (T1 20/40/100 · T2 50/80–150/300 · T3 150/200–400/750 · T4 300/500–1,000/2,000).
- **Ma's code:** no kids. No beasts that talk (once Ash tells her, Ch1). No fixed fights she doesn't fix herself. She always finds out who took a dive.
- **Healer:** Ma's sister-in-law **Dot Garrow**, 5 gp × tier for half HP between bouts.

### 4.5 The Bucket by act
- **Act I:** beast bouts on Restdays until Ash tells Ma the "rabbit" is a child (Ch1); then never again.
- **Act II:** Ch7's beast-bout (Plish, the otter-folk refugee) is the last attempt, by Ma's nephew without her knowledge; shut it down (Ch7 window). Ch11's Festival Card. In Ch13, Ma's crew are the Wharves district's muscle (SC#4).
- **After Ch13:** the Bucket is half-flooded (the cistern leaks after Lantern Night). Ma runs bouts on a raft in the middle. Purses go **up** (people need the distraction). "Knee-deep rules": every fighter has **−5 ft speed**, and a prone fighter must make a **DC 12 Con save** or swallow water (lose their next bonus action).
- **Act III:** the Bucket becomes a recruiting place for the Ch17 alliance. Ma Garrow sends her best fighters (Reece, Ned) with the river muster if she's friendly (R-062 at "loyal").
