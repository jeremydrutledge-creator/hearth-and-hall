# Highmarch: Side-Activity Menu (SEALED — DM ONLY)

> **Highmarch** (aka High March, "Highmarsh," a common mishearing). The royal capital, about 50,000 people on a long chalk ridge above the Tamber below the Greymarsh.
> **Government:** a **monarchy with a weak crown.** **King Edric II** (NPC-069) is 14. The realm is run by the **Lord Chancellor, Anselm Crane** (NPC-070), careful and colorless, not wicked, who wants the crown strong again. **Law:** the **Crown Guard** (red cloaks), the realm's only real army (about 2,000 in the city).
> **When visited:** Ch17 (the alliance; the Royal Tourney; optional Fight 30: Calm remnants try to assassinate the boy-king) and Ch18 (the Greymarsh rises; Highmarch sees the Hushed dead walking out of Old Varrow). **Tier: T4.**
> **The chapter wins.** Ch17's window runs the Royal Tourney (jousting, the commoner's melee, archery; DC 19; 1,000 gp; a knighthood candidacy for Gideon). This file adds the shops, the Lowtown Pits, the rival knight's block, odd jobs, things worth stealing, and the fence.
> **The Tourney's date:** the **King's Tourney** is normally **1–7 Haymonth**. In YF 1105, the Lord Chancellor holds a special **Royal Tourney in Blossom** to gather the realm's knights for the alliance (Ch17). Both exist; this year's is in Blossom.

## 1. The city at a glance

- **Look:** old, proud, a little shabby. A chalk-white castle and a cathedral on the Ridge; banners on every tower, faded; the great **Kingsbridge**, a bridge so wide it's a town, with shops and houses along it; the **Lowtown** packed and flood-prone along the river; the **Tourney Fields** outside the walls with stands and lists.
- **Sound:** bells (the cathedral's, constantly), processions, heralds, Lowtown street-sellers.
- **Feel:** nobles who are poorer than Emberford merchants and resent it. Highmarch folk think Emberford folk are "lamp-drunk upstarts." Ash's Fringe accent is noticed everywhere (**disadvantage** on Persuasion with Ridge nobles unless he's in fine clothes *and* says nothing about the Fringe).
- **Districts:** **the Ridge** (castle, cathedral, palaces), **Kingsbridge** (the bridge town), **the Lowtown**, **the Tourney Fields**, **Scholars' Gate** (the Royal Archive and a stiff rival college to the Lyceum).
- **T4 fame:** by Ch17 the party is known. On the Ridge, they're "the Emberford hirelings" (a sneer). In the Lowtown, they're heroes. On Kingsbridge, they're good for business.

## 3. The Royal Tourney (Ch17) and the rival knight

**Ch17's window runs it.** The rival is **Sir Edmund Carrow** (NPC-343; aka Ed Carrow, Edmond Carro), a young, rich, brave and insufferable knight of the Ridge who sees Gideon as an upstart sellsword with an Emberford accent. He's not a villain. He's a boy who's never lost. Gideon's natural opponent in the joust and the melee.

### SIR EDMUND CARROW (the rival knight; joust or melee)
*Medium Humanoid (human), Lawful Neutral*
**AC** 21 (plate, shield +1) · **Initiative** +2 (12) · **HP** 150
**Speed** 30 ft. (mounted: 60 ft., warhorse)

|   | Score | Mod | Save |   | Score | Mod | Save |   | Score | Mod | Save |

**Skills** Animal Handling +6, Athletics +10, Performance +8 · **Senses** passive Perception 11 · **Languages** Common · **CR** 13 (PB +5)
*Tuned for party level 17 (Gideon alone in the melee: ring DPR ~33 with +25% HP per `00-index` §5.2 → ~165; 150 here because the joust is scored by passes, not HP): solo knight. Weak saves: **Dex +2, Cha +3**.*

**Traits**
- ***Tourney Rules.*** Blunted lances and swords; nonlethal; heralds score passes.
- ***Born in the Saddle.*** Advantage on checks and saves to stay mounted.
- ***Round-2 Turn: "For the Ridge."*** At the start of round 2, the Ridge stands and cheers him: **+2 to hit** for the rest of the bout. (If the Lowtown stands for Gideon, it cancels. Ash can make the Lowtown stand: **Performance DC 18** from the benches.)

**Actions**
- ***Multiattack.*** Three Blunted Longsword strikes (melee), or one Lance (joust).
- ***Blunted Longsword.*** *Melee:* +11, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) bludgeoning, nonlethal.
- ***Lance (joust pass).*** *Melee:* +11, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 18 (3d8 + 5) bludgeoning, and **DC 19 Str save** or unhorsed (loses the pass and the bout).

**Reactions**
- ***Shield Up.*** +3 AC against one attack.

**Tactics:** honest, aggressive, proud. If Gideon unhorses him and then helps him up in front of the Ridge, Edmund is shattered, then changed: he swears his lance to the alliance and to Gideon personally (a new edge if it matters; log an EVT).

**Joust as a chain (if the player prefers checks to a fight):** three passes. Each pass: contested **Strength (Athletics)** (Gideon +12ish at L17 vs Edmund +10), with **Animal Handling DC 17** to keep the horse straight first (failure = disadvantage on that pass). Best of three. Ch17's DC 19 × 3 is the solo version against the field.

## 4. The Lowtown Pits (illegal fight ring)

A flooded cellar under a Lowtown tannery, raided twice a year by the Crown Guard (always on the same two days; everyone knows which). **Boss: Black Tom Rudge** (NPC-346; aka Tommy Rudge), a scarred, genial crook who sells beer, fights, information and stolen goods. Ring rules (`00-index.md` §5) except **cutting weapons allowed** (first blood or yield; healers, sort of).
- **Purses (T4):** undercard 300 / main 500–1,000 / champion **2,000 gp**.
- **Opponents:** T4 Street Tough (solo, +40 HP) for the undercard; **the Bellman** (`03-emberford.md` §4.4, if he hasn't retired) as a visiting main card; the Pits' champion below.
- **Raid nights** (roll a d20 each visit; 1 = raid): the Crown Guard comes down the stairs. **Acrobatics or Stealth DC 18** to get out; failing means a night in the Ridge cells and a very awkward meeting with the Lord Chancellor in the morning (who knows exactly who they are and is amused, a little).

### "THE DROWNED MAN" (the Lowtown Pits champion)
*Medium Humanoid (human), Chaotic Neutral* · **AC** 18 · **Initiative** +4 · **HP** 140 · **Speed** 30 ft., swim 30 ft. · **CR** 13
*Tuned for party level 18 (Ash alone, ring DPR 33 + 32 round-one with Thief's Reflexes, HP 129).* A Greymarsh fisherman who survived a night in Old Varrow's water and came back strange (calm eyes, warm hands). Not Hushed. Something else; nobody knows; he doesn't either. (Color for Ch18: he can tell the party what the drowned streets were like, if they win his respect.)
- ***Multiattack.*** Three Gaff strikes: *Melee* +11, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 2d6 + 5 piercing (first-blood rules).
- ***Pull Under (Recharge 5–6).*** One creature within 10 ft: **DC 19 Str save** or pulled into the Pits' water and grappled (escape DC 19); the grappled creature can't breathe until free.
- ***Round-2 Turn: "The Cold."*** At the start of round 2, the cellar's water rises a foot (the river's in flood): all creatures without a swim speed are slowed (−10 ft speed).
- ***Round-3 Turn: "He Doesn't Breathe."*** If he's losing, he goes under and stays there; he gets advantage on his next attack from below.

## 5. Street fights

Kingsbridge brawls are a tradition between the Lowtown and the Ridge's servants. **Local trick: the bridge.** A creature shoved off the side falls 30 ft into the Tamber (3d6, then swimming; the crowd cheers whoever's left on top). The Crown Guard arrives in round 2 and arrests the Lowtown side only. Use the Street Tough block (T4).
