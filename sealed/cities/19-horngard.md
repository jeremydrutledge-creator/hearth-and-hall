# The Horngard and Cairn Answer (Mirrowen): Side-Activity Menu (SEALED — DM ONLY)

> **The Horngard** (aka Horn Guard, Horngarde). The minotaur highlands in Mirrowen's north, mirroring the Kettlehorns. **Cairn Answer** (aka Cairn Anser) is its capital: a hill-fort of humming standing stones, with the **Quiet Pens** below it, where Hushed calves stand silent in the grey grass.
> **Government:** herd-clans under a war-chief; at campaign start, **Brannoc's Regency** (NPC-008, "until the blight is cured"). After Ch15's turning, Brannoc is with the alliance (`chapters/act3/ch15.md`, ch16). **Law:** the herd-clans' warrior-code, which values protecting calves above all.
> **When visited:** Ch15 (`chapters/act3/ch15.md`: across the Horngard to Cairn Answer; side window after the turning: the Drum Challenge, the Bull-Leap, ritual wrestling, sitting with Ama, the other Pens, going home for Walt). **Tier: T3.**
> **Minotaurs are not brutes** (`world/05`): proud, loyal, musical (great drummers), farmers of high pastures. Play the herd-towns as hill farmers at war, tired and grieving.
> **The chapter wins.** This file adds the herd-towns' trade, the Drum Moot, the wrestling ring's block, odd jobs, and the highlands after the turning.

---

## 1. The highlands at a glance

- **Look:** high green-grey pastures (grey where greywilt lies heaviest), stone byres, round stone herd-halls with turf roofs and great drums hung by their doors, cairns on every hill; **Cairn Answer**'s ring of standing stones on its crag, humming (to Ash, painfully, before the turning; afterward, like a choir).
- **Sound:** drums that talk across valleys (each clan's rhythm is its name); wind; cattle bells (the minotaurs keep ordinary cattle and think it's funny that Tall Folk find that funny).
- **Feel:** a warrior people who'd rather be farming. Before the turning, every herd-town is short of young bulls (at war) and calves (in the Pens). After it, the herd-towns are loud again.
- **Custom: the drum-name.** A guest is given a short rhythm by the herd-hall's drummer, and it's drummed when they arrive anywhere in the Horngard from then on. (Ch15's Drum Challenge gives Ash his own.)

## 2. Trade (the herd-towns)

The Horngard has no shops in the Tall Folk sense. Each herd-hall trades from its stores. **Barter or coin; the price is what the hall's elder says, and it's fair.** Before the turning, a Tall Folk visitor is charged **double** ("for the lamps you burn"). After it, nothing for anything a guest needs.

## 3. Festivals and contests

### 3.1 The Drum Moot (Mirrowen's Seedtide)
Every herd-clan sends its drummers to Cairn Answer, and for three nights they drum the year's news across the highlands. **Ch15 is in Seedtide.** If the turning happens, **the Drum Moot is the night after**, and it's the first one in two years with calves at it. Play it as the Horngard's joy: drums all night, every clan's rhythm, then Ash's (if he won the Drum Challenge).
- **The Drum Challenge** (Ch15): Performance DC 17 / 19 / 21 against a herd-clan drummer.
- **The Bull-Leap** (Ch15): Acrobatics DC 19 to vault a charging bull's horns; DC 21 twice.
- **Ritual wrestling** (Ch15): Athletics contest (minotaur +9), three falls; Gideon's place.

### 3.2 The Calf-Run (after the turning)
The woken calves of the Quiet Pens are run up to the high pastures for the first time. Everyone runs with them. **No roll.** The calves are slow, and some are scared; Lark and Ama sing to them. It's the beat. Let it be long.

## 4. The wrestling ring (ritual wrestling)

A ring of stones on every herd-hall's green. Three falls; a fall is a pin for a count of three or a throw out of the ring. Opposed Athletics, or a full bout with this block.

*Tuned for party level 15–16 (Gideon alone, ring DPR ~30 with +25% HP per `00-index` §5.2 → ~110): solo grappler. Weak saves: **Dex +1, Cha +1**.*

**Traits**
- ***Ritual Rules.*** No weapons, no horns (horns are for war; using them in the ring is a disgrace). Falls, not damage, decide.
- ***Round-2 Turn: "The Drum."*** At the start of round 2, the clan's drum starts: the champion gains **advantage on Athletics** until the end of round 3, unless Ash (or anyone) drums back with **Performance DC 17** (then neither side has it).

**Actions**
- ***Multiattack.*** Two Grapples or one Throw.
- ***Grapple.*** *Melee:* +9, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 2d8 + 6 bludgeoning, nonlethal, and grappled (escape DC 17).
- ***Throw.*** One creature it's grappling: **DC 17 Str save** or thrown 15 ft and prone (out of the ring = a fall).
- ***Pin.*** A prone creature it's grappling: **DC 17 Str (Athletics) or Dex (Acrobatics)** at the start of the target's turn or it's a fall.

**Tactics:** honest strength; delighted by skill. If Ash wins by Acrobatics and Panache instead of strength, the whole herd-hall laughs until it cries.

## 5. Betting and street fights

**Betting:** on wrestling and the Bull-Leap, in cattle (one cow ≈ 10 gp). Ash should not win a cow. Ash will win a cow. (A cow can be given to a herd-family with no cattle left; it's a lovely beat.)
**Street fights:** none. Minotaurs don't brawl among themselves; they wrestle, by rules, in rings.
