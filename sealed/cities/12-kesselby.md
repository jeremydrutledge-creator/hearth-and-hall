# Kesselby: Side-Activity Menu (SEALED — DM ONLY)

> **Kesselby** (aka Kessel-by, Kesselbee). A mining village of grey slate cottages huddled below the **Kessel Workings**, the Enchanters' Guild's main glimmer quarry-cave, a half-day upriver from Emberford. Built around a minor Wellgate (`world/02` DM notes).
> **Government:** none of its own. Kesselby is a **Guild company town**: the Enchanters' Guild owns the cottages, the store and the Workings, and the **Mine-Warden** (Orla Venn, NPC-041) runs it with lamp-wardens. There's no council, no vote and no appeal. (It's on purpose the one place in Eyrland with no real government: a town that belongs to a company.)
> **When visited:** Ch12 (`chapters/act2/ch12.md`: S12.2, the Pick and Lantern, SC 12-A; the short side window has the brawl, the wager board, Sal's Davey and the dwarven warning stones). Possibly Ch5 (the saltbloom for Martha's remedy grows at Kessel; Mother Brine's job). **Tier: T3** (Act I visits at T1–T2 use the same menu with lower DCs).
> **The chapter wins.** This file adds the company store, the Pick and Lantern's fuller menu, odd jobs, the fence, a brawl block, and what Kesselby becomes after the gate is closed.

---

## 1. The village at a glance

- **Look:** slate cottages in rows, all the same, numbered on the doors; the Workings' great dark mouth above, ringed with glimmer lamps that never go out; a lift-head and spoil heaps; a single chapel with its windows boarded; a river landing with Guild barges. Everything has a faint blue dust on it.
- **Sound:** the lift-bell, carts, coughing, and under it all, to Ash, a hum that's wrong (the gate, strained).
- **Feel:** frightened and angry. Since Kessel's output doubled, half the men have "gone quiet" (Hushed) and the other half are drinking. People sit on their steps at evening and don't talk.
- **Act I vs Act II:** in an Act I visit (Ch5), it's poor and grim but not yet broken: the quiet ones are few, and people blame "the dust." By Ch12, it's a town in mourning that hasn't been allowed to say so.

## 2. Shops and services

### 2.1 The Company Store
**Keeper: Clerk Amos Pruett** (NPC-342; aka Amos Prewitt), a thin, nervous Guild clerk who knows the prices are criminal and has a sick mother in Emberford. Everything is sold **on company scrip** to miners (scrip is worth half a gold piece anywhere else) and at **PHB ×2** to outsiders. *"I don't set them. I only write them down."*
**Interior (painter):** a cold counter-room with a barred window, shelves of tinned food and candles and pick-heads, a ledger chained to the counter, a Guild lamp-sigil painted large on the wall behind him.

### 2.2 The Pick and Lantern (the brawl tavern)
**Keeper: Sal Pickett** (NPC-153; Ch12), a broad woman whose husband Davey went quiet in the summer. She hates the lamp-wardens. She'll draw the party a map of the Workings on the bar in spilled ale if they promise to bring Davey out.
**Interior (painter):** a long low room with a bar made from mine-cart rails, a wager board chalked on the back wall, miners' helmets hung from the beams by their chin straps (one for every man who's gone quiet, with his name), a fire, and an empty chair by it that nobody sits in.
- Ale 4 cp, cider (from Sal's sister in the Hobbland) 1 sp, stew 2 sp. A room upstairs, Poor (1 sp).
- **Ma Garrow's cousin** (Ch5 thread) sits on the step every evening at the same time, Hushed.

## 3. Contests and the wager board

**No festivals.** The Guild cancelled them three years ago ("lost shifts"). The miners keep one anyway:
- **Lamp-Out Night** (any night the lift-bell breaks): every lamp in the village is turned to the wall for an hour, and people talk in the dark, the only hour the lamp-wardens can't see their faces. A gentle scene, not a contest. If Ash is there, people tell him things (any one rumor, no roll; and **Insight** is unnecessary: they mean it).

**The miners' wager board** (Ch12): bets on the week's output, on who goes quiet next (grim; Sal hates it), on whether the Fenwicks will come back out (5 to 1).

**Pick-cart race** (when the wardens aren't looking): ride an ore cart down the spoil-heap track. **Dexterity (Acrobatics) DC 15** (stay in) · **Strength (Athletics) DC 15** (brake) · **Dexterity DC 17** (the bend). **Prize:** a round from everybody; the miners' trust (SC 12-A link 3 at advantage, as Ch12's brawl).

## 4. Brawls at the Pick and Lantern

**Ring formats (`00-index.md` §5.1, CHG-19):** the Pick and Lantern brawl is **Last-Standing** (fists and furniture). The wager board will also book a **Ring-Out** on the cart-bridge over the slurry ditch. No magic in any format: no smites, no spells, no Wild Shape, no familiars. Dexterity fighters can win here.

Ch12: miners vs off-duty lamp-wardens, nightly. Join (unarmed), bet, or end it (**Intimidation DC 17**, or Gideon's Abjure Foes on a table, which is funny). Reward 25 gp and the miners' trust.
Use the **Street Tough** block (`03-emberford.md` §4.3) at **T3** for miners, and this for the wardens:

### LAMP-WARDEN, OFF DUTY
*Medium Humanoid (human), Lawful Neutral*
**AC** 15 (in shirtsleeves; on duty they're masked, armored and use the chapter's blocks) · **Initiative** +2 · **HP** 60
**Speed** 30 ft.
STR 15 (+2) · DEX 14 (+2) · CON 14 (+2) · INT 11 · WIS 12 · CHA 10
**Skills** Athletics +5, Perception +4 · **Senses** passive Perception 14 · **Languages** Common · **CR** 5 (PB +3)
*Tuned for: T3 brawl, party level 12 (crew of 4 wardens + 2 miners on their side ≈ ordinary target 320 HP total with the toughs).*
- ***Lamp-Trained.*** Immune to the blinded condition from bright light.
- ***Cosh.*** *Melee:* +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 2d8 + 3 bludgeoning, nonlethal. **Two attacks.**
- ***Whistle (bonus action, once).*** Calls the on-duty patrol: they arrive at the end of round 3 (the brawl ends; anyone still fighting is arrested; the Guild fines are steep: 50 gp).
**Local trick: the helmets.** Once per round, a miner pulls a dead man's helmet down off the beams and throws it: one random warden **DC 14 Dex save** or is hit (1d6, and the room goes silent for a moment, and then roars).

## 9. Kesselby by act

| When | What it's like |
|---|---|
| **Act I (Ch5)** | Grim, poor, coughing. A few quiet ones. "It's the dust." |
| **Ch12, before the gate** | Half the men Hushed. Lamp-wardens everywhere. The Pick and Lantern is the only warm room. |
| **Ch12, after the gate is closed with the duet** | The recently Hushed wake over the next night, like people coming out of a long fever (Ch12 S12.8). Sal sends the Lane a barrel of cider every month for a year. |
| **After Ch13** | The Guild's gone or broken. The Workings are sealed. Kesselby's people stay: they own their cottages now (if the party pushes the Concord for it: **Persuasion DC 18** with Provost Teague in Ch13–17, or a public speech at the Ford Stone). The chapel bell is rung on the first night. |
| **Act III** | Kesselby's miners volunteer to dig for the alliance: sappers for Ch17–19 (a squad of 10 Street Toughs T4 with picks; they're excellent at collapsing tunnels on purpose). |
