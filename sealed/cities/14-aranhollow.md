# Aranhollow: Side-Activity Menu (SEALED — DM ONLY)

> **Aranhollow** (aka Aran Hollow, Arenhollow; Elvish **Ar-Aranel**, ar-AR-an-el). The elven tree city of about 8,000 in the Westwood, up the Brindle.
> **Government:** a **queen in the tree city.** **Queen Amarys** (NPC-068; aka Amaris, Ah-MAR-iss) has reigned four centuries from the **Crown Tree**, advised by the **Grove of Elders** (seven ancient elves who must agree before she can go to war). Gracious, guarded, carrying great sorrow. **Law:** the **Wardens of the Leaf** (forest rangers). Humans enter only by invitation through the **Leaf Weir**.
> **When visited:** Ch17 (the alliance: Amarys is moved only by proof that the seal can be held; she gives back Moss's last shrine-piece). **Tier: T4.**
> **DM notes (world/03):** SEC-15. Amarys was a young elf at the end of the Gate War and remembers the Bear dying. She knows the whole truth and chose neutrality. **The Queen's Mirror** is a Wellgate she keeps closed by her own will.
> **Elves here are not cruel or tricksy** (no fae-court feel, ever). They're slow, careful and deeply sad. Play them as old people who've been to too many funerals.

---

## 1. The city at a glance

- **Look:** green-gold light through leaves the size of shields; homes grown into living branches; hanging bridges of woven rope and living vine; quiet music from nowhere in particular; the **Crown Tree** (a vast oak-like tree with halls in its branches); at its foot, the **Queen's Mirror**, a still pool in a hollow, the only perfectly still water Ash has ever seen that doesn't feel wrong (it feels *held*).
- **Sound:** wind in leaves, a harp somewhere, birds that sing in rounds.
- **Feel:** centuries-old manners. Everything is done slowly, properly, and with meaning. Hurrying is rude. Bart is told, firmly, to be quiet in the Crown. He isn't.
- **Levels:** **the Roots** (guest halls, the market for humans), **the Bough Walks** (homes on living branches), **the Crown** (the queen's halls), **the Queen's Mirror**.
- **The Leaf Weir:** the checkpoint. **An invitation** is needed (Ch17 provides one, or the party earns it: a Warden's vouch, a message from Granny Tull, the Queen's own summons). Without one, the party waits at the Weir, politely, for as long as it takes.

## 2. Shops and services (the Roots market)

Elves don't haggle, and find it puzzling. Prices are fixed. **Paying without counting** is a courtesy, and the elf will tell you if you've overpaid (always, exactly).

### Skill contests (`00-index.md` §11; CHG-21)
- **The Canopy Run** (Gauntlet, Hard, T4): branch-paths, rope-bridges and a long glide on a vine. Stations at **DC 19–20**; elves (+12 Acrobatics) usually pass. Climb speed gives advantage on every climb and jump station. **No fee for invited guests; prize: the Queen's notice** (a token, and one favor, unspecified).
- **Silent Arrow** (archery, T4): before each shot the archer makes **Stealth DC 18** to move to a new mark unseen by the judges; a seen archer shoots with disadvantage. Then three arrows at **AC 19 / 21 / 23**. **Prize 1,500 gp and an elven arrow-case.** Ash's Stealth expertise makes this his best chance in Aranhollow.

## 4. The Duel of Blades

**Ring formats (`00-index.md` §5.1, CHG-19):** the Duel of Blades is the elves' **Touches** (first to three, waster blades, no shield). There's no other format; the elves don't brawl. No magic in any format: no smites, no spells, no Wild Shape, no familiars. Dexterity fighters can win here.

**No fight ring.** Elves find it vulgar. A formal **Duel of Blades** can be requested to settle a grievance: first blood, witnessed by a Warden, at dawn, by the Mirror. **Win** and the grievance is ended forever (elves keep that). **Lose** and you apologize, and you mean it.

## 5. Street fights

**None, ever.** A raised voice in the Bough Walks makes every elf in earshot stop and look at you, sadly, until you stop. (That's the whole consequence. It works on everyone except Bart.)

---

## 6. Betting

Frowned on, so done quietly by young elves at the Roots: on the Long Shot, the Silent Walk, and **how long the humans will last before they hurry** (the current record is four days; Gideon will break it; Ash won't).

## 8. Things worth stealing

**A leaf from the Crown Tree:** the gravest offense in Aranhollow. **Don't.** If Ash does, the Queen herself asks for it back, in front of everyone, without anger, and the alliance's Aranhollow commitment is gone (Ch17 ★3 needs two of three powers). There is no fence for anything stolen here, anywhere in Eyrland.
