# Hobb's Ferry: Side-Activity Menu (SEALED — DM ONLY)

> **Hobb's Ferry** (aka Hobbs Ferry, "the Ferry"). A village of about 600, half a day upriver-and-across from Emberford where the King's Road crosses the Hobb. First stop out of the city.
> **Government:** a hereditary ferry family. The **Ferrywarden** is always the eldest Pike: **Old Nan Pike** (NPC-075) owns the ferry, keeps the inn and settles disputes. Her grandson **Tobin Pike** (NPC-076) runs the ferry.
> **When visited:** Ch4 (Tobin as a Bargerace teammate, in Emberford), Ch7 (the night escape passes the ferry-rope at dawn, SC 7-A link 6), Ch8 (a stop on the way to the Glasswater, S8.1), and any trip down the King's Road. **Tier: T1–T2.**
> **The chapter wins** for anything it writes. Shared rules: `00-index.md`.

---

## 1. The village at a glance

- **Look:** a broad brown river, a ferry-raft on a rope as thick as a man's arm, a winch-house with a bell, a mill with a waterwheel that squeaks in a minor key, one inn, one chapel, a green with a maypole that's never quite straight, fifty cottages with thatch and geese.
- **Sound:** the ferry bell (one ring: coming across; two: wait; three: trouble), the mill, geese, Nan's voice.
- **Feel:** everybody knows everything by suppertime. Strangers are welcome until they're interesting.
- **Law:** Nan. There's no watch. Disputes go to the inn's back table on the evening they happen. Nan's punishment of choice: you work the ferry winch for a day, unpaid, with everyone watching.

**Nan Pike, as a fixer:** she knows everything that crosses. A **Persuasion DC 12** (or a pint and a story, no roll) gets one true thing about who's been on the King's Road in the last ten days. Lie to her once and she's deaf to you for a year.

## 3. Festivals and contests

### 3.1 Rope-Breaking (3 Thawing)
The ice ropes come off the ferry and the summer rope goes up. The whole village pulls. Then they eat for six hours.
**The Ferry-Pull (contest):** teams of four haul the ferry across against the current, timed. **The chain:** three team checks (the best member rolls, the rest Help): **Strength (Athletics) DC 13 / 15 / 14** (the middle is the current's strongest). **All three:** first place, **25 gp** and a barrel of cider. **Two:** second, 10 gp. The Pikes' team (+6) and the millers' team (+5) are the ones to beat.
**The Rope-Walk dare** (unofficial): walk the summer rope across the river before it's tightened. **Acrobatics DC 15 ×3**. Fall = a cold swim and the whole village's laughter. Ash will do this. Win: free drinks all night and Tobin's undying admiration.

### 3.2 The arm-wrestling table (any night)
The groove in the table is from forty years of elbows. Contested **Strength (Athletics)**, best of three. 1 gp a bout, house takes nothing; the winner of the night gets their name chalked on the beam.
**The table's best (roll d4 for tonight):** 1, Tobin Pike (+5); 2, Gerald Hatherley the miller (+6); 3, a passing Grey Wolf sellsword (+7; a T2 tell that something's moving on the road); 4, **Molly Pike** (+8; nobody has beaten her in nine years; Gideon might).
**Gideon** is the natural entrant (+5 at L1, +7 at L5). If he beats Molly, the whole inn goes quiet, then roars, then Molly makes him a pie.

### 3.3 Ferry Bell Night (the evening before a Tenth-day)
Anyone may ring the ferry bell a tune; best tune (by cheering) gets free supper. **Charisma (Performance) DC 13.** Ash's tune, rung on the bell, makes every hair on Nan's arms stand up. She doesn't say why (she doesn't know why). Color, not a seed.

### Skill contests (`00-index.md` §11; CHG-21)
- **The Ferry-Rope Run** (Gauntlet, Standard, T1; Restday afternoons in summer): hand-over-hand along the ferry cable, then the four stepping-posts, the swinging fish-net, a sprint up the landing and the bell on the ferry-house. Stations: cable ⏱ (Athletics or Acrobatics 12; climb speed: automatic), posts ⬇ (Acrobatics 12), net ⏱ (Sleight of Hand 12 to slip the snag), sprint ⏱ (Athletics or Con 12; Dash: automatic), landing ⬇ (Dex save 12), bell ⏱ (Athletics or Acrobatics 12). **Entry 2 gp**; Old Nan Pike's family takes **10%** (honest). Rivals: the Pike grandsons (+5 Athletics, −2 Acrobatics).
- **The Pike Shoot** (archery, T1; any market day): carved wooden pike bob on the current at 30 / 60 / 100 ft (**AC 13 / 15 / 17**, moving: the river). Three arrows, scored as §11.4. **Entry 1 gp; prize 15 gp and a smoked eel.** Rival: a King's Road courier who shoots +5.

## 4. Ring and street fights

**Ring formats (`00-index.md` §5.1, CHG-19):** **Bare-Knuckle** on the landing, or **Ring-Out on the ferry-barge's gangplank** over the river (the village's favorite: the loser swims). No magic in any format: no smites, no spells, no Wild Shape, no familiars. Dexterity fighters can win here.

There's no ring. There's the green.
- **Green bouts:** after the arm-wrestling, sometimes someone wants to settle it standing up. Use the street-fight rules (`00-index.md` §6) and the **Street Tough** block (`03-emberford.md` §4.3), T1 or T2. A crowd of thirty and a hat (1d6 × 5 gp × tier).
- **Local trick: geese.** Once per round, the village geese charge a random creature: **DC 12 Dex save** or knocked prone. It's not dangerous. It's humiliating.
- **Nan stops every fight at the end of round 3.** She bangs a pot. Everyone stops. Everyone.

## 6. Betting

On the ferry-pull, the arm-wrestling, the Rope-Walk dare, and whether the mill's wheel will squeak in the morning. Odds per `00-index.md` §4. Nan holds all stakes in a tin; she has never cheated anyone, and everyone knows it.

## 7. Things worth stealing

Ash's code applies here like the Fringe: **Hobb's Ferry is folk like his folk.** He won't steal here. If he does, Nan knows by morning, and the Pikes' attitude drops two steps; Tobin stops speaking to him.
| Target | Security | Fence value | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| A Toll Guard tally-barge's strongbox, moored overnight on the way to Tollhaven (T2) | Medium | 150 gp | Stealing **from Tollhaven**, not the Ferry. Nan won't mind. Nan will want to know nothing about it. |
| A lamp-warden patrol boat's manifest (T2) | Low | none | A seed-carrier in Act II: it lists "cargo: 1 (hooded)" on the Ch7 dates. Proof for later. |

**Fence:** none. "Nobody fences on the Ferry. Take it to the city."
