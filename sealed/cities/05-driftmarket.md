# Driftmarket: Side-Activity Menu (SEALED — DM ONLY)

> **Driftmarket** (aka Drift Market, "the Drift"). A raft-town of about 6,000 on lashed barges that drifts slowly round the Glasswater on a yearly circuit.
> **Government:** the **Captains' Moot**. Every barge owner has a voice, but the town is steered by the **Seven Mothers**, the eldest barge matriarchs of the seven founding river-clans. Decisions are made by **lashing** (a knot for yes, a cut thread for no). **Eldest Mother: Granny Wenna Tull** (NPC-062), nearly ninety, never leaves *The Patient Goose*.
> **When visited:** Ch7–9 (the Drift shelters Lark, Ch8; `chapters/act2/ch08.md` has the full side-activity window and the Mothers' knots, SC 8-A), and Ch20 (a T4 return). **Tier: T2, with T4 notes.**
> **The chapter wins.** Ch8's window already covers swim races, fish-auction betting, the Barge-Jump, the Dive, the Plank Run, the Lashing Dance, otter-slide, barrel bouts, odd jobs and stealing back for the Drift. This file adds the shops, the fight-ring block, the year's festival, the rivals, and the T4 return.

---

## 1. The Drift at a glance

- **Look:** a quarter-mile of barges lashed in rings, painted every color, joined by planks and rope bridges. Washing lines from mast to mast. Cooking smoke. Children diving between hulls. At night, lanterns (oil, mostly; the Drift distrusts glimmer more every month) reflected in black water.
- **Sound:** fiddles, a bell on the Chapel Barge, gulls, everyone shouting a relative's name.
- **Feel:** everyone's related to everyone. Very welcoming to anyone who works. Very hard on anyone who steals *from* the Drift.
- **Law:** none written. Disputes go to the Mothers. Thieves are put ashore wherever the town happens to be (sometimes a very long way from anywhere).
- **Clusters:** the **Galley Ring** (food barges), the **Swap Deck** (trade without coin), the **Chapel Barge**, the **Long Plank** (a quarter-mile boardwalk), **Tull's Raft** (the Mothers' barges in a ring).

**Working your passage:** anyone who poles for an hour (**Athletics DC 12**) eats free at the Galley Ring that night. The party will be fed whether they pole or not; poling earns respect.

## 2. Shops and services

### 2.1 The Swap Deck (barter only)
A broad flat barge covered in blankets, each blanket somebody's stall. **No coin changes hands.** Everything is a trade. Use the item's book price as its swap value; the trader wants something worth **10% more** to them. **Persuasion DC 13** to trade even; a good story about the item you're giving gets advantage.
| Trader | Wants | Has (book value) |
|---|---|---|
| **Old Corrie Bask** (NPC-322; aka Cory Bask), Skipper Ottilie Bask's grandmother, knits | anything with a story; any glimmer charm (she breaks them for fishing lures) | **Cloak of the Manta Ray** (Uncommon, 400 gp; she knitted it from net-thread and a drowned man's luck); woolens; a sea-chest of old charts |
| **Hob Rudd** (NPC-323; aka Robb Rudd), a young otter-folk-loving fisherman | fishing gear, good rope, a Bramblecog cheese | **Potions of Water Breathing** ×2 (Uncommon consumable, 200 gp each); eel-traps; an otter-folk raft-song written down (Mirrowen script, Zeke can read it after Ch10) |
| **The Merrilee family** (refugee otter-folk, only from Ch8; NPC-145's kin) | tools, blankets, anything to rebuild | river pearls (10 gp each, ×6); a carved otter-folk **swim charm** (glimmer-free; *Feather Fall*-like: once, if the wearer would sink, they float for a minute; Common, 100 gp) |

### 2.2 The Chapel Barge
A white barge with a squat bell tower. **Father Malory Wake** (NPC-324; aka Mallory, Father Wake), a lean, wind-burned priest of the Bright Lady who believes the lake listens. He's the priest who gives Zeke the **Pearl of Power** in Ch8 (or Moss, if Zeke refuses).
- **Services (donation):** *Cure Wounds* 10 gp · *Lesser Restoration* 40 gp · *Remove Curse* 90 gp (T2) · *Greater Restoration* 450 gp (T3+, if he's alive and the Drift's still afloat).
- **The bell:** ringing it wakes the whole Drift (Ch8's fight; a bonus action, adjacent). In peacetime, ringing it without cause is the one crime that gets you put ashore *at once*.
- **Seed (color):** Father Wake keeps a book of "lake-songs" the clans sing; one verse is about a stag who drinks from the still water and is never thirsty again. He thinks it's about the Bright Lady. (SEC-01 color; not a required seed.)

### 2.3 The Galley Ring
Food barges in a circle: fried perch, eel pie, river-crab stew, cider, an otter-folk fish-skewer stand from Ch8 on. Meals 2 sp; a feast 1 gp. **The Galley Ring's Big Pot** never empties: anyone may eat from it, and anyone who eats must add something. Lark adds a song.

### 2.4 Tull's Raft (the Mothers)
Not a shop. But the Seven Mothers' barges are where you **get things done**: a lashing (SC 8-A), a passage anywhere on the lake, a hiding place, a refugee berth. **Granny Tull's price for anything** is a true story and a hand at her mending.
- **The Seven Mothers' knots** (SC 8-A in Ch8): each Mother has a knot that is a promise. Once earned, a knot can be **called in once** for: free passage on any Drift barge, a hiding place for a person for a month, a crew of six river-clan polers and fighters for one night (use the **River-Clan Fighter** block below), or a word in a Tollhaven ear (the Drift trades with every Purse house).

### 2.5 Hettie Brine-no-relation's *Lucky Eel*
The gambling barge. Hettie runs fish-auction betting (Ch8) and a Kings and Ferrymen table (Street sharp, `00-index.md` §3.5). She's a Mother (SC 8-A); she rates people by how they lose.

## 3. Festivals and contests

### 3.1 The Lashing (20–21 Haymonth: the Drift's year-turn)
The Seven Mothers re-tie the town's ropes, one knot per barge, all night, by lantern. Every barge-family sings its own verse. At dawn, the whole Drift pushes off together from the year's mooring and begins the next circuit.
- **Ch8 is set in Haymonth.** If the party is on the Drift on the 20th, the Lashing is the backdrop of their last night (play it gently; it's beautiful).
- **The Lashing Dance** (Ch8 window): Performance or Acrobatics DC 13; the Mothers watch who dances (+1 to one SC 8-A check).
- **The Knot Contest:** tie the most knots on a running line in a minute, **Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)** contest against the best river-clan knotter (+7). Ash's Sleight of Hand is made for this. **Prize:** a knot-charm from Granny Tull (no rules; every river-clan in Eyrland recognizes it and treats the bearer as kin).

### 3.2 The Dive, the Plank Run, the Barge-Jump, swim races
**Ch8's window has them all.** For a T4 return (Ch20), the DCs go up by 4 and the prizes are T4: the Dive's silver ring becomes a **Ring of Swimming** (Uncommon; Granny Tull's late husband's), the Plank Run pays 100 gp.

## 4. Barrel bouts (the Drift's fight ring)

Wrestling on a floating barrel until someone falls in. **Ch8's version:** opposed Athletics, best of three falls, 10 gp. For a fuller bout (an entrant wants a real fight, or a T4 return), use the ring rules (`00-index.md` §5.1) with these changes:
- **The barrel:** every creature on it must start its turn with a **DC 12 Dex (Acrobatics) save** (DC 15 at T4) or fall prone (and prone on a barrel = in the water = **loses the fall**). Best of three falls.
- **Ash's Fancy Footwork / Second-Story Work don't help on a spinning barrel.** Acrobatics proficiency does (he has it).

**Traits**
- ***River-Born.*** Advantage on the barrel's Acrobatics saves.
- ***Round-2 Turn: "Spin It."*** At the start of round 2, Big Dace spins the barrel with his feet: every other creature on it makes the barrel save at **disadvantage** this round.

**Actions**
- ***Multiattack.*** Two Grapples, or one Grapple and a Heave.
- ***Grapple.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) bludgeoning, nonlethal, and the target is grappled (escape DC 15).
- ***Heave.*** One creature Big Dace is grappling: **DC 15 Str save** or thrown off the barrel (loses the fall).

**Tactics:** grapple, then heave. If he loses, he climbs out laughing and buys the round. **Purse:** 50 gp (T2), and his mother's knot moves one step easier (SC 8-A).

## 5. Betting

In shells, buttons and favors as much as coin (`00-index.md` §4 odds). A favor is worth about 5 gp × tier and is always honored. **Hettie's *Lucky Eel*** takes coin. The Drift's favorite bets: which barge-child will dive deepest, which Mother will cut the first thread at a lashing, and how long Bart can stay on a mast in a gale.

## 7. Things worth stealing

**Nothing on the Drift.** It's bad form. Nobody steals from the Drift twice. If Ash does, the Mothers put him ashore at the far end of the lake, and Granny Tull won't say his name for a year.
**Stealing *for* the Drift** earns real favor (Ch8: the *Kindly Turn*'s cooking pots). More of the same:
| Target | Where | Security | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|
| The *Kindly Turn*'s cooking pots (Ch8) | a Tollhaven tally-barge | Low (DC 14–15) | A knot easier (Ch8) |
| A clan's "confiscated" net-boat | the Weighing Docks, Tollhaven | Medium | 60 gp from the clan and a feast |
| Red Tilly Hake's loot cache (pirate island) | an islet in the north lake | Medium | 1d4 × 50 gp of river-clan goods, returned; the Mothers let Ash keep a fifth |

**Fence:** none on the Drift. Hettie knows a man in Tollhaven (`06-tollhaven.md`).

## 8. Rivals

- **A rival clan's showboat captain:** **Captain Rafe Lennick** (NPC-325; aka Raif, Lenick), a young, handsome, insufferable skipper of the *Golden Perch* who races everyone, everywhere, and loses gracefully about one time in three. He challenges Ash to the Barge-Jump and the Plank Run. Beat him twice and he becomes a friend who'll ferry the party anywhere on the lake at night, no questions.
- **Red Tilly Hake** (lake pirate; Ch8). Not a rival so much as a recurring nuisance.

## 9. Driftmarket by act

| When | What the Drift is like |
|---|---|
| **Ch7–9** | Sheltering refugees (otter-folk especially). The first to welcome beastfolk. The Galley Ring has otter-folk cooks by Ch9. |
| **After Ch13** | Glimmer's gone or rationed; the Drift barely notices (oil lanterns). It becomes a refuge for Emberford folk too, and the Mothers' ring of barges grows by thirty hulls. |
| **Act III** | The Drift is the lake's ferry for the Ch17 alliance muster: every Mother sends barges. If the party earned four or more knots in Ch8, the Drift sends **fighters** too (twelve River-Clan Fighters, T4) for Ch19–20. |
| **Ch20** | The Drift is wherever it needs to be. Granny Tull is alive (unless the chapter says otherwise). She wants to see Lark's ears. |
