# Saltreach: Side-Activity Menu (SEALED — DM ONLY)

> **Saltreach** (aka Salt Reach, Saltreech). The Sea Gate: a city of about 20,000 at the river mouth, where the delta meets the Sundersea. Salt wind, gulls, tar, sea shanties, white houses with blue doors.
> **Government:** a **crown governor**, appointed for seven years. **Lady Governor Maud Penrose** (NPC-071), a former navy captain, white-haired, one-eyed, unshakably fair, short of money and short of patience. A mentor figure in Ch19; no romance. **Law:** the **Harbor Watch** and the **Governor's Marines**. Stronger law than Emberford, but the delta is lawless.
> **When visited:** Ch19 (`campaign/02` Ch19: hire or win a ship — the Governor's navy, the Lanternless smugglers via Captain Jago Trelawn, NPC-072, or steal one). **Tier: T4.**
> **DM notes (world/03):** the lighthouse cistern is a Wellgate. The **Lanternless** (FAC-13, smugglers' guild) know about Ommu's Rest; one old captain has seen "the shell under the hills" (SEC-12). Final-act staging town.
> **The chapter wins.** This file supplies the town around Ch19: shops, the Gull Race and the Shanty Contest, the Hold (the fight ring), the smugglers' race and card game that can win Jago's respect (★1 route B), odd jobs, stealing, the rival.

---

## 1. The city at a glance

- **Look:** a white town stepping down cliffs to a harbor; blue doors; the old **lighthouse** on Lighthouse Point beside the governor's squat fort; the **Saltpans** shining in squares along the delta; the **Shambles** (fish markets, sailors' taverns); **Smugglers' Stair** (a cliff-cut maze of cellars); the **Navy Yard** with three tired warships.
- **Sound:** gulls, shanties, the harbor bell, the sea.
- **Feel:** superstitious about the sea and about **the Glass** (the stretch of Sundersea that's gone flat and still). People touch iron when they say its name.
- **In Midsummer YF 1105 (Ch19):** the Glass is spreading. Fishing boats come back with nets full of grey, motionless fish. The Gull Race is going ahead anyway, out of stubbornness.

## 3. Festivals and contests

### 3.1 The Gull Race (10–12 Midsummer)
A small-sail regatta around the harbor and out past the point. **Ch19 is in Midsummer**; the race is a natural window (and in YF 1105, the course runs *along the edge of the Glass*).
- **The chain (a boat of two or more; best sailor rolls, others Help):** **Wisdom (Vehicles (water)) DC 17** (the start) · **Strength (Athletics) DC 17** (the beat to windward) · **Survival DC 19** (the point, where the current turns) · **Dexterity (Acrobatics) DC 17** (the jibe) · **Vehicles (water) DC 21** (the Glass edge: the wind dies; Zeke's magic, the Wind Fan, or Moss as a seal towing gets advantage).
- **Rivals:** the **Lanternless** boat (Captain Tamsin Gale, +11; §8), the Navy Yard's cutter (+10), the fishermen's boats (+8).
- **4–5:** win, **1,000 gp** and the Gull Cup. **Winning against Tamsin earns Jago's respect** (Ch19 ★1 route B: "a smugglers' race").

### 3.2 The Shanty Contest (the Barnacle; any night in Midsummer)
**Charisma (Performance) DC 17**, then a contest against Gussie's best (+9). The whole room sings the chorus. **Ash's tune** as a shanty: the room goes quiet on the second verse, then Gussie starts to cry and joins in with the low part. **Prize:** the Barnacle's tab for the visit, and a shanty with Ash's name in it that Saltreach sailors will sing for a hundred years (epilogue line).

## 4. The Hold (fight ring)

Bouts in the hold of a rotting warship hulk in the Navy Yard's mud. Ring rules (`00-index.md` §5); **the ship lists**: at the start of each round, roll a d6; on a 1, everyone in the ring makes a **DC 15 Dex save** or slides 10 ft downhill and falls prone.
- **Purses (T4):** 300 / 500–1,000 / **2,000 gp** (championship).
- **Opponents:** Brannagh Reece came from here (`03-emberford.md` §4.4; if she was beaten in Emberford, she's back, at T4: +2 AC, +4 to hit, HP 140, and she'll call Ash "the one who taught me the river"). The **Hold's champion** is below.

### "SALT" MERRIDY (the Hold's champion)
*Medium Humanoid (human), Chaotic Good* · **AC** 18 · **Initiative** +6 · **HP** 137 · **Speed** 30 ft., climb 30 ft. · **CR** 13 (PB +5)
*Tuned for party level 19–20 (Ash alone; ring DPR 35 + 32 round-one, HP 136–143).* A retired navy bosun with a laugh like a foghorn and tattoos of every ship she's sunk (none; she means "outlived"). Weak saves: **Wis +2, Int +0**.
- ***Sea Legs.*** Immune to the Hold's listing.
- ***Multiattack.*** Three Belaying-Pin strikes: *Melee* +12, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 2d8 + 6 bludgeoning, nonlethal.
- ***Rigging (bonus action).*** She swings on a hanging rope up to 20 ft without provoking; her next attack has advantage.
- ***Round-2 Turn: "All Hands."*** The crowd stamps: the ship lists on a **1–3** this round.
- ***Round-3 Turn: "Splice."*** If losing, she grabs a rope and ties the opponent's ankle: **DC 19 Dex save** or restrained until the end of her next turn.
- ***Reaction: Roll With It.*** Halve the damage of one hit (once per bout).

## 5. Street fights

Shambles brawls between sailors, fishermen and Marines. **Local trick: the fish-gutting tables.** A creature shoved onto one is **slippery**: disadvantage on its next attack and Dex save. The Harbor Watch arrives at the end of round 2 and fines everyone 50 gp (T4). Use the Street Tough block (T4).

---

## 6. Betting

Ship arrivals (grim in YF 1105: "which boats come back from the Glass"), the Gull Race, the Hold's books, and **cockfights**. Ash may refuse those; Moss won't stay in the room. If Ash sets the birds loose (**Sleight of Hand DC 18** on the cage latches, during the betting), the room erupts and it becomes a street fight, and every bird in Saltreach seems to like him afterward.

## 8. Rivals

- **Captain Tamsin Gale** (NPC-350; aka Tam Gale, Tamzin): a smuggler captain of the Lanternless, in her forties, always one step ahead, sharp, funny, older-sister energy; **no romance** (`world/03`). She beats Ash to every job in Saltreach until he beats her at something (the Gull Race, the card game below, or a job). Then she's the one who rows him out to Jago's ship.
- **The card game for Jago's respect** (★1 route B): Kings and Ferrymen on Jago's own ship. **Legend-grade** sharp (`00-index.md` §3.5): Jago +14. Three hands. Win two, or catch Jago cheating (**Insight DC 20**) and call it with a smile (he roars with laughter; that counts).
