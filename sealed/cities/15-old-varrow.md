# Old Varrow: Side-Activity Menu (SEALED — DM ONLY)

> **Old Varrow** (aka Old Varo, Varrow). The drowned ruin in the Greymarsh: once the old capital of Eyrland, destroyed four hundred years ago in the **Gate War** when a Wellgate under it breached. A half-sunk city of towers and drowned streets. Mist, bells under water, herons, silent ghosts.
> **Government:** none. The dead keep it (`world/03` DM notes: ruled by the **Hushed dead** of the Gate War, preserved, grey, calm, endlessly "keeping" their drowned city).
> **When visited:** Ch10 (passing along its edge, optional), Ch18 (the dungeon: `chapters/act3/ch18.md`; its side window has treasure diving, Ring the Bell, the Fenn-wyck tomb and, if the Heron is redeemed, flying with her). **Tier: T2 (the edge) and T4 (the ruin).**
> **The chapter wins.** Ch18 owns the ruin itself. This file is for the **marsh-edge**: the scavengers' camp, their dares and dives, their trade, and a T2 brush with the ruin if the party passes in Act II.

---

## 1. The marsh-edge at a glance

- **Look:** reed-flats under mist; a causeway of old paving stones half underwater leading toward the ruin; the ruin's towers on the horizon like broken teeth; and, on a hummock at the causeway's start, **Drowners' Rest**: a scavengers' camp of tarred huts on stilts, a bell on a post, a rowing-boat for every hut, and a fire that's never allowed to go out.
- **Sound:** frogs, herons, wind in reeds, and, on still days, a bell under the water out toward the ruin (one note, slow).
- **Feel:** the scavengers are cheerful in the way people are when they've all nearly died. They tell everyone the rules three times.
- **The three rules of Drowners' Rest** (Bertie tells you; everyone repeats them):
  1. Never go out on a still day. (On a still day, the dead walk the causeway.)
  2. Never take anything with a name on it. (The dead come for it.)
  3. If you hear your own name from the water, don't answer.

## 3. Dares and dives (the marsh-edge)

### 3.1 Ring the Bell (the dare)
Row to the Great Bell Tower and ring it. **Ch18 owns the T4 version** (if the Heron was redeemed, it rings clear and every Hushed dead in the marsh lies down). **At T2 (Act II, passing):** it's a dare with real teeth.
- **The chain:** **Survival DC 15** (the channels) · **Stealth DC 15** (past the drowned street where the dead stand) · **Athletics DC 16** (climb the tower's slick outer stair; advantage with climb speed) · **Strength DC 14** (ring it).
- **Ring it:** the bell gives one cracked, terrible note, and the dead in the streets **turn and look at the tower.** Get down and out (**Acrobatics DC 16, then Survival DC 15**). Fail: a significant fight on the stair with **Hushed Gate War soldiers** (T2: four, use the Act II Hushed block from `chapters/act2/` with "treated as undead for spells," `world/05`), and the boat is a long way off.
- **Prize:** Drowners' Rest throws a party; Bertie names a hut after the ringer; and Ash's name is carved on the bell-post (the only other name on it is from forty years ago).

### 3.2 Edge-dives
The drowned houses on the city's edge can be dived for small finds. **One dive:** **Athletics DC 14** (T2) / 19 (T4) and **Investigation DC 14 / 19**. A failed dive: **2d10** (T2) / 4d10 (T4) bludgeoning (falling masonry) and nothing found.
**Finds (d6 per successful dive):** 1, a handful of Varrow coins (50 gp at a collector's) · 2, a silver cup (25 gp) · 3, a soldier's badge with a name (**rule 2!** Taking it brings a Hushed soldier to the camp that night, quietly, to stand at the water's edge until it's returned) · 4, a **Potion of Healing** in a sealed bottle, still good · 5, a small bronze bell (5 gp; Bertie's camp buys every bell) · 6, a carved stag-and-ford crest on a broken tile (**seed color**: the Fenn-wyck crest, matching Ash's locket, SEC-23 / `world/03`; place it only if the outline allows)

### 3.3 Still-Day Watch (a dare the scavengers don't do)
On a still day, sit on the causeway at dusk and watch the dead walk past. **Wisdom DC 15** save each hour (failure: Ash feels very calm and wants to walk with them; an ally must pull him back: Athletics DC 13). Who does it learns the dead are *keeping* the city, walking the same rounds they walked in life (Ch18 color). Nobody else in Drowners' Rest will speak to you that night, out of respect.

### Skill contests (`00-index.md` §11; CHG-21)
- **Ring the Bell** (§3.1) is a **Mast Climb** variant: climb speed gives advantage.
- **The Salvagers' Night Climb** (`§11.6`, T2): from the scavengers' camp into a half-sunk tower at the marsh-edge and back with a marked token. Pot **100 gp** staked by two salvage crews. Stealth and Perception stations; the drowned bells ring if anyone fails Stealth by 5 or more, and then it isn't a contest any more (Hushed dead, `§4` below).

## 4. Fights

**No ring.** The marsh is the ring. Scavengers settle disputes by **the Long Pole**: two people stand on two boats, each with a pole, and try to knock the other in (opposed **Athletics**, best of three; a fall is a cold swim). Bertie's six are all +5. Bertie himself is +8 with one arm.

---

## 5. Betting

On whether the newcomers will Ring the Bell and come back (Bertie gives **3 to 1** at T2, evens at T4), and on the Long Pole.

## 7. Things worth stealing

Anything with a name on it brings the dead (rule 2). Everything else is the scavengers' living. **Ch18's treasure diving** is the real haul (a Gate War royal crown, a chest of old coin, a Varrow lantern).
**Fence:** Bertie, at 40%. Black Tom Rudge in Highmarch buys Varrow coins at 30%.

## 8. Rivals

None. Everyone at Drowners' Rest has the same enemy, and it's very patient.
