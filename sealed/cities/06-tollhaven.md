# Tollhaven: Side-Activity Menu (SEALED — DM ONLY)

> **Tollhaven** (aka Toll Haven, Tolhaven). The City of Purses: about 40,000 people on the north shore of the Glasswater where the Tamber enters the lake.
> **Government:** the **Eleven Purses**. Eleven merchant houses hold the eleven seats of the Purse Council; votes are weighted by taxes paid; the chair (**the First Purse**) rotates yearly to the richest house. **First Purse this year: Dame Constance Abernathy** (NPC-063). **Law:** the **Toll Guard**, hired by the council, polite and well paid, strict about contracts, lax about everything else.
> **When visited:** Ch8–10 (Ch9 is set here: `chapters/act2/ch09.md` has the full side-activity window), Ch14 (passing), Ch20. **Tier: T2–T3.**
> **The chapter wins.** Ch9's window covers the Grand Wager, masquerade card tables, the Counting Race, Purse duels, the regatta, a Golden Cage bout, stealing targets and courier jobs. This file adds the shops, the Golden Cage's full card and champion block, the fence, the rivals and the street-fight trick, and keeps a T3 version ready.

---

## 1. The city at a glance

- **Look:** buildings painted loud colors to show off money (saffron, cobalt, apple green), gilt on every lintel, a famous bronze coin-statue in the Counting Quarter as tall as three men. The Weighing Docks' great scales creak all day.
- **Feel:** money everywhere, everyone haggling. Contracts are holy. A handshake is worth nothing; a wax seal is worth everything.
- **Districts:** the **Counting Quarter** (banks, the Purse Hall), the **Weighing Docks** (every barge pays toll by weight), **Lamplight Row** (theaters, gambling houses, bathhouses), the **Netyards** (fishermen), **Gull Hill** (rich villas over the lake).
- **The Toll Guard rule:** break a contract and you're arrested before your nose stops bleeding. Break a nose and they'll ask whether there was a contract about it.
- **Everything is legal if it's written down.** That's the joke and the danger. Ash can sign a contract to do almost anything. He should read it (**Investigation DC 14** to find the clause that hurts).

## 2. Shops and services

Haggling is a sport here: **every shop's Haggle DC is −2** (they expect it), but **failing by 5+** gets you a lecture on value and a **5%** surcharge "for wasting my afternoon."

### 2.1 Fairbanks' Counting-House of Curios (Counting Quarter)

**Interior (painter):** a cool marble hall of glass cases with brass number-tags; a wall of clocks that all tell slightly different times (she buys Dame Abernathy's rejects); a great ledger on a lectern, open; a pair of jeweler's scales in the window.

### 2.3 Mother Hollyhock's Bathhouse (Lamplight Row)
**Keeper: Mother Hollyhock** (NPC-328; aka Holly Hock), a vast, serene, gossiping woman who knows every Purse secret worth knowing because every Purse heir eventually sits in her hot pool and talks. Baths 5 sp; a private pool 5 gp; a rumor: **1 gp and Persuasion DC 13**, or free if you make her laugh. A natural information source for Ch9.

### 2.4 The Toll Guard's Contract Office (Purse Hall)
Write and seal any contract (2 gp); witness a duel (5 gp); post a bounty (10% of the bounty). **Every odd job in Tollhaven goes through here.** The clerk is a dried-up man called **Mr. Petch** (no ID) who has never smiled.

## 3. Festivals and contests

**Ch9 runs it** (buy-in 50 gp; three rounds: cards, dice, riddles; final purse 600 gp; Julian Hume in the final). If the party is in Tollhaven in Harvest of a later visit (unlikely), run the same chain at **DC +4** and a **2,000 gp** final purse, with a Legend-grade sharp (`00-index.md` §3.5) in the final.

### 3.2 The Counting Race, the regatta, Purse duels, masquerade tables
**Ch9's window.** For a T3 return, DCs +3 and purses ×3.

### 3.3 The Seal-Breaking (any month's first day)
Each month the Purse Council breaks the old month's seal on the Weighing Docks' great scales and stamps a new one. Public, boring, and the one hour a month when the Weighing Docks' strongroom is open with everybody watching the ceremony instead of the strongroom (§7).

### Skill contests (`00-index.md` §11; CHG-21)
- **The Eleven Locks** (lock-picking, Hard, T2–T3; the first Tenth-day of each month, in the Exchange courtyard): each of the Eleven Purses mounts a lock on the board, DCs **14 / 15 / 16 / 17 / 18** then six more by the House sponsoring the finals (DC 18–22, two trapped). Sand-glass of eleven minutes. **Entry 25 gp; prize 300 gp (T2) / 750 gp (T3)** and a Purse's letter of employment (which is a trap of a kind: the winners' list is the Toll Guard's favorite reading). Rivals: House Ellery's pet picker (+9), a "retired" Saltreach burglar (+8).
- **The Ribbon Pull** (`§11.6`, T2; Countinghouse Fair): in the Exchange square, the marks are Purse clerks, passive Perception 13–16. **Entry 5 gp, prize 80 gp and a Purse's silk purse.** The Toll Guard watches; a bell rung is a fine of 5 gp, not an arrest (during the Fair).

### New contests (`00-index.md` §12–13; CHG-22)
- **Strange Arms: the Purse Cup** (Grand Wager, circuit stop 2; a Purse sponsors it; weapons #2, 4, 5, 10). Bets are enormous.
- **The Switch** in the Counting Quarter: swap a wax seal on a contract under three clerks' eyes (the clerks are +6 Perception; the Toll Guard officiates and is amused).
- **Who's the Duke** at the masquerade tables (Lamplight Row): the hunters are Toll Guard off duty, Insight +6.
- **Steal the Purse** at the Seal-Breaking: a Purse's showpiece strongbox (T2 DCs).


## 4. The Golden Cage (legal fight arena)

**Ring formats (`00-index.md` §5.1, CHG-19):** **Touches** (Tollhaven's fashionable format: waster rapiers, judges with flags), **Bare-Knuckle** (the cheap seats' card), and **Last-Standing** on Purse holidays. Contracts are signed for the format; a fighter who switches format forfeits. No magic in any format: no smites, no spells, no Wild Shape, no familiars. Dexterity fighters can win here.

A gilded iron cage in a round hall on Lamplight Row. Velvet boxes for Purse families, cheap benches for everyone else. Armed bouts with **blunted** steel (all damage nonlethal on a 0, but blunted weapons deal full damage dice). **Healers on hand** (free between bouts). **No magic, no familiars.** **Cage-master: Leopold Quarry** (NPC-329; aka Leo Quarrie), sleek, bored, charming, keeps a ledger of every bout since the Cage opened. **Fighters may bet on themselves only through a registered agent** (5% fee; `00-index.md` §4).
**Entry:** 10 gp × tier (the Cage is expensive). **Purses:** T2 undercard 50 / main 150 / Cage champion **200** (Ch9) · T3 150 / 400 / **750**.

- ***Parry.*** +2 AC against one melee attack.

## 5. Street fights in Tollhaven

Rare and expensive. **Local trick: the Toll Guard fines.** At the end of round 2, the Guard arrives (always polite). Everyone standing pays a **10 gp × tier fine** "for breach of the public peace (no contract on file)." Anyone who can produce a written challenge (signed before the fight) pays nothing. Toughs in the Netyards carry pre-signed challenges in their caps. The party learns this the hard way.
Use the **Street Tough** block (`03-emberford.md` §4.3), T2–T3. Netyards toughs swing **gutting hooks** (piercing, same dice) and can **Hook** (bonus action, pull 5 ft).

## 6. Betting

Everything, legally, with the Purse taking **5%** off every winning bet (on top of the bookie's shave). Every bet is written and sealed. Welching is a contract breach (arrest).
**Odd Tollhaven books:** which Purse house will go bankrupt this year (Merrow at 20 to 1 before Ch9; 1 to 3 after), how long Leopold Quarry's ledger has to get before he retires, how many clocks Dame Abernathy owns (nobody knows; the book's been open six years).

## 8. Rivals

- **Julian Hume** (NPC-142): the smug young broker in a plum coat who outbids the party for every odd job and turns up everywhere (Ch9). In side activities: any T2 odd job in Tollhaven, roll a d6; on 1–3, Julian has bid for it too, and Ash must win a **Persuasion contest** (Julian +6) or take 20% less. Beat him at anything public and he becomes a source (Ch9).
- **Valentine Crewe** (NPC-141): not a side-activity rival. The chapter owns him.
- **The Velvet Box** (the Purse house that keeps Cassius Dray): **House Ellery** (no ID; Tier 3 if used), who'd love to own a Fringe champion.
