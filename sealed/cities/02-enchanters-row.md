# Enchanters' Row: Workshops, Commissions and Gifts (SEALED — DM ONLY)

> The Row is where Ash buys gifts for the people he grows close to. **Every gift is a relationship beat, and every gift comes back once, later, in a scene** (`campaign/05` §4). This file gives each of the eight workshops a keeper, a voice, an interior for the painter, a full commission menu (price and lead time), and what changes as the campaign moves. Then it gives the commission procedure and the Gifts Given log format.
> **Read with:** `world/06` (the Row's place in the city), `chapters/act1/ch02.md` Scene 4 (the first visit, the Lamplit flare, EVT-40), `campaign/05` §4 (the planned gift moments), `00-index.md` §2 (prices).
> **Glimmer.** Every Row trinket has a glimmer core (`world/06`). **They all die if glimmer dies** (Endings 1 and 2). Tell the player in the epilogue, and make it beautiful: the gifts go dark, the people keep them anyway. The exceptions are marked **(true)**: a few makers can work real enchantment, slower and dearer, and those items survive every ending.

## 1. The Row at a glance

**Where:** Glimmerway, a narrow glass-roofed lane about two hundred feet long. Open from mid-morning to the first lamp-lighting, six days in ten; closed on Tenth-days (everyone's at the market). The **Lamplit** opens only one day in five (Clara's apprentice day: the 2nd, 7th, 12th, 17th, 22nd and 27th of each month).
**The sound:** chimes, a music box playing three notes over and over (Keepsake & Locket's window), the hiss of a kettle Wat Ember argues with, Dagny swearing in Dwarvish.
**The rule of the Row:** every item carries a Guild license stamp (a tiny lamp sigil, stamped by a Lamplighters' clerk who walks the Row each morning). Unlicensed work is cheaper (**−25%**) and illegal: the item is confiscated if a lamp-warden spots it (it hums oddly; **DC 15 Perception** to notice). Corwin Lockley does unlicensed work for people he likes. Nobody else will.

## 2. Commission procedure (every workshop)

1. **Describe it.** The player says what Ash wants, in plain words. The keeper says what it'll do, using the nearest menu line below, or builds a new one (§2.3).
2. **Price and time.** The menu gives both. The haggle rules in `00-index.md` §2 apply, with the keeper's own lever. **Rush job:** half the lead time for **+50%** (Dagny refuses; Elspeth refuses for anything with a voice in it).
3. **Half up front.** The rest on pickup. Ash can leave a deposit and come back after a paid job; keepers hold work for a month.
4. **Inscription and wrapping are free.** Always ask the player for the inscription. Write it down word for word.
5. **Pickup is a scene.** A short one: the keeper shows it working; the keeper says one line about who it's for. Then Ash gives it, and **the giving is the beat.** Play the recipient's reaction from their Truth File entry (VOICE; ATTITUDE "up if").
6. **Log it** in the Campaign Bible's **Gifts Given** list (§5) the same session.
7. **The callback.** Every gift comes back once. Planned callbacks are in `campaign/05` §4. For a gift that isn't planned there, write a callback idea in the log and use it within two or three chapters.

### 2.1 What a gift does to the ledger
A gift isn't an event by itself. It becomes one when it **matters**: the moment it's used, saves someone, or is noticed. At that moment, log the EVT (new ID if it isn't a planned one) and move the recipient's edge toward Ash one step. A gift given to someone who already loves Ash doesn't move the edge. It adds a line to their VOICE: something they say about it, later, unprompted.

### 2.2 Materials Ash brings
Some items need something only Ash can get (a hair, a feather, a bit of Mirrowen moss, a scale). The menu marks these **[needs]**. Bringing an unusual material can also upgrade a Common item to a better version at no extra cost (keeper's call; one upgrade per item).

### 2.3 Building a new commission
If the player asks for something not on the menus:
- **Does one small cantrip-sized thing, always, or once a day:** Common, **100 gp**, 3 days to 2 weeks.
- **Gives a real mechanical edge** (a +1, advantage on one kind of check, a spell once a day): Uncommon, **400 gp** plus a material **[needs]**, 2–4 weeks. Only Hearthglow, the Dry Cloak, Keepsake & Locket, Lightfinger and Quickhand can do Uncommon. Nobody on the Row does Rare.
- **Pure fashion or fun, no rules effect:** a **glimmer charm**, 5–50 gp, 1–3 days.
- **(true) magic** (survives the end of glimmer): Uncommon only, from **Dagny** (tools) or **Elspeth** (memory), **×2 price and ×2 time**, and only after Ch10 (when Dagny's oath-shame turns into pride, and Elspeth admits she learned the old way first).

## 3. The workshops

### 3.1 Hearthglow — Old Wat Ember (NPC-050)
**Voice:** a big, slow, bald man with a voice like a warm stove, who talks to his kettle as if it's his wife. *"She's sulking. Aren't you, love. Don't mind her. Now. What's cold that oughtn't be?"*
**Interior (painter):** low beams, a stone hearth taking up a whole wall with a brass kettle forever on the hob. Blankets folded on shelves glowing faintly amber. Mittens hung on a line like flags. A cat asleep on a heat-stone. Everything is a shade of orange or brown. Steam on the glass.
**Haggle lever:** compliment the kettle by name ("Bettina") and he takes 10% off. Advantage on the haggle if Ash brings a cold person in and lets Wat fuss over them.

### 3.5 Lightfinger — Corwin Lockley (NPC-054)

### 3.6 The Lamplit — Clara Grane (NPC-005)
**This is the most important shop on the Row.** Play it exactly as `chapters/act1/ch02.md` Scene 4 writes it the first time. After that:
**Voice:** warm, precise, apologizes too much; tucks hair behind her ear when nervous; hums the first line of *his* tune while she works.
**Interior (painter):** a tiny shop at the middle of the Row, its window full of lamps in every color: amber, rose, sea-green, one that cycles slowly through a sunset. A brass bell over the door that plays two notes. Her bench is under the window: a jeweler's glass, fine brushes, a lamp the size of a teacup. Glimmer dust everywhere.
**No haggling.** She undercharges everyone. **Insight DC 12** spots it; if Ash pays full price anyway, R-052 (Clara → Ash) notes it (no step; a line in her VOICE: "You overpaid. I noticed.").
**The flare:** every time Ash and Clara are within 5 feet in this shop, every lamp on the Row flares. The Row's keepers keep joking about it ("The lamps can't tell you two apart!"). Don't let anyone explain it. **Never play it as romance** (she's his twin, `world/00`, and the player's rule is no romance with female allies). If the player ever leans that way, Clara cheerfully isn't interested ("You're like a brother I misplaced"), which is both true and a seed.

| **Act III (Ch14–20)** | The Row is half-shuttered and proud of the half that's open. Only (true) commissions are reliable. In Ending 3, the Row comes back (the epilogue can show it reopening, lamps first). |

**Rules for the log:**
