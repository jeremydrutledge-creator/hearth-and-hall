# Wolfsgate: Side-Activity Menu (SEALED — DM ONLY)

> **Wolfsgate** (aka Wolf's Gate, Wolfgate). A town of about 2,000 in a high valley on the Pass Road into the Kettlehorns.
> **Government:** a **mercenary company charter.** An old king granted the town to the **Grey Wolf Company** (FAC-16), and the **Captain-General, Ulric Fane** (NPC-065), rules it under the charter, with the company's sergeants as council. **Law:** company law. Strict on order, indifferent to morals. Anything can be hired. **Nobody may draw steel inside the Wolf Hall.**
> **When visited:** Ch10 (`chapters/act2/ch10.md`: the Wolves hold Bearback Pass for a Calm cutout; the side window has the mercenary card house, the hiring fair, knife duels and the Wolf Run). **Tier: T2 (T4 notes).**
> **The chapter wins.** This file adds shops, the Yard (the sparring ring) with a block, odd jobs, rivals, and what Wolfsgate becomes later.

---

## 1. The town at a glance

- **Look:** a walled town of grey stone and black timber in a snowy valley; the **Wolf Hall** (a long timber hall with a wolf's skull over the door the size of a cart); the **Hiring Yard** (a muddy square with a chalked board of open contracts); **Gate Market**; the **Barracks Rows**. Wolf-pelt cloaks everywhere.
- **Sound:** drill-sergeants, smiths, dogs, a horn at every watch change.
- **Feel:** hard, practical, and oddly honest. A Wolf keeps a contract to the letter and never past it. Everyone here is for sale, and everyone tells you so.
- **The rule of the Hall:** steel drawn inside the Wolf Hall is a death sentence, enforced at once. Fists are fine. The Hall's floor is famous for bloodstains from fists.

### 2.1 The Quartermaster's (the Barracks Rows)
**Keeper: Quartermaster Dorran Kemp** (NPC-338; aka Doran, Kempe), a lame old Wolf with a ledger and a ferocious memory for who owes what.
**Interior (painter):** a stone storehouse with racks of spears, crossbows, shields painted with grey wolves, sacks of oats, snowshoes, and a counter made from a siege-door.

### 2.2 Vonn's Card House (Gate Market)
**Keeper: Hesketh Vonn** (NPC-337; aka Hesky, Von), a soft-voiced ex-Wolf with one hand, who runs the town's only gambling house and throws cheats out the window (literally, into the snow). Kings and Ferrymen with sellswords, 25 gp a hand (Ch10). **Professional** sharps (`00-index.md` §3.5).
**Interior (painter):** a smoky upstairs room with a big window over the snow (the window is on hinges; there's a cheat-shaped dent in the drift below); round tables, wolf-pelt chairs, a stove.

### 2.3 The Hiring Yard

**Ring formats (`00-index.md` §5.1, CHG-19):** the Yard spars in **Touches** (Company rules: waster swords, three touches, the sergeant judges) and **Last-Standing** on payday. Knife duels (§3.2) are Touches with waster knives. No magic in any format: no smites, no spells, no Wild Shape, no familiars. Dexterity fighters can win here.
Anything can be hired (Ch10). **Rates:** a Grey Wolf Veteran (`act2/ch10` block) **200 gp** a fight (Ch13 allies), a squad of five Wolf soldiers (Street Tough T2 with spears and shields, AC 16) 100 gp a night, a guide over the pass 25 gp, a sapper 50 gp. Every hire is a written contract (Investigation DC 13 for the clause that says "unless the other side pays more" — it's always there, and Ulric's Wolves never use it on a party that's been honest with them).

## 3. Contests

### 3.1 The Wolf Run (25 Harvest; Ch10 runs it any night as a challenge)
An endurance race up the mountain at night (Ch10: **Athletics DC 15 ×3, Con DC 14 ×2**, 50 gp, Ulric's attitude up a step). The official race, on the 25th, has 200 runners, torches, and a wolf-pelt for the winner: at T2, **150 gp and the pelt**.

### 3.2 Knife duels and the Yard
**Knife duels** (Ch10): first to draw blood, in the Yard, opposed attack rolls best of three, 25 gp.
**The Yard (sparring for pay):** recruiters watch. Ring rules (`00-index.md` §5) with real (blunted) steel and the company surgeon (free). **Win a bout and a recruiter offers a contract** (200 gp a season; decline politely).

**Skills** Athletics +7, Perception +4 · **Senses** passive Perception 14 · **Languages** Common · **CR** 5 (T2) / 11 (T4) (PB +3 / +4)
*Tuned for: T2 party level 9–10 (Ash alone; ring DPR 20, HP 66–73): solo soldier. T4: +3 AC, +4 to hit, damage +1 die, Multiattack 3.*

**Traits**
- ***Drilled.*** The sergeant can't be flanked (no advantage from allies next to it), and Ash's Sneak Attack needs **advantage** (an ally nearby doesn't count).
- ***Round-2 Turn: "Shield Wall."*** At the start of round 2, the sergeant plants their shield: **+2 AC** and **Shove** as a bonus action until the end of the bout.

**Actions**
- ***Multiattack.*** Two Blunted Longsword strikes (three at T4).
- ***Blunted Longsword.*** *Melee:* +7 (T2) / +11 (T4), reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 9 (1d10 + 4) bludgeoning, nonlethal (T4: 2d10 + 5).

**Reactions**
- ***Read the Feint.*** When Ash makes a Cunning Strike against the sergeant, the sergeant has advantage on the save (once per bout).

**Tactics:** textbook. Won't chase. Respects anyone who fights smart. (Sergeant **Ruth Dane**, NPC-321, from Hobb's Ferry, runs the Yard; use this block for her.)

### Skill contests (`00-index.md` §11; CHG-21)
- **The Hiring Yard Trials** (archery and knife-throwing, T2; any morning): the Grey Wolf Company tests would-be hires on the butts: the archery short and long rounds (§11.4) and the knife board (§11.3). **No entry fee.** Top score of the day gets **50 gp** and a **contract offer** (one job, 100–300 gp, the Company's terms). Rivals: Company archers (+7 ranged), a mountain hunter (+8 long range).
- **Escape Artist's Chains** (`§11.6`, T2; the Company stockade, Restday): Company manacles (DC 15), a grain sack (Acrobatics 14), the punishment box (Thieves' Tools or Athletics 15). Fastest out wins **80 gp** and the sergeants' grudging respect. Ash brought his own manacles; the sergeants think that's hilarious.

## 4. Street fights

Inside the walls: fists only, anywhere but the Wolf Hall's doorstep (where it's a duel and needs a sergeant to witness). The **local trick: snow.** A creature shoved prone in the street is half-buried: it takes an extra 5 ft of movement to stand, and **disadvantage on Dex saves** until it does. Use the Street Tough block (T2), with Wolf off-duty soldiers at **AC 15**.

## 7. Things worth stealing

- **The contract ledger** proving the Company took Brannoc's coin (`world/03`: from Act II, the Wolves are hired by a Calm cutout to hold the pass). It's in the Wolf Hall's contract chest. **Very High** security (DC 19, Alarm 2; no steel inside the Hall). **Ch10 owns this**; the chapter wins.
- **A sergeant's pay chest**: Medium security, 400 gp. Stealing from the Wolves is stealing from the people who keep their word. Ulric hunts the thief personally, and he's very good at it.
**Fence: Vonn** (NPC-337), at **30%**, no questions, and never anything stolen from a Wolf.

## 8. Rivals

- **Captain Merrick Dunstan** (NPC-339; aka Merik, Dunstun): a mercenary captain (not a Grey Wolf; a free company called **the Red Hands**) who keeps showing up on the other side. Courteous, ruthless, never personal. He appears in Ch10 on the Calm cutout's payroll if the chapter wants another face, and again in Act III, where he can be **hired away** (Persuasion DC 18, plus 500 gp more than the other side pays). If the party ever saves his life, he switches sides for free and won't say why.
- **Gert Volker** (NPC-148), if he left Copperhill in disgrace: he signs on with Dunstan.
