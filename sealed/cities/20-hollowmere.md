# Hollowmere and the Rennet Otter-Rafts (Mirrowen): Side-Activity Menu (SEALED — DM ONLY)

> **Hollowmere** (aka Hollow Mere). Hare-folk warren-towns under meadow-hills around a still, round lake: the lake is the Mirrowen side of the **Ford Pool** gate. Mirrors Emberford. Lark's home. The **Rennet otter-rafts** are the otter-folk raft-clans that work the river below it (like Eyrland's Driftmarket).
> **Government:** **the Warren Mothers**, a council of eldest mothers. **Eldest: Mother Burdock** (NPC-019; aka Burdoc), Lark's grandmother. Under occupation (Act I–II), the Mothers hide in the deep burrows; Brannoc's banners fly over the warrens.
> **When visited:** Ch3 (the Ford Pool's far side, glimpsed), **Ch6** (`chapters/act1/ch06.md`: the crossing; Hollowmere under occupation; the Free Clans; Mother Burdock; Fight 5, the Hollowmere Patrol — no side-activity window, "this is the rescue"), **Ch14** (`chapters/act3/ch14.md`: Hollowmere's free warrens, the route to Saffra and Tuskhold). **Tier: T1 (occupied) and T3 (freed).**
> **The chapter wins.** Ch6 has no side window by design. This file is mostly for **Ch14 and later**, when Hollowmere is free and the party has time: the warren-market, hare-folk contests, otter-raft fun, odd jobs and the town by act.
> **IDs:** this file's provisional block is used up; new characters here are **Tier 3** (one line in `truth/07` if they recur).

## 1. Hollowmere at a glance

- **Look:** rolling meadow-hills dotted with round doors and windows (the warrens), smoke from chimneys poking out of the grass, a still round lake in the middle with a **stone ring on an island** (the Hall of Two Rivers' Mirrowen door, `world/05`), flower-meadows everywhere, and willows that lean over the water exactly the way the Bend's willows do (Ash notices; it's eerie and comforting).
- **Sound:** hare-folk singing (they're wonderful singers; everything is sung: chores, arguments, directions), bees, the lake's silence (unnatural under occupation; *held*, after).
- **Feel:** quick, nervous, brave in bursts. Hare-folk hug strangers who save their children, and they hug for a long time.
- **Under occupation (Ch6):** grey on the meadow-edges, Horned patrols, the warrens shuttered, nobody singing. **Freed (Ch14+):** the singing's back, all of it, at once, all the time.

### WARREN BOXER (a hare-folk spring-boxer)
*Small Humanoid (hare-folk), Chaotic Good* · **AC** 16 · **Initiative** +6 · **HP** 79 · **Speed** 40 ft. · **CR** 7 (PB +3)
*Tuned for party level 14 (Ash alone; ring DPR 26, HP 101).*
- ***Spring.*** The boxer's long jump is 30 ft and high jump 15 ft, without a run-up.
- ***Multiattack.*** Two Kicks and one Box.
- ***Kick.*** *Melee:* +9, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 1d8 + 5 bludgeoning, nonlethal; the target is pushed 5 ft.
- ***Box.*** *Melee:* +9, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 1d6 + 5 bludgeoning, nonlethal.
- ***Round-2 Turn: "Up!"*** At the start of round 2, the boxer leaps straight up and comes down on Ash: **DC 16 Dex save** or 2d8 and prone.
- ***Reaction: Hop Back.*** When targeted by a melee attack, the boxer hops 10 ft away without provoking; the attack has disadvantage (once per round).

## 4. The Rennet otter-rafts

The otter-folk raft-clans on the Rennet below Hollowmere: bargemen, fishers and jokers, natural friends to river humans (`world/05`). They're who carries the party to Saffra by water in Ch14 (two days by otter-raft). Plish (NPC-159), if freed in Ch7, is here with his family by Ch14, and they will not let the Fenwicks pay for anything.
**Raft fun (no rolls needed, or light ones):**
- **Swim races:** Athletics against an otter-folk champion (+11). They cheat (they tickle). Win: a fish, presented with great ceremony.
- **The otter-slide:** a mud-slide down the bank into the river. No roll. Just slide. Lark goes forty times (Ch8's joke, home edition).
- **Fish-tickling:** **Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) DC 15** to catch a fish by hand. Ash's Sleight of Hand is famous among the otters by the end of the day.
- **Raft-songs:** the otter-folk sing the same lake-songs as the Drift's river-clans (`05-driftmarket.md` §2.2), in Mirrowen. Zeke notices the verses match. That's a lovely, small, true thing about the two realms.

## 6. Betting, stealing, rivals

- **Betting:** hare-folk bet on everything in buttons, and lose cheerfully.
- **Things worth stealing:** nothing in Hollowmere (they'd give it to you). Before Ch6's rescue: **Horned patrol orders** (Low security) — Ch6 may use them.
