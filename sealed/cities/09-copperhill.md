# Copperhill: Side-Activity Menu (SEALED — DM ONLY)

> **Copperhill** (aka Copper Hill). A town of about 5,000 in the Kettlehorn foothills at the head of barge navigation. Copper dust turns everything green-orange.
> **Government:** **ruled by the winner of the yearly Champion's Bout.** Every Haymonth (the 15th), anyone may challenge the sitting **Champion** in a three-round bout in the town square. The winner holds **the Champion's Seat** for a year: they judge disputes, set the copper tax and lead the militia. **Current Champion: Hilde Strand** (NPC-064), grey-braided, in her fifties, four-time champion, fair and tired, looking for a worthy successor. **Law:** the **Champion's Hundred** (militia).
> **When visited:** Ch10 (`chapters/act2/ch10.md`: Fight 13 is the Champion's Bout; the side window has Gert Volker's challenge, copper-weight lifting, the miners' drinking contest and clearing the abandoned mine). **Tier: T2 (T4 notes for a return).**
> **The chapter wins.** This file adds shops, the year in town, what happens if a Fenwick holds the Seat, odd jobs, rivals and a T4 return.

---

## 1. The town at a glance

- **Look:** a steep town of slate and green-stained stone climbing a hill above the river; smelter chimneys in a row like organ pipes; the Square at the top, a bare-earth ring with stone seats; miners' cottages (the Pick Rows) stacked like steps; the barge-head at the bottom, where copper ingots go downriver.
- **Sound:** the smelters' roar, mine bells, loud miners, cheering whenever two people square up for any reason.
- **Feel:** a town that loves a fight and respects the winner, whoever it is. Rough, fair, funny.
- **Districts:** the **Smelter Ward**, **Barge Head**, **the Square**, **the Pick Rows**.

## 3. The Champion's Bout and the Seat

**Ch10 Fight 13** is the full bout. Notes for play around it:
- **The Seat for a year.** If a Fenwick wins (most likely Gideon; possibly Ash), they rule Copperhill until next Haymonth. It's big, funny and real (`world/03`). They can't actually live here and save the world. So:
  - They appoint a **Seat-Warden** to act for them. Hilde, if asked, will do it, and enjoy it, and send a letter each month asking about disputes she wants their ruling on (comic relief by message-moth or bird).
  - **Monthly ruling letters** (optional table fun): one dispute a month, written in two lines. The player rules on it. Log the rulings in the Campaign Bible. In Act III, if the rulings were fair, the **Champion's Hundred** marches to the Ch17 muster under the Fenwick colors (40 militia, T4 Street Tough block with spears).
  - **The copper tax:** the Champion sets it. Lowering it makes the miners love them; Penk grumbles; the town is fine.
  - **The challenge returns:** next Haymonth (YF 1105, after the campaign's likely end), someone will challenge. That's an epilogue line.
- **Hilde's mentorship:** if Gideon wins, Hilde becomes an older-sister/mentor figure to him for the rest of Act II (no romance). She teaches him one fighting trick (story; if the player wants mechanics, Gideon gains advantage on one Athletics check per day to shove or grapple).

## 5. The Square (sanctioned bouts) and street fights

**Ring formats (`00-index.md` §5.1, CHG-19):** the Square's yearly Fist fight (Ch10) is its own scene (Ch10's wasters rule already fits **Touches/Last-Standing**). Ordinary sanctioned bouts offer **Bare-Knuckle** (the miners' choice) or **Ring-Out on the pit-head beam** over a slurry pool. No magic in any format: no smites, no spells, no Wild Shape, no familiars. Dexterity fighters can win here.

The Square is always open for sanctioned bouts: any two people can fight there with a Hundred-man as referee. Ring rules (`00-index.md` §5) with two changes: **gauntlets allowed** (Copper Cuffs work), and **the crowd can throw a fighter back in** once per bout (a fighter pushed out of the ring is shoved back in prone instead of losing, the first time).
**Opponents:** Copperhill miners (use the Street Tough block, T2, solo, with +20 HP), **Gert Volker** (NPC-148; Ch10 block), and Hilde herself (Ch10 block; she'll spar with a friend any morning for nothing, nonlethal).
**Street fights:** nobody brawls in the street when the Square is right there. A street fight in Copperhill is a scandal; everyone stops and walks the fighters up to the Square to do it properly.

## 8. Things worth stealing

- **The Champion's Belt** (a dare, not a theft): **Stealth DC 16, Sleight of Hand DC 16** from the Champion's hall. Returning it publicly the next morning wins the town's laughter and a free drink everywhere. Keeping it is theft from the town (the Hundred will come).
- **Gert Volker's purse** (if he cheated with the weighted glove in Ch10): 80 gp. Nobody will mind.
**Fence:** none. Copper's too heavy to fence anyway.

## 9. Rivals

- **Gert Volker** (NPC-148): the swaggering mercenary who wants the Seat (Ch10). If he loses and leaves, he resurfaces at Wolfsgate (`10-wolfsgate.md`) hiring on with the other side.
- **The Pick Rows' champion, "Little" Wat Corby** (no ID; Tier 3): a miner who challenges every visitor to arm-wrestle (Athletics +7). Beat him and he follows the party around for a day like a puppy.
