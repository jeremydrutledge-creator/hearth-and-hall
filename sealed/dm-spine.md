It **points** to the full files. It doesn't replace them. When this file and a full file disagree, the full file wins (chapters > campaign > world). Log the fix as a CHG.

## 0. The story in six lines

1. Ash (20) hears a frightened hare-folk girl, **Lark** (NPC-004), calling across still water from **Mirrowen**, the beastfolk realm. He's the Eyrland **Hearkener** (Hearing half, SEC-02). Nobody else hears it.
2. **Moss** (NPC-003) is **Mossoran the Hart**, an ancient Warden who carried Ash out of the fire that killed his parents in YF 1085 (SEC-01). He has been veiling Ash's gift for 19 years.
3. Ash's **twin, Wren**, didn't die. She's **Clara Grane** (NPC-005, Voice half), raised by **Hollis Grane** (NPC-006), the kind old Enchanters' Guildmaster who ordered the fire and leads the **Calm** (SEC-03, SEC-04).
4. **Brannoc the Horned Regent** (NPC-008) conquers Mirrowen and keeps Lark silent, because the **Grey Heron, Vessimer** (NPC-009), a corrupted Firstbeast, promised his Hushed calves were "cured" (SEC-07, SEC-08).
5. Both men serve **Omeruth, the Stillness** (ENT-01), sealed under the **Drowned Gate** in the Glass. Glimmer mining thins the seal from above (SEC-05). Silencing the Hearkener thins it from below.
6. **Rescue (Act I), then protect (Act II), then save two worlds (Act III).** Three endings, chosen by Ash's choices.

**Tone check:** classic high fantasy. No demons, devils or hells, no fae courts, no romance with female allies. Companions die only by the dice. Only two scripted deaths (§5).

## 5. The two planned deaths (loss level B)

1. **Hester Grane (NPC-007), Lantern Night, Ch13.** She finally looks, sees the truth, gets the Lamp House children out of Old Ford and holds the chapel door.
   - **Preconditions:** her vouches in Ch5 and Ch7, and the Ch11 eye-fleck moment, have been played (or their routes used).
   - **The one route to save her:** Ash goes to Lamp House instead of the Guildhall cellars. Then the Reserve isn't dispersed: SEAL +3, not +1, and Hollis escapes un-reached. **Make the choice clear and unbearable. Don't hide it.**
   - **Her death breaks Hollis.** That's why he can be reached at all.
2. **Grandmother Umbari (NPC-021), siege of Tuskhold, Ch16.** She gives the full Sundering song memory-to-memory to Ash, Clara and Lark, and it costs her life. It's her calm choice. **Tactics can't prevent it.** If Ash refuses, she does it in the night and leaves him a message. Without it, Ending 2 is impossible.
   - **Seeds:** Ch8 (Lark: "Gran Umbari's stories"), Ch14 ("I have been remembering this for you"), Ch15 (failing, asks for a song).

**Not on the list:**
- Gideon, Zeke and Moss die by the dice only.
- Lark, Clara, Rook and the Willow Lane cast can be threatened, hurt, Hushed and woken, but **never killed by script.**
