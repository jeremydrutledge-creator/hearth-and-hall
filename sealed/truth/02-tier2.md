# 02 · Tier 2 Entries (SEALED — DM ONLY)

> Two or three lines each: who they are, what they hide, where they matter. Promote anyone to Tier 1 (full block) if they start carrying plot. Alternate spellings are in parentheses. FAC IDs are from `06-factions.md`.

---

## Willow Lane and the Fringe (FAC-05)

- **NPC-026 · Pip Carter** (Pipp, Pip Cartter), 9. Bess's son. Idolizes Ash and suspects the doorstep gifts are his. Lark's first human best friend (Ch7). Sneaks onto a Driftmarket barge to reach the gathering (Ch20). Edges: R-020, R-067.
- **NPC-027 · Nell Carter** (Nel, Nelly), 5. Bess's daughter. Shy and always cold. The natural first Row gift is a Hearthglow blanket (Ch2–4), and she's found warm under it in the flood.
- **NPC-028 · Martha Pratt** (Marth, Mother Pratt). Rook's mother, sick with lung-rot. Treating her (EVT-55) is the strongest lever on Rook's redemption. Proud; won't take charity from a Fenwick unless it's disguised. Edge: R-039 (target).
- **NPC-029 · Hugh Dale** (Hew, Huey Dale), young Fringe watchman (FAC-04). By-the-book, secretly admires Ash. Useful for "the Watch is coming" warnings. Edge: R-036.
- **NPC-030 · Old Bram** (Bramm, Bram the ferryman). A local hero who pulled forty people from the Great Flood of YF 1079. Runs a ferry-barge from the Bend. Default ride for the Ch7 night escape. Kept alive by design.

## Emberford: the Concord and the guilds (FAC-01, FAC-02)

- **NPC-031 · Provost Margery Teague** (Margie, Marjorie Teeg). Bargemen's Guildmaster, Provost of the Concord. Gruff, honest, distrusts "lamp people." Up for re-election at Harvest. Becomes a real ally if the party saves the city in public (Ch13, Ch17). Edge: R-091.
- **NPC-032 · Osric Vane** (Ozrick Vain, Oswald). Merchants' Guildmaster. Wants to break the Enchanters' glimmer monopoly for profit. Hires the party off the books (Ch5, Ch9). The right ally for the wrong reasons. Edge: R-090.
- **NPC-034 · Dorcas Fell** (Dorkas, Dorcus). Lamplighters' Guildmistress. Depends on the Enchanters. Can be turned for Lantern Night (Ch13), if shown proof.
- **NPC-035 · Big Tam Hollin** (Tam Hollen, Tom Hollin). Smiths' Guildmaster, Gideon's sometime employer. Honest and loud. Votes for the truth in Ch17. His smiths smash lamp chains in the Commons (Ch13).
- **NPC-036 · Widow Agnes Brack** (Agness, Brak). Brewers' Guildmistress; runs half the taverns and sponsors festivals. Do not confuse her with Aggie Thorne. Sponsors the Bargerace purse.
- **NPC-037 · Lionel Hartley** (Lyonel, Hartly). Weavers' Guildmaster, Crownhill money, fashion. A Concord swing vote (Ch17): vain, and moved by public opinion.
- **NPC-038 · Garrick Stone** (Garrik, Gary Stone). Masons' Guildmaster. Knows every tunnel under the city, which makes him a route into the Guildhall cellars (Ch13).
- **NPC-039 · Doctor Prudence Ames** (Prue, Prudance Aims). Physicians' Guildmistress. Licenses healers and would love to shut down Moss's shop. A recurring nuisance; a side hook about the Green Jar's license.
- **NPC-042 · Chancellor-Professor Imelda Rusk** (Imelda Rusck, Rusk). Head of the Lyceum (FAC-17). Proud, money-minded, decent. Controls access to the crooked tower's sealed mural.
- **NPC-047 · Madame Ottoline Brightwell** (Otteline, Brightwel). Brightwell & Daughters, Crownhill. Glossy glimmer-fashion, snobbish until you have money. A heist target and a Crownhill gossip line.

## Emberford: the Watch and the Calm (FAC-03, FAC-04)

- **NPC-033 · Captain Roderick Hale** (Rod, Roddy Hale, Rodrick). Captain of the Ford Watch. Practical, tired, takes small bribes for small things, won't sell out a murder. **Vouches sincerely for Sgt. Mabry (vouch #6, R-057).** Edges: R-057, R-058, R-059.
- **NPC-040 · Professor Cyril Doone** (Sirril, Cyrel, Dune). Lyceum professor who teaches Zeke's resonance seminar. Calm inner circle. Grooms Zeke for the circle. Can be caught and interrogated (SEC-04 dig-early). Edge: R-061.
- **NPC-041 · Mine-Warden Orla Venn** (Orlah, Ven, Orla Fenn). Runs the Kessel Workings. Half-Hushed devotee who knows Hollis's name. Mini-boss of Ch5 with glimmer-light attacks. Edge: R-060.

## Emberford: the Wharves and the Row (FAC-18, shops)

- **NPC-043 · Mother Brine** (real name Bethany Rourke; Beth, "Brine," Mother Bryne). Mother Brine's, the sailor's potion shop. Wooden leg, foghorn voice, tough love. Can treat Martha Pratt. Her sailors help on Lantern Night.
- **NPC-044 · Hob and Arn Tolley, "the Tolley brothers"** (Tolly, Toley). Debt collectors for the Merchants' Guild who freelance for Ma Garrow. Hunt Thistle in Ch1. Can be won over, and own a smuggling skiff (Ch7 Route B).
- **NPC-045 · Jory Tolliver** (Jorrie, Tolliver, Jorey). Odd Lot, the junk-magic pawnshop on Market Steps. A sly half-elf fence with a gap-toothed grin. Ash's main fence.
- **NPC-046 · Master Ambrose Webb** (Ambros Web, Ambrose Weeb). The Patient Owl. An ancient, glacially slow scholar who won't sell to the impatient. Sells scrolls; tests the patience of whoever asks.
- **NPC-048 · Nim Gearwright** (Nimm, Nim Gearwrite). The Clockwise, a young gnome tinker whose demos sometimes explode. Zeke's favorite shop.
- **NPC-049 · Hakon Stonebrow** (Haakon, Hakkon). The Iron Kettle. A dwarf smith in exile by choice who hates glimmer and keeps the Oath (SEC-11 dig-early: he'll break it for Gideon after Ch10).
- **NPC-050 · Old Wat Ember** (Watt, Wat Embers). Hearthglow on the Row: warmth items. Nell's blanket, Aggie's mug.
- **NPC-051 · Sister Rosalind Fairweather** (Roz, Rosalyn). The Dry Cloak: weather items. Gideon's cloak (Ch7).
- **NPC-052 · Jem Tapper** (Jemm, Jem Taper). Bottomless Barrel: food and drink items. Gideon's never-empty flask.
- **NPC-053 · Widow Elspeth Marsh** (Elsbeth, Elspith). Keepsake & Locket: memory items. Clara's locket (Ch11), Moss's compass (Ch13).
- **NPC-054 · Corwin Lockley** (Corwen, Lockly). Lightfinger: "stage magician" items. Ash's natural shop. Pip's lucky coin.
- **NPC-055 · Dagny Coalbeard** (Dagney, Coalbeerd). Quickhand Tools. The only dwarf who uses glimmer, and ashamed of it. Seeds SEC-05 and SEC-11 (Ch2, Ch10).
- **NPC-056 · Tobias Neat** (Toby, Tobias Neet). Small Mercies: charity and healing items sold at cost. Hester's supplier; grieves her after Ch13.
- **NPC-078 · Tansy** (Tansey), 8, a Lamp House orphan who hums Ash's tune without knowing where she heard it (world/02 seed). Runs to find the party on Lantern Night (Ch13 ★4 Route B).

## Mirrowen (FAC-07, FAC-08)

- **NPC-057 · Russet Tamsworth** (Rusty, Tamsworthe, Russett). Fox-folk field captain of the Free Clans. Clever, tired, distrusts humans. Won over by the rescue (Ch6). Edges: R-072, R-073.
- **NPC-058 · Captain Hoard** (Hord, Horde). Minotaur loyalist, warden of Stillwater Keep. Respects courage. Boss of Ch6 (light legendary). Edge: R-085.
- **NPC-059 · Ama** (Amma, Amah). Brannoc's daughter, a minotaur calf, Hushed in the Quiet Pens (SEC-18). The ribboned calf of Ch6. Woken in Ch15 (EVT-29). Edge: R-079 (target).
- **NPC-060 · Borra** (Bora, Borrah). A bear-folk conscript, Hushed, who blundered through the Ford Pool in Ch3. If woken, a scared, grateful source: "the Regent took the little Hearkener to the Glass Mere." First Mercy point.
- **NPC-061 · Saffi-of-the-Lamplight** (Saffy, Saffie). Saffra's tabaxi Tale-Keeper. Collects stories about "the boy who hears"; an information broker. Arranges the Ch14 truce.
- **NPC-079 · Moth-of-the-Long-Dusk** (Moth). A Hushed tabaxi tracker who leads Brannoc's hunters on the Ch7 siege. Can be woken later (Ch15+) and becomes a scout.

## The wider realm

- **NPC-062 · Granny Wenna Tull** (Wena, Gran Tull). Eldest Mother of Driftmarket (FAC-09), nearly ninety, never leaves *The Patient Goose*. Remembers "the Stag who never grows old." Shelters Lark (Ch8). Edge: R-089.
- **NPC-063 · Dame Constance Abernathy** (Connie, Abernethy). First Purse of Tollhaven (FAC-15). Sharp, cheerful, ruthless, collects clocks. Owns the Sundering-era astrolabe. Will hand over the Merrow ledger for a deal (Ch9 Route B).
- **NPC-064 · Hilde Strand** (Hilda). Copperhill's Champion, a grey-braided ex-mercenary in her fifties, looking for a worthy successor (Ch10). An older-sister/mentor type; no romance.
- **NPC-065 · Captain-General Ulric Fane** (Ulrich, Ulrik). Grey Wolf Company (FAC-16). Keeps contracts to the letter, never past it. Holds Bearback Pass (Ch10); parleys at half strength.
- **NPC-066 · King Torvald Deepanvil** (Thorvald, Tor). Dun Morrach (FAC-11). Old, stubborn, honorable. Breaks the Oath of the Quiet Stone for the Bear's chosen (EVT-17). Edge: R-086.
- **NPC-067 · Princess Brynja** (Brinya, Brynya). Torvald's granddaughter and heir. Warrior-engineer, older-sister ally to Gideon, no romance. Joins in Ch10 (EVT-53). Edge: R-087.
- **NPC-068 · Queen Amarys** (Amaris, Ah-MAR-iss). Aranhollow (FAC-12). Has reigned four centuries. Knows everything and chose neutrality (SEC-15). Moved only by proof (Ch17). Holds Moss's last shrine piece. Edge: R-088.
- **NPC-069 · King Edric II** (Edrick, Edric), 14. The boy-king in Highmarch (FAC-10). Hears faint things at the castle well and dreams of a grey lake. Not a Hearkener. Calm remnants try to kill him (Ch17, optional Fight 30).
- **NPC-070 · Lord Chancellor Anselm Crane** (Ansel Crain). Runs the realm for Edric. Careful and colorless, not wicked. Guards the Royal Archive's Fenn-wyck Charter.
- **NPC-071 · Lady Governor Maud Penrose** (Maude, Pen-rose). Saltreach. Ex-navy captain, white-haired, one-eyed, fair, short of money and patience. A mentor figure in Ch19; no romance.
- **NPC-072 · Captain Jago Trelawn** (Yago, Jago Trelawne). Smuggler-king of the Lanternless (FAC-13). Has seen "the shell under the hills" (SEC-12). Ship for Ch19 if Ash wins his respect.
- **NPC-073 · Dabble Quickwhistle** (Dabbel). Bramblecog's reluctant Lot-Mayor, a nervous cheesemaker. Hit by Ch8–9 minor-gate raids.
- **NPC-074 · Constable Fenna Hobbs** (Fena, Hobs). Bramblecog's single constable, with clockwork alarm geese.
- **NPC-075 · Old Nan Pike** (Nann). Ferrywarden of Hobb's Ferry, first stop on the King's Road. Knows everything that crosses.
- **NPC-076 · Tobin Pike** (Tobyn). Nan's grandson, runs the ferry. Possible Bargerace teammate (Ch4 Route A).
- **NPC-077 · Mother Hilary Vance** (Hillary). Abbess of the Abbey of the Still Lamp at Corriemount. Gentle, unshakeable, and genuinely good. A Calm faction works inside her abbey without her knowledge (a side thread for Act II).

## Historical and dead (no NPC ID needed at the table)

- **HIST-01 · Tom Fenwick** (Thom). The brothers' father. Bargeman and ford-pilot. Died in the barge fire, YF 1085.
- **HIST-02 · Elinor Fenwick** (Eleanor, Ellie). The brothers' mother, the previous Hearkener. Singer and seamstress. Sang the twins the lullaby (a fragment of the Sundering song). Died in the fire.
- **HIST-03 · Wynne Fenn-wyck** (Win, Fenwick). The first Hearkener, who sang the Sundering with the four Firstbeasts. Moss's oath was to her.
- **HIST-04 · Ursk, the Mountain Bear** (Ursc, Ursek). A Firstbeast. Died holding the mountain gate 400 years ago. His echo marks Gideon (Ch2, Ch10).
- **HIST-05 · Clara Grane (the elder) and Edmund Grane** (Ned). Hollis's wife and son, dead of river fever in YF 1079. Clara Grane the younger is named after his wife.
