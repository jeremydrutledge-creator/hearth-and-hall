## Moss: what the sheet doesn't say
- Mechanically he is a **Circle of the Moon druid at the party's level**, and the player sees only that. His "Human" species on the sheet is his cover. Under the hood he's using human-equivalent traits as a stand-in. Do not reveal this.
- **Wild Shape lean (seed for SEC-01):** whatever beast he picks, narrate it with antlers, moss or a forest look where it fits: an antlered elk instead of a plain one, a bear with moss in its fur, a wolf with lichen-grey markings. Don't explain it. If the player picks his form, honor the choice and add the look.
- **Story upgrades** (from `campaign/02` and `truth/01`): #1 Ch3 speak with animals and plants at will (no slot) and advantage to sense Wellgates · #2 Ch6 the Hart form at the Glass Mere shore · #3 Ch10 Bearback shrine piece · #4 Ch16 permanent antlers (cosmetic plus intimidation) · #5 Ch17/19 last shrine piece. The **Hart** stat block is a chapter-writer task (`truth/_handoff-notes.md`). It's used for one or two rounds per tier, as a story moment, not a summon on demand.
- **He doesn't age.** Never describe him as aging between acts.
- **Ending 2 / Ending 3 costs** are in `campaign/04`. The sheet never hints at them.

## Gideon: what the sheet doesn't say
- The oath comes at **L3, in story, at the Ford Stone vigil** (Ch2 ★5; fallback Ch3). Until then, play him as "a paladin without an oath": he feels the pull, can't name it, and keeps a vigil "for luck" like Fenwick men before him.
- Holds SEC-09 and SEC-16. **Bearback (Ch10)** is his "second oath" moment. Give him a story-only boon there (a bear-shaped aura effect when Aura of Protection is up; dwarves kneel). No extra mechanics unless the chapter writer adds a specific one.

## Zeke: what the sheet doesn't say
- **Abjuration** was chosen because Zeke's sealed gift is to **unweave glimmer workings** (Ch12–13) and read Sundering-script (Ch3+). Spell Breaker (L10) and Counterspell (L5) make the unweaving feel earned at the table. Discovery moments in `campaign/02` say *when*. The sheet says nothing about it.
- His Crafter feat lets him make small gifts for Ash to give. Tie this to the Enchanters' Row gift engine.

## Ash: what the sheet doesn't say
- **The locket (SEC-23):** see `campaign/03` SEC-23. The sheet gives only the surface and his belief.
- His Hearkener gift has **no stat line** until the story gives one. Waking the Hushed is DC 15 Performance with the tune (the world file's Hushed template). Ash isn't proficient in Performance (+4 from Cha). That's deliberate: it should be hard early, and the locket token and Lark help later. At **Ch9**, when he opens his first gate, consider granting **Performance proficiency** as a story reward ("the song settles in him"). Log it as a CHG if you do.
- **Use Magic Device scrolls use Int (+1).** That's weak for Ash. If the player asks at L13, the DM may let him use **Charisma** instead (his Magic Initiate ability). That's a reasonable house tweak, and it needs the player's yes. Log it as a CHG.

## Consistency-pass rulings: story rewards and boons (CHG-15)
Add to the sheets **only when they happen at the table** (then update `/player/party-sheets.md`).
- **Ash, Ch9:** when he opens his first gate (EVT-16), offer **Performance proficiency** ("the song settles in him"). Waking DCs from tier 3 assume it.
- **Ash, Ch13 (Use Magic Device):** scrolls use Int by the house rule; if the player asks, the DM may allow Cha. Ch13 works either way.
- **Gideon, Ch8:** still-water and greywilt terrain effects don't affect creatures inside his Aura of Protection while he's conscious.
- **Gideon, Ch10 (EVT-17): Bear's Ward.** Once per long rest, when a creature in his aura would drop to 0 HP, he can use his reaction to have it drop to 1 HP instead.
- **Gideon, Ch16: leads an army.** Once per day, his aura covers the whole line out to 300 ft until the end of his next turn.
- **Gideon (optional, Copperhill):** Hilde Strand's trick, advantage on one shove or grapple Athletics check per day, if he wins the Seat.
- **Zeke, Ch17:** can Help on any Hearkener song check as if casting a verbal-only spell (no slot, no concentration). In the Ch20 finale he counts as a fourth voice "for the words" (advantage to one singer per round).
- **Zeke (optional, Bramblecog):** Tinker's Tools proficiency from Pernella Gearspring's lesson, if he lacks it.
- **Moss:** Tier 2 Hart story-form block in `chapters/act1/ch06.md` §9; upgrade #4 full ally block (Mossoran, the Moss-Crowned Hart) in `chapters/act3/ch16.md`. #4 replaces the once-per-tier round.
- **Blessings (Act III):** Blessing of the Hall (Ch17 route A: each Hearkener who sang there has advantage on the first song check of each encounter); Vessimer's Blessing (Ch18, if redeemed: each party member one reroll of a save against a Stillness effect); Ommu's Blessing (Ch19: Water Breathing and a swim speed equal to walking speed). None stacks with another source of the same benefit.
