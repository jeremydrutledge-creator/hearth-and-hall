## CR-A1-01 · Waking the Hushed in Act I (clarifies CHG-01) — **needed**

**The conflict:** `campaign/00` §2 (CHG-01) says Ash can wake Hushed creatures "one at a time (from Ch15)." But `campaign/02` Ch3 and Ch5 let him wake Borra (DC 15) and Hushed miners in Act I, `campaign/04`'s Mercy track rewards both, and `rules/02` §5 lists waking as a hope lever at every tier. Meanwhile Walt Finch (Hushed in Ch4) "can only be woken after Ch12's duet or Ch15's discovery" (`truth/01` NPC-013).

**The ruling the Act I files use:**
- **Half-Hushed** creatures (grey to the elbows or shoulders; hushed within about a season; "still in there") **can be woken from Ch1** by Ash's hum: Charisma (Performance) DC 15 / 17 / 19 / 21 by tier.
- **Fully Hushed** creatures (grey all the way, or gone under still water at a turn of light without struggling, or hushed for years) **need Ch12's twin duet or Ch15's discovery** (CHG-01's "from Ch15" applies to them).
- Ash senses which is which without a roll (Ch5's discovery moment: he can tell which miners are "still in there"). Walt, the Ch4 Bend-eels, the Ch6 Wall-Sleepers and Ama are fully Hushed.

**Proposed CHG:** *"CHG-## · build (chapters, Act I) · Waking the Hushed, early and late. CHG-01's 'from Ch15' applies to the fully Hushed. Half-Hushed creatures can be woken from Ch1 at the tier DC. Why: reconciles Ch3 Borra, Ch5 miners and the Mercy track with Walt's Ch12/15 timing. Touched: campaign/00 §2, world/05 (Hushed template note), truth/01 NPC-013 (Walt: fully Hushed), rules/_handoff-notes."*
