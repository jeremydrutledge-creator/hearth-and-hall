## 1. ★ Must-happen beats (with routes)

### ★1 · Thistle is taken (or nearly)
Lamp-wardens come for Thistle at the Green Jar, quietly, at night: *"an unlicensed animal."* Aggie Thorne fights them off with a broom and is hurt. **(EVT-43.)**
- **A. The party arrives in time for a fight** (Fight 3, §5). Masked lamp-wardens: professionals, not killers.
- **B. The party is away:** Thistle is taken. Ash *hears* her terror (the bond with Lark carries a little; Thistle is close to the Warren Mothers' song). He can track her to a Wharves warehouse that night: **Tracking Thistle** (§4), and Fight 3 runs there (Route B variant).
- **C. Thistle escapes on her own** and hides on the Fenwick roof. The lamp-wardens are waiting at the bottom of the Lane at dawn. Fight 3 runs on the Lane at dawn (Route C variant).

### ★2 · Hester vouches (vouch #1)
Ash brings the lamp-warden question to someone who knows Hollis. Hester Grane says, sincerely and warmly:
> *"My brother? Hollis would never send men after a frightened creature. Someone's using the Guild's name. I'll ask him myself."*

She does. He tells her a soothing lie, and she believes it. **(EVT-41. Ledger R-040: she BELIEVES he's the kindest man in Emberford; BLIND SPOT: his evening meetings; she WILL VOUCH for his kindness, sincerely and warmly.)**
- **A. Ash goes to Lamp House** (he delivered there in Ch2).
- **B. Zeke goes to Hollis's office at the Lyceum** (Hollis sits on its board), and Hester is there, and answers for him.
- **C. Hester comes to Willow Lane with bread after the flood**, hears about the lamp-wardens from Netta Lowe, and says it unprompted.

### ★3 · The glimmer trail
The party learns the Guild's glimmer comes from the **Kessel Workings** upriver, that volumes have doubled while the city's lamps haven't, and that Kessel workers come home "calm."
- **A. Steal or copy the Enchanters' Guild shipping manifests:** **The Manifest Job** (§4), a heist at the Guild warehouse on the Wharves.
- **B. Talk to Ma Garrow or the Tolley brothers:** a cousin of theirs came back from Kessel "grey round the eyes, wouldn't even fight."
- **C. Zeke feels it.** Glimmer *sings*, and the shipments from Kessel sing louder, and *wrong* (Zeke's discovery: **glimmer resonance**).
- **D. Thistle:** *"The stones in the Warren Mothers' songs, the singing stones. You're digging them up. That's why our water's going grey."*

### ★4 · Kessel Workings
The party goes upriver (half a day by barge) and gets inside: lamp-wardens, Hushed miners, a minor Wellgate at the heart of the quarry-cave, raw glimmer growing on the pool stones. They learn three things: **(1) the mine is on a gate, (2) the miners are being Hushed, (3) huge crates are going to "the Grand Reserve, Guildhall."** They also learn the way across: Moss says the Ford Pool will open fully at **Midsummer's Eve** sunset, the strongest turn of light of the year. **(EVT-05, EVT-06.)**
- **A. Infiltration:** disguised as miners, on a supply barge, or a night climb (**Into Kessel**, §4).
- **B. A job:** Osric Vane (NPC-032, Merchants' Guild) hires the party off the books to "find out what the Enchanters are hiding at Kessel."
- **C. Walt Finch used to work there** (if Hushed, Dora tells his stories for him); following the trail of what Hushed him leads there. **Or** the Mine-Warden hires Gideon as a guard: the Calm's way of looking him over.

### ★5 · Rook is bought
The Calm pays Rook to watch Ash (SEC-14). Show it from the outside: Rook suddenly has money, new boots, and is always *around*. **(EVT-42.)**
- **A. Ash notices Rook flush with coin at the Bucket.**
- **B. Netta Lowe gossips** that Rook paid off his mum's debts in silver.
- **C. Rook turns up at the Kessel Workings, or on the barge there,** "by coincidence."

## 2. Before the session

**What Hollis knows now (`campaign/00` §3B):** it's Blossom. He's heard that a hare-folk scout is in the city asking for "the one who hears." (From the Ch4 lamp-wardens' questions, a Watch clerk's note, the Bucket's gossip, or Ch2/3 consequences, if any moved it up.) **He does not yet connect it to Ash.** He thinks the "one who hears" is a Mirrowen story, and that the only Eyrland heir is Clara. He wants the hare-folk *off the street and quiet*. So he sends lamp-wardens with a license.

**The license:** a real Enchanters' Guild warrant, "for the seizure of an unlicensed glimmer-sensitive animal," countersigned at Watch headquarters by **Sergeant Giles Mabry** (NPC-023). It's legal. That's the point.

**The call (Ch5):** Lark can hear Ash at any still water at the turn of light now, words and all. She's getting greyer (her voice is thinner every week). She asks if he's coming. She tells him about a little calf in the courtyard with a ribbon who never moves (SEC-07 seed; don't explain). **If Ash promises to come, she says, "Promise on something." Let him choose what.**

**After Ch4:** check the Sealed GM Notes. Is Walt Hushed? Did the Willow burn? Is the Green Jar's stock lost? Did Ash help Rook? Is the family reconciled? Play all of it.

## 3. Scenes (Session 1: the city)

### Scene 1 · The night raid (★1)

**When:** a few nights into the chapter, at about the second hour after midnight. **Where:** the Green Jar, halfway up Willow Lane. Thistle sleeps in the room above the shop; Aggie sleeps in the back; Moss is home (he's asleep in his chair, unless the party has him elsewhere, in which case he's at the Ford Pool, holding).

**How the party learns of it (Route A):** Bart, on the Fenwick roof, wakes Ash: *"Ash. Ash. There's lamps on the Lane with no people holding them. Wait. There's people. Wait. There's a* dog*. I hate dogs."* Or Thistle's thin scream, carrying the tune (Ash hears it as he heard her in Ch1). Or Aggie's voice, loud enough to wake the dead: *"GET OUT OF MY SHOP."*

**Fight 3 (§5).** The wardens are there to take Thistle, not to kill. Aggie is hurt before the party arrives (she fought them alone for a minute with a broom): she's at 3 HP behind the counter, refusing to lie down.

**After the fight (whatever the result):**
- **Aggie is hurt** (EVT-43; R-013, R-032 move). A cracked collarbone and a cut scalp. She lets Moss splint it while cursing him by name. If Ash defended her shop, she says (with her teeth gritted): *"You did all right. Don't let it go to your head. Wipe your feet."* **Gift hook (`campaign/05` §4):** a Hearthglow mug that keeps tea hot. She'll pretend to hate it. She'll drink from it every day for the rest of her life.
- **The warden left behind** (if one was captured) has the warrant on him. **The warrant is real.** Countersigned **"G. Mabry, Sgt., for the Watch."** (SEC-21 seed.) The warden himself is a professional: he says nothing but his name (**Warden Abel Crouch**; Tier 3, see `_change-requests.md`) and "I want my Guild advocate." He is not Calm; he's just employed. He's ashamed of the dog.
- **Moss** (R-094: a good civic man who sells too much glimmer; he smells the Calm in still water but doesn't know whose) is cold with fury: *"They came into my house for a child."* He says he'll move Thistle somewhere safer. He doesn't say where. (Ash can insist on knowing; Moss tells him: "The Fenwick roof, if your brothers will have her. Nobody looks up.")

>
> *"My brother? Hollis would never send men after a frightened creature." She says it simply, the way you'd say the river runs downhill. "Someone's using the Guild's name. There's always someone. I'll ask him myself."*

- **If Ash pushes** (the warrant, the license): *"Then someone in his Guild signed it without asking him. It happens. He has three hundred people under him and a heart too big for any of them. I'll ask him tonight."*

### Scene 4 · The glimmer trail (★3)

Use whichever route(s) the party takes. More than one is fine and gives more detail.

#### Route A · The Manifest Job
The Enchanters' Guild's riverside warehouse on the Low Wharf: brick, barred, lamp-lit, two lamp-wardens on the door at night. The shipping manifests are in the clerk's office upstairs. Run **The Manifest Job** (§4). **What the manifests show** (Zeke or anyone with **Intelligence DC 12** can read the numbers; the world file's seed):
- Kessel output **doubled** in the last two years. The city's lamps haven't.
- Most of it isn't going to the Lamplighters or the Row at all. It's going in big sealed crates to **"Grand Reserve, Guildhall."**
- Every Reserve crate is marked with a little private sigil: **a lamp inside a circle.** (SEC-04 seed #2.)

#### Route B · The Bucket, or the Tolleys
- **Ma Garrow** (R-062; Ma's own arc lists "Ch5 a cousin came back from Kessel grey"): if Ash has her respect, she tells him quietly, cracking a walnut: *"My cousin Davy worked Kessel two seasons. Came home grey round the eyes. Sits in the window. Wouldn't even fight. Davy, who used to fight the river for fun."* She looks at Ash. *"You find out why, Fenwick, I'll owe you."*
- **The Tolleys** (if won over in Ch1): Hob says the same about a cousin of *theirs*: *"Went up the river for the good money. Came back like he was listening to something in another room."*
- **Either way:** the Kessel Workings, half a day upriver; the Guild pays triple wages; nobody who goes stays more than two seasons, and nobody comes back quite the same.

#### Route C · Zeke's resonance (discovery moment)
Zeke's been hearing glimmer as a "headache" since Ch2. At the Wharves (any reason: Mother Brine's, the Guild warehouse, a lamp-chip delivery), a crate of raw Kessel glimmer is being unloaded. Zeke stops dead.
> *"It's singing." He has both hands over his ears and it doesn't help. "All glimmer sings. I've always— it's a chord. But this is* louder*. And it's out of tune. It's like a choir with someone screaming in the middle of it. Ash. Where did that come from?"*
- **Arcana DC 13** (Zeke's +9): he can follow the "scream" to every Kessel crate on the Wharves. They all sing wrong. The city's ordinary lamp-chips (refined, older) sing right.
- **Discovery:** glimmer resonance (`campaign/05` §6). Zeke doesn't know what it means. He thinks something's wrong with *Kessel*. He's right.
- **R-007 (Zeke → Hollis):** if Ash says "Hollis's Guild," Zeke says at once: "Master Grane can't know about this. If he knew, he'd stop it. I'm going to tell him." **Ash decides** whether to stop him. If Zeke tells Hollis (a letter, or at the Lyceum), Hollis writes back a kind note thanking him for his "remarkable ear" and inviting him to "talk resonance over supper sometime." Professor Doone (NPC-040, R-061) hears about Zeke's ear the same week. (Raises the Calm's interest in *Zeke*. Good for Ch11. Note it.)

#### Route D · Thistle's singing stones
Thistle, on the Fenwick roof, watching the Glimmerway lamps come on: *"Those are singing stones. In your lamps. The stones in the Warren Mothers' songs, the ones round the old gates. You're digging them up. That's why our water's going grey."* She's angry, and frightened, and blunt. **SEC-05 seed.** Moss, if he's there, doesn't contradict her. He says only: "I'd rather not say, until I've seen where it comes from." (He knows. He wants to be sure *how much*.)

### Scene 6 · The Quiet's chapel (optional; SEC-04 seed)

**Where:** a rented chapel in Old Ford, three streets from the Ford Stone. Small, plain, spotless. A sign: *"The Quiet. A circle for the grieving. All welcome. Tea after."*

> *Inside it's very calm. A dozen people on benches, mostly older, mostly in mourning grey, sitting with their hands in their laps. Nobody talks. In the middle of the room, on a low table, sits a wide bronze bowl of water, perfectly still, and people take turns going to kneel by it and look in. There's a faint smell of lavender. Over the door, small and neat, is a painted sign: a lamp inside a circle.*

- **The sigil** is the same one on the Kessel crates. (SEC-04 seed #2. Let the player connect it. Don't.)
- **The bowl:** if Ash goes near it, he realizes something he's never noticed before. For the first time since Ch1, near still water, **he can't hear Lark at all.** Not faint: *gone*. Total, soft, perfect silence. It's peaceful. That's the frightening part. The moment he steps away from it, she's back, mid-hum, frightened: *"Where did you go? You went quiet. Don't do that."*
- **Sergeant Mabry** may be here (he comes most evenings). If he sees Ash, he nods politely and says, "Good. It helps."
- **Nobody here is wicked.** They're grieving. Some of them are a little grey round the eyes. One old woman (Tier 3) takes Ash's hand and says, "It doesn't hurt any more. Isn't that a mercy?"
- **Dig early:** the chapel can be infiltrated at night (Stealth DC 15, Thieves' Tools DC 15). Its little office holds a list of members (Mabry among them), a box of letters with the sigil, and nothing with Hollis's name on it. (`campaign/03` SEC-04: Hollis is careful.) Catching and questioning Mabry or Doone gives "the Keeper of the Still Lamp leads us" and "the Keeper is a great man of the Guild," and not the name.

> *"You've come a long way to be angry. You needn't be. Look at them, out there. No more fear. No more grief. No more anything that hurts. The Keeper showed me. He'll show you, if you'll be still."*

**Enemy plan:** Orla keeps her distance on the platform and lances; the wardens hold the ladder; the foreman grapples whoever reaches the water. **Focus:** whoever's the biggest threat to the pool (Moss if he's near it; otherwise Zeke, because she can feel him *hearing* the glimmer).

#### PHASE 1 · rounds 1–2 · "The Warden's light" (Orla 150 → 100)
- Orla lances and uses Still Light. Wardens hold the ladder. The foreman wades.
- **Counter (telegraphed):** **Orla targets Zeke's Shield with saving-throw effects** (rules/02 §6). Round 1, when Zeke casts *Shield* or she sees the shimmer: she smiles and switches to **Still Light** (a Wisdom save, not an attack roll).
- **Telegraph for Phase 2 (when she drops below 100):** she plunges her bare left hand into the raw glimmer on the nearest stone, and it climbs her arm like frost, and she *lights up*.

#### PHASE 2 · trigger: Orla at 100 HP (~2/3) · **FORM change**
- **Orla Lit.** She glows from the inside; her eyes go white-blue. **Her AC drops to 14 and her speed rises to 40 ft; she gains a 10-ft aura of hard light** (difficult terrain for others; bright light, so no Hiding near her) and her Lamplight Lance deals +1d10 radiant. She leaps down from the platform (no fall damage; she floats the last 10 ft).
- **Glimmer Surge** (spike) as she changes: she bursts the seam nearest the largest group of PCs.
- **New tactics:** she fights in melee now, moving through the party, using Still Light on anyone who flanks her.
- **Telegraph for Phase 3:** the gate pool *bulges* in the middle, exactly as the Ford Pool did in Ch3. Moss (if here) goes white: "She's tearing it. She doesn't even know she's doing it."

#### PHASE 3 · trigger: Orla at 50 HP (~1/3), or round 5 · **OBJECTIVE change** (the pool)
- **The gate pool starts to tear.** The raw glimmer around it is singing so loud Zeke has a nosebleed. Orla, not knowing what she's doing, is pulling on it. **Lair action each round: the Tear** (as Ch3's Tear block, stage track, but starting at 1 and advancing only when the lair action picks Advance).
- **The objective:** stop Orla *or* steady the pool. **Ash's hum works here too** (**Charisma (Performance) DC 14** as an action within 10 ft of the water: the tear doesn't advance this round; it's a small gate, and it remembers being sung to). Moss can Hold (Wisdom DC 13) as in Ch3. **Two successful hums or holds** and the tear stops for the fight.
- **Orla's last resort:** at 0 HP she doesn't fall. She **walks into the gate pool**, smiling, and starts to sink, and the grey climbs her to the eyes. **Someone can grab her** (Athletics DC 13 as an action, within 5 ft of the pool's edge) or let her go under (she becomes fully Hushed; she doesn't drown; she stands at the pool's bottom, still, forever unless woken in Act II–III).

#### FINAL BEAT
- **Orla down, captured, or gone under.** The wardens surrender (they always do once she falls). The foreman miner stops and stands still.
- **Waking Orla:** she's **half-Hushed by choice** (a devotee, not a victim). **DC 17**. It *partly* works: the grey cracks and she screams, and weeps, and is herself, and is still the Keeper's to the bone. She won't betray him. She'll say "the Keeper of the Still Lamp" and "a great man of the Guild" (SEC-04 dig-early rule), and the sigil, and that's all. **If the party uses magic to compel the name** (*Zone of Truth* or similar), she says it: **"Hollis Grane."** Then follow `campaign/03` SEC-04's dig-early rule: **Hollis goes on the offensive early** (Ch11's assassination attempt moves up; Hester's next vouch becomes a confrontation; the fire ledger is still needed for proof). Don't block it.
