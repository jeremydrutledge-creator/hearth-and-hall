## Scenes

### S19.1 · Down the river (opening)
The last journey down the Tamber, past every place they've been: Tamber's towns, the Glasswater, Driftmarket's lanterns, Tollhaven's towers. Pick what matters:
- **Driftmarket:** the Seven Mothers come out on the Drift to see them pass, and Granny Tull shouts across the water: "We'll be at the delta when you need us! Don't you dare not need us!"
- **Pip** is found (if they took Driftmarket's barge or passed it) under a tarp with the Bargerace Cup. "For luck." Ash can send him home. He won't go. Lark sides with Pip, loudly.
- **Gideon and Ash** at the rail at night. Gideon: "Mum used to say you'd be the one who got us all into trouble." Ash's answer is the player's. Gideon, after a while: "She said you'd get us out of it too."
- **Zeke** works out the date on his astrolabe and puts it away without saying anything. Then he says: "Midsummer's Eve. Your birthday. That's when the keyhole opens." (The Ch20 clock.)

### S19.2 · Saltreach (★1)
Salt wind, gulls, tar, white houses with blue doors. The last city before the sea. Superstitious about the Glass: every door has a little bell on it, "so the quiet can't get in." At Lighthouse Point, the **Lady Governor Maud Penrose**: white-haired, one-eyed, unshakably fair, short of money, short of patience. She's read Crane's letters. She looks at them and says, "So you're the hearers. You're younger than the letters."
- **Maud** (older, wiser, mentor; proposed edge R-237). She gives the realm's best advice about the Glass in one sentence: *"Don't sail into it. If you must sail into it, don't stop. If you stop, sing."* She knew a sailor who stopped in the Glass. He's still there.
- **The Shambles and the Barnacle tavern:** where Jago's people drink. Fight 33 starts here if they go looking for the Lanternless without an introduction.
- **Smugglers' Stair:** a cliff-cut maze of cellars; the Lanternless's home ground.

### S19.3 · Captain Jago Trelawn (★1 B; SC 19-A)
Old, weathered, a gold earring, one hand missing two fingers. He's been ashore on Ommu's Rest once, thirty years ago, and seen "the shell under the hills." He hasn't sailed near the Glass since. He doesn't do favors for the Crown. He *might* for someone who earns it. SC 19-A.
- **What Jago says, once he trusts them** (SEC-12, the last seed): *"There's an island in the Glass that isn't on any chart but the old ones. Green hills, no birds. We put ashore for water. And I walked over a rise and the ground under the grass was *shell*. Plates the size of houses. And I put my ear to it, and it was breathing. Slow. One breath in an hour. I've never told anyone but my mother, and she said I was drunk. I wasn't drunk."*

### S19.4 · The rival: Hattie Slade
The smuggler-girl captain who's always one step ahead (`world/03`'s rival; older, sisterly, no romance). **Captain Hattie Slade** (NPC-195, provisional) is forty, sharp, quick, and runs the fastest lugger on the coast, the *Magpie's Daughter*. She's been beating the Navy to every salvage in the delta for twenty years. She's Jago's niece. She doesn't trust hearers, Crown letters or anyone younger than her.
- She's Fight 33's opposition if it's a brawl, SC 19-A's race opponent, and (if Ash earns it) she sails the *Magpie's Daughter* alongside them into the Glass. In Ch20, she's in the gathering's fleet, shouting at the Crown's captains.

### S19.5 · Out of the harbor (★2; SC#5 begins)
The morning they sail. The sea beyond the harbor mouth is glass. Every bell on every door in Saltreach rings once, by itself, as they pass the lighthouse. Maud (on the *Resolute*, or on the lighthouse) raises a hand. Then they're in it.

### S19.6 · The Glass (★3; SC#5; Fight 34)
No wind. No waves. No sound but the oars and the ship's own creaking. The water is a perfect mirror; the ship floats in sky. After a day, the reflection starts to lag a little behind the ship. After two, the reflected crew look *calmer* than the real ones. The Stillness is in their heads.
- **The Mirrowen side** (if Brannoc turned): on the second day, through the water, they see the Little Sea's reflection under them, and on it, the Horned Host's long-boats, rowing in mirror, a hundred oars in time, keeping pace. The crew cheers for the first time in two days.
- **Fight 34** comes up out of the mirror halfway across.

### S19.7 · Ommu's Rest (★4; Fight 35; SC 19-B)
An island of green hills and no birds, in the middle of a sea that's stopped. They land on a beach of grey shingle. Over the first rise, the grass is thin, and under it: shell. Plates the size of houses, ridged, ancient, warm. The island breathes: one breath in an hour, the ground rising a foot and falling. At the island's highest point, a great rounded hill that is the top of her shell, and on it, standing in a ring around something, the **Stillness's guardians**: Hushed sea-giants, grey and dripping, keeping her asleep. Fight 35. Then the songs.
- **Moss's last shrine-piece** (if Aranhollow didn't give it): a ring of mossy antler-wood grown into the shell at the hill's crown, where Mossoran left it eleven hundred years ago "to keep my sister company." He kneels and takes it back. Upgrade #5.
- **The keyhole:** from the hill, they can see it: a mile off the island's south shore, the Glass is *darker* in a circle half a mile across. Under it, deep down, a light that isn't light. The Drowned Gate. Ommu has been lying over it for eleven hundred years, holding it shut with her body.

### S19.8 · Ommu wakes (EVT-39; exit)
The three songs (SC 19-B). The island moves. The ground under them *lifts*, steadily, and keeps lifting: the shell rising out of the sea, the hills tilting, water pouring off the edges in white falls. A head the size of a church rises out of the sea at the island's edge, grey-green, crusted with eleven hundred years of coral and weed, and opens **one eye**, gold and enormous, and looks at the three Hearkeners. She speaks in a voice like the sea moving, which it hasn't done in a week: ***"Little ones. You sang it the way she did. Is it time?"***
- Then, to Moss (if he's there): ***"Mossoran. You've kept them a long time."*** And to Vessimer (if she's redeemed and there): ***"Sister. You came back."*** The Heron says nothing; she lands on Ommu's head and folds her wings.
- **Level 20.** The finale is tomorrow at sunset, on this shell, at the keyhole of the world's prison. It's Midsummer's Eve. It's Ash's birthday.

### FIGHT 33 · The Smugglers' Stair (or the Cistern Gate) · Ch19 · Ordinary · Party level 19

**Target numbers (L19):** total HP **440** · enemy DPR **90** (10% of effective HP 905) · AC 18–20 · attack +11 to +13 · DC 18–19.
**Two versions** (use whichever fits how the party approached Saltreach):
- **A. The Stair brawl** (they went looking for the Lanternless without an introduction, or got caught cheating at cards): Hattie Slade 150 · 5 Lanternless Bruisers × 58 = 290 → **440**. **Non-lethal.** Nobody draws steel unless the party does. Bar-brawl rules: anyone dropped to 0 by fists, chairs or the flat of a blade is unconscious, not dying.
- **B. The Cistern Gate** (★1 D, or curiosity): the lighthouse cistern's Wellgate is guarded. A Cistern Keeper (a Hushed sea-thing, recent, that came up through the gate) 240 · 4 Kraken Tentacles (from Fight 34's block, 50 each = 200) → **440**.
**HP math:** 157 × 3.5 ≈ 550 × 80% ≈ 440. **Expected 3–4 rounds.**
**DPR math (A):** Hattie (3 × 15 × 0.55 = 25) · Bruisers 5 × (2 × 12 × 0.55 = 13) = 65 → **~90** (non-lethal).

**Battlefield A: the Smugglers' Stair** (crop-friendly: a cliff-cut stair, 15 ft wide, zigzagging down 120 ft of chalk cliff in four flights; cellar doors along it; ropes and cargo-hoists; the harbor at the bottom).
1. **The stair.** Every flight is 30 ft long and 15 ft wide. Shoving someone off a flight is a 15-ft fall to the next.
2. **Cargo hoists.** Ropes over the cliff edge: swing from flight to flight (Acrobatics DC 15; Ash: no roll). Hattie loves them.
3. **The cellars.** Lanternless come out of them; the party can go into them.
4. **The barrels.** Rum, salt, contraband. Smash one for difficult terrain (slippery) in 10 ft.
**Enemy plan (A):** humiliate, not kill. Throw the party down the stair into the harbor. Hattie wants to see if they're worth her uncle's time.
**PHASE 1 · rounds 1–2 · "Down the stair."** Bruisers block; Hattie swings on the hoists and kicks.
**PHASE 2 · trigger: round 3 or three Bruisers down · "The harbor" (TERRAIN).** *Telegraph:* Hattie grins and cuts a hoist rope. A cargo net of barrels drops on the stair. The fight spills down to the quay and into the harbor (water: swimming, and the harbor's water is *still*, which scares the Lanternless more than the party does). **Counter (Bart):** *Telegraph:* Hattie's parrot, Admiral, goes for Bart. It's a dogfight in the air above the stair (no mechanics: Bart is busy for 2 rounds and can't Help; the commentary is extraordinary).
**FINAL BEAT.** Hattie yields when she's the last one up, or when one of the party does something she respects (Ash's Panache counts; so does Gideon picking a Bruiser up out of the still water and setting him on the quay). "All right. All *right*. Uncle'll want to see you." (SC 19-A: one automatic success.)
**Fail-forward.** The party's thrown in the harbor, wet and laughing at. Jago comes down the stair himself to fish them out: "Hattie, love, they're the *hearers*." SC 19-A starts with 1 failure.
