## ★ Must-happen beats

### ★1 · War is declared (EVT-27, SEAL +1)
Brannoc's herald, **Ronan Ironvoice** (NPC-182, provisional), speaks the declaration. It is *formal*, *honest* and frightening: *"Hear the Horned Regent, Tall Folk under the water. You have taken our song to light your lamps. You have taken the child who sings it. Our calves went grey while your streets went bright. We are coming for what is ours. When the thaw is done, the Host will come up through your wells. Every one. Make your peace with your lamps."*
- **A. At the Ford Pool,** delivered to Moss as "the Stag's keeper," at sunrise, the herald standing waist-deep on the Mirrowen side so that his head and horns show in the pool's surface like a reflection that speaks. He adds a line meant only for Moss: *"The Heron says: Brother, come home before the water rises."*
- **B. Through every still pool in Emberford at once,** at sunrise: the Lane Well, the horse troughs, the Bend's eddies, every rain-barrel on Willow Lane. Netta hears it at the well and rings the well-bell. Pip hears it in a washtub and runs straight to the Fenwick door. (Good at the table if Ash is in the city.)
- **C. Through Lark,** in terror, wherever she is: *"He's coming. All of them. The drums, Ash. I can hear the drums through the water."* She draws what she sees on a slate: rows of horns, a hill of standing stones, and a grey bird on the tallest stone.
- **D. (SEAL 7 path.)** The vanguard already holds the Fordwood side of the Ford Pool. The herald shouts the declaration across the clearing, under a white-and-iron banner, and the vanguard's captain offers a one-day truce "so the Tall Folk may choose their side." It's the same declaration, closer.
- Whatever the route, include the declaration's **one invitation**, because Brannoc is honest: *"If the Tall Folk have one among them who hears, let that one come under the Tale-Keepers' banner and say why we should not."*

### ★2 · Into Mirrowen
The party crosses to stop the war at its root.
- **A. Through the Ford Pool to Hollowmere** (S14.5). The Free Clans now hold the eastern warrens (they took them back over the winter while Brannoc gathered at Tuskhold). **Mother Burdock** (NPC-019) and **Russet Tamsworth** (NPC-057) meet them. From there it's four days by the Rennet's bank to Tuskhold, or two by otter-raft to Saffra.
- **B. Through the Tollhaven/Varga gate to Saffra.** Ash can open it (EVT-16). It surfaces in the flooded counting-house's mirror under Saffra's **Hall of Bells**. Saffi's people are waiting: they've watched that pool since Ch9.
- **C. Saffi-of-the-Lamplight's invitation** (NPC-061). A tabaxi trader comes up the drift-gate at Driftmarket (or an otter brings Granny Tull a letter): *"The Regent will parley with the boy who hears, at Saffra, under the Tale-Keepers' truce, on the night of the Great Telling. Come and be a story. — S."* It names a gate and a time, and she sends a guide (**Tesh Twelve-Bells**, NPC-181).
- **D. Through Lark.** If the party won't go, Lark will. She sets out alone for the Ford Pool at sunset ("You said you'd always come. So I'm going first."). Bess or Pip catches her packing. The party goes after her, and arrives in Hollowmere a few minutes behind her: route A, with a scared, stubborn eleven-year-old who's made her point.
- **Lark insists on coming** on every route (`campaign/01`: "Lark grows up fast; insists on her part"). Leaving her behind without telling her is the one thing she won't forgive (`truth/01` NPC-004). Taking her is dangerous and the right call; say so through Moss: *"She's not a parcel, lad. She's the other half of the song. Let her carry her half."*

### ★3 · Grandmother Umbari (seeds SEC-12)
The party reaches besieged Tuskhold, or meets Umbari under truce. She says: ***"I have been remembering this for you."*** She's old and failing.
- **A. Into besieged Tuskhold** through the lines (SC 14-A, Fight 22), and up to the Remembering Hall (S14.7).
- **B. Under truce in Saffra.** Umbari's granddaughter **Nkemba** (NPC-183) brings her down the Rennet on a barge under the Tale-Keepers' banner for the Great Telling. Brannoc lets the barge pass: he won't fire on the Rememberer (and Umbari knows it, and uses it).
- **C. Through Lark's dream-song.** If the party reaches neither, Lark (S14.8) finds Umbari asleep and brings her into Ash's dream: a grey plain, a stone city, an old elephant-woman sitting on a long stone. *"There you are. Come to me awake, child. I can't give it to you here."* (It moves the party toward route A or B; the meeting itself still happens in person, before the parley or at it: route D.)
- **D. At the parley.** Umbari comes to the truce herself, carried on a litter through the lines. She sits beside Ash, not beside the Regent. That's a lever in SC 14-C (it's automatic).
- **Her words (SEC-12 seed, always):** *"When I am gone, the Old Tortoise will need waking. I'll tell you how. Not yet. It's long, and I'm slow, and I'd like to see the spring."*

### ★4 · The parley (EVT-28)
Ash meets Brannoc under a truce banner. Brannoc is honest: the Tall Folk *were* stealing their song. Ash can show proof (SC 14-C). Brannoc doesn't believe it all, but agrees to one thing: **if Ash can wake his daughter Ama in the Quiet Pens, he'll listen.** If Brannoc is killed or refuses outright, **Fergan Ironhorn** (NPC-022) offers the same deal in secret.
- **A. At the truce in Saffra** (natural): the Hall of Bells, on the night of the Great Telling, after the champion's challenge (Fight 23).
- **B. Ash sneaks into Brannoc's war-camp** before Tuskhold to talk face to face (SC 14-B). Very Ash: Second-Story Work, Panache, a bull's tent at midnight, and a Regent sitting up awake because he doesn't sleep much anymore.
- **C. The party captures Fergan in battle** (Fight 22, phase 3 variant) and treats him well. Fergan becomes the go-between and brings his brother to a ruined shrine between the lines.
- **D. If talks fail,** Fergan comes to them at night: *"My brother's calf. Wake her and he'll follow you anywhere. I've seen his face when he visits."* The deal is the same; Brannoc doesn't know yet. Fergan will get them to the Pens (Ch15 ★1 route B).

### S14.5 · Hollowmere's free warrens / Saffra (★2 A or B/C)
- **Hollowmere (A):** the warren-towns are half-burned and half-rebuilt. Hare-folk children come out of the burrows to see Lark and go very quiet when she walks past, and then a small one starts singing, and they all do. **Mother Burdock** (R-066, R-071) holds Lark for a long time. **Russet Tamsworth** (R-072: distrust → respect; he's past "grudging" since EVT-08) gives the party the war's shape: Brannoc holds the Horngard, the Glass Mere and the siege lines around Tuskhold; the Free Clans hold the east warrens, the Wending's edge and the otter-banks; Saffra is neutral and "selling rope to both ends of the hanging." He offers otter-rafts to Saffra or a fox-guide to Tuskhold.
- **Saffra (B/C):** bright awnings, bells on everything, story-halls with doors painted in the colors of the best tellers. Brannoc's officers drink here under the truce; so do Free Clans fighters; nobody draws steel within the bell-bounds. **Saffi-of-the-Lamplight** (NPC-061; R-203 if earned) greets Ash like a manuscript she's been waiting to read: *"The boy who hears. You're shorter than the story. Stories always are."* She sets the terms: the Great Telling is in three nights; the Regent will come; Tall Folk speak after a champion's challenge, by old custom. She'll host the parley in the Hall of Bells.

### S14.6 · Through the siege lines (★3 A; SC 14-A; Fight 22)
Tuskhold sits on its plain like a grey stone loaf, ringed by the Host's earthworks, siege ramps and drum-towers. Getting in is SC 14-A. Getting in *right now*, because a relief raft is coming up the moat-channel and the Host is about to sink it, is Fight 22.

### S14.7 · Tuskhold and the Remembering Hall (★3)
Inside, the city is hungry and orderly. Elephant-folk stand guard on walls built for them, with otter-folk and fox-folk filling the gaps. Bear-folk defenders see Gideon, see Ursk's mark (it shows on him as a faint bear-shaped shadow when he's moved), and **kneel**, one after another, in the street, in their armor (Gideon's discovery). Nobody tells them to. Their den-chief, an old bear-woman, says only: *"Hearth-Warden. The Bear said you'd come."*
The **Remembering Hall** is a long stone room lined with long stones, each carved with a memory. **Grandmother Umbari** (NPC-021) sits on the longest. She touches Ash's forehead with the tip of her trunk. ***"I have been remembering this for you. Sit. It's long, and I'm slow."*** She tells them the siege's truth (R-075: Brannoc is a good father who made a terrible bargain) and the war's root (the Heron), and the SEC-12 seed (★3). She asks Lark to sing her one warren song and closes her eyes for it. She's failing; let the player see it (she forgets Zeke's name once, then laughs at herself).
- **She'll go to the parley** if the party asks (route D), and she'll insist on sitting on Ash's side.

### S14.8 · Lark's dream-song (discovery moment)
The night before the parley (or the night before Ash goes into the camp), Lark can't sleep. She says she can hear the Regent: not his voice, his *missing*. "He's sad the way Gran Burdock was sad when I was gone." She asks Ash to hum with her. **She carries the song into Brannoc's dream.** The player sees what she sends (describe it in second person, as Ash): a grey pen, a calf with a ribbon, and the calf turning her head and *looking*. Lark wakes crying and says, "He saw. He knows it was us."
- **Effect:** SC 14-C (or 14-B) gets one automatic success: Brannoc arrives having dreamt of Ama looking at him, and he's angry about it, and he can't stop thinking about it.
- **Cost:** Lark is exhausted (she can't add advantage to a waking check until after a long rest), and the Heron felt the bond move. (Ch15 footprint: Vessimer knows the party is coming.)

### S14.9 · The champion's challenge and the parley (★4 A; Fight 23; SC 14-C)
The Hall of Bells at night, every bell muffled with felt. A circle of drums. Brannoc Ironhorn comes in with his brother and a dozen officers, iron-capped horns, the war-maul **Answer** on his back, unhooked and set on the floor at the truce-line (a courtesy that makes his officers flinch). He's exactly as `truth/01` says: deep, slow, formal. *"You came under the banner. Good. Then you'll hear me first, little hearer, and after that I'll decide whether to hear you."*
- **First, the Regent speaks.** Let him make his case, all of it, honestly: the calves greying, the Heron's cure, the Tall Folk's lamps drinking the song (true), the child "kept quiet for her own safety" (he believes it). Then: "Before Tall Folk speak in a horned hall, they stand in the ring." **Mairead of the Seven Drums** (NPC-180) steps into the drum-circle (Fight 23).
- **Then the parley** (SC 14-C), whatever the duel's result. If the party's champion won or lost bravely, it's a lever.

### S14.10 · The deal (EVT-28; exit)
Brannoc says it with his hand on the iron cap of his left horn (a promise): *"My daughter is in the Quiet Pens at Cairn Answer. She is safe. She doesn't cry. She doesn't eat. She doesn't grow. She doesn't look at me. The Heron says this is peace. You say it's a cage. Wake her, and I'll listen to anything you say. Fail, and I'll come for your wells."* He gives Ash a **safe-conduct horn-token** (iron, carved with the Ironhorn mark) and seven days.
- **Fergan** (R-080, R-081) catches Ash outside afterward and walks with him in the dark. He's careful, low-voiced: he hates the Pens; the Heron frightens him; "the Pens' warden answers to the bird, not my brother. Be ready."
- **Level 15.**
