# Chapter 20 · The Drowned Gate (SEALED — DM ONLY)

> **Act III, chapter 7 of 7. The finale.** Level **20**. 2–3 sessions. Midsummer's Eve, YF 1105: Ash's 22nd birthday, a year to the day after he first crossed. On the shell of the waking Tortoise, at the keyhole of the world's prison, at sunset.
> Built on `campaign/02` Ch20 (and the Addendum's Ch20 routes), `campaign/04` (all three endings, the three tracks, "if the player tries for an ending they can't reach"), `campaign/00` §1, §4, §7, `campaign/05` §2–§8 (every relationship pays off here), `world/00` §6 (Omeruth's form and phases), §8, `truth/01` (everyone), `truth/03` (every edge resolves in the epilogue), `truth/05` (EVT-37), `rules/02` (L20 targets; the 15-round finale rule). Shared rules in `_index.md`.
> **The point of this chapter:** everyone Ash ever helped shows up. The oldest silence in the world stands up out of the sea and shows each of them the life where nothing ever hurts again. And Ash, with his sisters, decides what kind of peace is worth having.

- **B. Allies arrive based on relationships:** anyone the party helped comes on their own, whether they were summoned or not. (Use this list *as well as* A: the people who weren't asked come anyway.)
- **C. Minimum version, if alliances failed:** Willow Lane and the Free Clans still come. **They always come.** (Bess with a lamp. Russet with his foxes. Peg with a pot.)

The Stillness in body: the Lake, the Mirror, the Unmade.
- **A. At sunset as scheduled.**
- **B. Early, if SEAL hit 10** (see SEAL 10 above).

Re-sing, End, or the Warden's Seal (`campaign/04`).

- **C. Forced by events if Clara is lost** (Warden's Seal), or if the Hearkeners are downed in Phase 4, or if Ash chooses it to spare someone else.

## Scenes

### S20.1 · The night before (the talks)
On Ommu's shell, under stars that are moving again, the night before Midsummer's Eve. The ships of the gathering are still a day out; the shell is quiet. Ommu breathes, slowly, awake now: one breath a minute instead of one an hour. Each of these talks is **the player's**. Offer them; don't force them. Play them as long as the player wants.

- **Moss asks Ash for a talk.** This is the one that must happen (it's Ending 2's prerequisite: "let Moss choose it before the song, in a quiet talk with Ash"). They walk to the shell's edge, where the grass meets the sea. Moss says:
  > *"I've been thinking about what the Hall showed us, lad. The three ways. You'll pick, tomorrow. I won't. It's yours, and Clara's, and the little one's. But I want you to know two things before you do. One: whichever it is, I'm ready. I've had eleven hundred years. I've had you. That's more than enough. Two: the middle one, the one where it rains... it needs something to hold the two worlds while they knit. Something old that's willing to stay. I'd stay. If that's the one. I'd be glad to. I'd like to be a tree, I think. I've always liked trees. Your mum used to sit under one by the Bend."*
  - **If the player asked where Moss would want to stay (Ch17's fishing trip), he names it now**: "You said the Ford Stone. I think that's right."
  - **Ash's answer is the player's.** He can refuse. If he forbids it, Moss says, "All right," and means it, and Ending 2 now needs a different anchor. (There isn't one. The center panel's stag is Moss. Let Ash discover that; Zeke can read the small figures again if asked.) If Ash says nothing, Moss pats his shoulder and says, "Think on it."
  - **Moss gives Ash the compass back** (the Ch13 gift, if given): "Hold onto this. Wherever I end up, it'll point there."

- **Clara and Ash.** She's sitting on the crown plate with her knees up, holding the locket with the moving picture of the brothers (the Ch11 gift, if given). She asks: "What did Mum sound like? Does anyone know?" (Dora does. Moss does. Gideon remembers her humming.) If Twin Bond is 5+, she says, "Whatever we sing tomorrow, I want to sing it with you. Not *for* you. With." (★3 B: they can agree on the ending now.)

- **Gideon and Ash.** On the shell's highest ridge. Gideon has his oath-sword across his knees. "I've been angry at you for about fifteen years." Pause. "I'm done." That's all he can say. It's enough.

- **Zeke and Ash.** Zeke has written the whole song's words out in Sundering-script, in Ch9's never-blotting pen (if given), and he hands Ash the pages. "You don't need them. I just wanted you to have them. In case." He's crying a little and pretending he isn't. The lamps are going out at home and he's pretending that doesn't hurt either.

- **Lark and Ash.** She's twelve now (her birthday party was in Ch17). She asks whether he's scared. If he says yes, she says, "Good. Gran Burdock says only stupid people aren't." If he says no, she says, "Liar." Then she falls asleep against him.

- **Bart.** Bart has a speech prepared. He's been preparing it since Saltreach. He gives it at last, only to Ash:
  > *"Listen. You're an idiot. Your brothers are idiots. The druid is a very old idiot. But you're* my *idiots, and if you get stilled tomorrow I'll be* furious. *That's all. That's the speech. Don't look at me."*

- **(If Clara was lost: SC 20-B, the side chapter.)** See SC 20-B. It replaces Clara's talk.

### S20.2 · The day (preparation)
Midsummer's Eve. The sea around Ommu's Rest is moving again for a mile in every direction (she's awake), and beyond that, the Glass. A mile off the south shore, the keyhole: the dark circle in the Glass, getting darker. Things to do (each is a quick beat; let the player pick):
- **Lay the song-circle** (★2 C): the Hearkeners sing a ring on the crown plate. Performance DC 19 × 3 (any Hearkener). Success: Phase 4's singers start with half cover and advantage on their first SC 20-A check.
- **Plan with the allies** as they arrive (S20.3): where each unit stands. Each placed unit is a Tide bonus later.
- **Wake the last of the Hushed sailors** (from SC#5's failures): Performance DC 21 each, or the twins with no roll.
- **Rest.** A long rest is available (house rule: no gap). Everyone starts Fight 36 at full.

### S20.3 · The gathering (★1)
Late afternoon. The sun going down in the west over the Glass. Then, from every side, they come. **Read this in order, one arrival at a time, and give each one a beat.** Only include the ones the party earned (★1 A and B), plus Willow Lane and the Free Clans always (★1 C).
1. **From the north, down the river's mouth:** **Driftmarket's barges**, lashed together into a floating town, lanterns lit (oil lanterns now), Granny Tull at the front shouting. On the lead barge, Willow Lane: **Bess Carter** (she marches straight to Pip and takes him by the ear: "*Ten years old!* On a *barge!* To the *end of the world!*"), **Peg Mallory** with a pot of stew big enough for an army ("Somebody's got to feed you"), **Netta Lowe** ("I *always* said"), **Walt and Dora Finch** (Walt waves; Dora doesn't let go of his arm), **Simon Shaw** with a ledger ("I've extended your credit, Ash. Indefinitely."), **Ma Garrow** and the Wharves, **Sergeant Ostley** in his Fringe watch-coat, and **Aggie Thorne**, who walks up to Moss, looks at his antlers, and hits him on the arm. "You old stump."
2. **Rook** (`campaign/05` §2: he comes either way). He climbs off a barge and walks up to Ash. If redeemed: ***"Don't make it weird, Fenwick."*** If fallen and woken: he doesn't say anything; he just stands beside Ash. Martha Pratt (if her lung-rot was treated) is on the barge, waving.
3. **From the east, the Crown's ships** (if Highmarch committed): the *Resolute* (Maud Penrose on the quarterdeck), twenty sail of the royal navy, and on the flagship, **King Edric II** in armor too big for him and **Lord Chancellor Crane** looking seasick. Hattie Slade's *Magpie's Daughter* cuts across their bows and Crane shouts at her.
4. **From the west, by gate:** the Hall's pool (in Emberford) is linked to the Little Sea's center now that the keyhole is opening, and through the dark water, **the dwarves** (if Dun Morrach committed) come up in diving bells of iron and bronze: **King Torvald**, **Princess Brynja**, the clans' shield-wall. Brynja walks straight to Gideon: "You didn't think you'd do this without me?"
5. **From the sky:** **Vessimer** (if redeemed), circling once, and behind her a thousand herons from the Greymarsh, the ones that woke. And under them, on great owls of the Wending, **the elves** (if Aranhollow committed): the Wardens of the Leaf, and **Queen Amarys** herself if the twins sang for her.
6. **From below, in mirror:** the Little Sea's side of the keyhole shows through the dark water, and on it, the **Horned Host's long-boats** (if Brannoc turned), a hundred oars beating in time, and **Brannoc** (or Fergan) standing in the prow with Dunmore's maul (Ch16). The Host's drummers play **the drum-pattern named for Ash** (Ch15's Drum Challenge, if won). **Russet Tamsworth** and the **Free Clans** (always) are with them: foxes, otters, hare-folk, bear-folk (**Hulda Greypaw**), elephant-folk with **Umbari's long-stone** carried on a raft (Ch16), **Nkemba** standing beside it. **Mother Burdock**. **Saffi** with her bells. **Ama**, beside her father, waving at Lark across the water between the worlds.
7. **Last, from the shell's own edge:** the **woken sea-giants** (Ch19) climb up out of the sea and take their places along the shell's rim, holding the edge. One of them looks at Ash and nods.

When they're all in place, the sun touches the horizon. Ommu lifts her great head out of the sea and looks at all of them. ***"So many,"*** she says. ***"She'd have liked this."***

### S20.4 · The Stillness rises (★2; Fight 36 begins)
The sun is half-set. The keyhole goes black. And the Glass, all of it, horizon to horizon, *lifts*. A mile of flat grey water rises into a shape: a figure of grey glass water and silence, a hundred feet tall, featureless, its "voice" a pressure in the ears like being deep underwater. Every lantern in the gathering goes out at once. Every sound stops: the drums, the shouting, the sea. **Omeruth.** It doesn't speak. It doesn't have to. Everyone hears what it's offering, in their own heads, in their own words:
> *No one you love will ever die again. Nothing will ever change again. Rest. You've done so well. You can stop now.*

Roll initiative.

### S20.6 · The ending (EVT-37)
Whichever song is sung. Read the ending's scene (below), slowly. Then go to the epilogue.

#### Ending 1 · Re-sing the Seal ("The Long Quiet")
The twins (with Lark) sing the Sundering whole again, the way Wynne sang it alone. The song goes into the keyhole like a key. Omeruth *bows*, very slowly, and sinks: not destroyed, not angry, just going back to sleep. The Glass lies down. The sea starts moving. The wind comes back all at once. And at that moment, across all of Eyrland, **every glimmer crystal goes dark**: in Emberford, in the Row, in Peg's pots, in the Guildhall. Somewhere in the gathering, Zeke's little brass bird (Ch17, Ottoline's gift) stops singing in his pocket. He takes it out and holds it.
- Ommu settles back into the sea over the keyhole with a long sigh: "Another thousand years, little ones. I'll keep it."
- Moss stands up out of the grass, alive, tired, mostly himself again. "Another thousand years, lad. I've done it before." (If the Glimmer Choice was No: he adds, quietly, "Less, maybe. They'll start digging again. Someone will have to keep an eye on it." He's looking at Ash.)
- Mirrowen and Eyrland stay apart. But the twins can keep a few Wellgates open as friendly doors. **The Ford Pool, always.**

#### Ending 2 · End the Sundering ("The Rain")
Ash, Clara and Lark sing the *other* song, the one Wynne couldn't sing alone: not a cage, an unmaking. Ommu rises under them. Vessimer (if redeemed) stands at the Gap with her wings spread, or the Horned Host holds it from below, a hundred bulls with their shoulders to the dark. Zeke speaks the words. And **Moss walks out into the keyhole's center**, on the water, as the Hart, and stops there, and *roots*.
- Omeruth doesn't sleep. It *moves*. The grey glass figure loses its shape, and runs, and falls, and it's **rain**: warm, heavy, summer rain over the whole sea, the first time the Stillness has moved in all of time. It rains on the gathering. It rains on Ommu's shell. It rains on Emberford, four hundred miles away, and the Lane comes out into the street in it.
- Where Moss stood, there's a stag of living wood, antlers grown into branches, white flowers opening in the rain. Then the image goes, and the Gap closes like a healed cut, and the stag is gone. **He's at the Ford** now: in Old Ford, by the Ford Stone, a great tree is growing out of the stone, very fast, antler-shaped branches, moss and white flowers, and everyone in Old Ford watches it grow in the rain. (If Ash asked him to stay somewhere else, that's where the tree is.)
- Ash can still hear him. In the leaves. Moss's last words, from the tree, to Ash, only to Ash (and Bart): ***"There you are, lad. Look at all this rain."***
- Over a generation, the realms grow back together. Wellgates become rivers. Beastfolk and humans meet again, as the old songs said.

#### Ending 3 · The Warden's Seal ("The Stag at the Gate")
The song falters (or Clara is lost, or the Hearkeners are down, or Ash chooses it). Moss stands up as the Hart, whole, and walks past Ash, and says, very gently, "It's all right, lad. This is what I'm for." He walks out across the water to the keyhole, and turns around to face them, and **stands**. Omeruth comes down on him like a wave. And stops. And sinks back, slowly, into the dark, and the dark closes, and where it closed there's a **standing stone shaped like a stag**, at the edge of the Glass, its head turned toward the island. The realms stay split. Glimmer dims but doesn't die: the seal is Moss now, not the song.
- Aggie Thorne sits down on the shell where she's standing and doesn't get up for a long time.
- Ash loses his oldest friend, and the herbalist who carried him out of the fire. **Give the player time. Don't fill the silence.**
- (If Clara was lost: she isn't here. Ash can still find her afterward: an epilogue option.)

#### If the player tries for an ending they can't reach (`campaign/04`)
Never block the attempt. If Ash tries the unmaking song without everything Ending 2 needs, the song **fails partway**: the Stillness is wounded but not unmade, and the Hearkeners can **switch** to re-sealing (Ending 1, if Twin Bond 3+) or Moss steps in (Ending 3). **Narrate what was missing**, so the player understands, in hindsight, why:
- No redemption: *"You needed one of the old Wardens, or the Regent's host, holding the Gap. There was nobody there."*
- Glimmer Choice No: *"Every lamp in Emberford was still pulling the song out of the wall. It couldn't hold."*
- Ommu not woken: *"The shell didn't listen. She was still asleep."*
- Mercy too low: *"It wasn't a song you'd sung before. You'd never learned it."*
- Twin Bond too low: *"Clara couldn't find your note. You'd never sung together enough."*

## Skill challenges

> **L20 DCs:** easy 15, standard 17, hard 19, very hard 21. Song DCs are **21**.

### SC 20-A · "The Song" (★3; EVT-37) · inside Fight 36 Phase 4 (rounds 12–15)
**Situation:** the Hearkeners stand on the crown plate (or the song-circle) and sing, while everyone else holds Omeruth's last weight off them. **The ending is decided by which song they sing** and whether it's finished. The player declares the song at the start of Phase 4 (or it was agreed in S20.1: ★3 B).

**Who sings and how**
- Each round of Phase 4, each **Hearkener** (Ash, Clara, Lark) spends their **action** on a song check: **Performance DC 21**.
- **Ash:** Hall's Blessing (advantage on his first) · Vessimer's Blessing (one reroll, if it's still unspent) · the Ford-Warden's reed in his hand (once, advantage) · Moss's compass (it points at Moss: once, when Ash can't find the note, it shows him: advantage).
- **Clara:** the locket with the moving picture (Ch11 gift): once, advantage. The Stillness targets her first (Omeruth's *Hush the Voice*).
- **Lark:** +10. **Leads** (as SC 19-B): if she succeeds, the next roll by each other singer that round has advantage.
- **Zeke** speaks the words (Help, every round: advantage to one singer).
- **The duet:** Ash and Clara singing together, one roll: **counts double** (Twin Bond 3+).
- **Moss** (as the Hart, upgrade #5, the whole of Phase 4): protects them with *Stand* every round, and for Ending 2 he is the anchor (no roll; he walks out on round 15).

**Successes needed (by the end of round 15)**

| Song | Needs | Successes | What happens on completion |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ending 1 · Re-sing** | Twin Bond 3+ (Ash + Clara, with Lark) | **8** | The Long Quiet (S20.6) |
| **Ending 2 · Unmake** | Twin Bond 5+, Mercy 4+, a redemption holding the Gap, Glimmer Choice Yes, Ommu woken, Umbari's song, Moss's consent (S20.1) | **10** | The Rain (S20.6) |
| **Ending 3 · The Warden's Seal** | none | **0** (Moss's choice; no roll) | The Stag at the Gate (S20.6) |

**If the song isn't finished by the end of round 15** (or all three singers are down at once): **Ending 3** (Moss steps in: ★3 C). That's the fail-forward. It isn't a loss. It's what Moss always said he'd do.
**Switching songs:** at any point, the singers can switch from Ending 2 to Ending 1 (successes carry over: Ending 1 needs 8 total). They can't switch *up* from 1 to 2 (the unmaking needs the whole of Phase 4 to build).
**Ash chooses Ending 3 to spare someone else:** at any point in Phase 4, Ash can stop singing and tell Moss to go. Moss goes. (It's his choice too; he'll ask once: "You're sure, lad?")

### SC 20-B · "Bring Her Home" (the Clara-lost side chapter; optional, before the finale)
`campaign/04`: *"If Twin Bond is 0 at the end of Ch13, Clara leaves... She can return in Ch20 only if Ash goes after her. That's a big optional side chapter."*
- **When:** any time in Ch17–19 if the player asks where she is, or the night before the finale (S20.1) as a desperate last journey. Lark can feel her through the bond (she's in Saltreach, singing in the Barnacle under another name; or, if the Heron took her in Ch15–18, in Old Varrow's drowned palace, Hushed, and woken when the Heron was redeemed or destroyed, sitting in the rain, not knowing where to go).
- **The chain (5 links, all must succeed; no rolls, only choices):** the player plays the conversation. Each link is a thing Ash has to *say or do* honestly. The DM judges whether it's honest. There's no DC.
  1. **Find her** (no roll: Lark's bond, or the compass, or just going).
  2. **Say the true thing he's never said** (what he got wrong: using her, forcing her, keeping her out of it; whichever he did).
  3. **Don't ask her to come** (yet). Ask her what *she* wants.
  4. **Give her something** (a Row gift; the locket's half; a song; nothing but time).
  5. **Ask her** (now). She answers.
- **If it all lands:** Twin Bond becomes **3** (not higher: there wasn't time to build more). Ending 1 opens. Ending 2 stays closed (5+ needed). She comes to the shell.
- **If any link fails** (Ash lies, pushes, or makes it about himself): she says, "I'll think about it." In S20.3, at the very end of the gathering, there's a small boat, and in it, alone, Clara. She doesn't come up onto the shell. She sits in her boat and *listens*. (In Phase 4, she can sing from the boat: she's a fourth singer for Ending 3's moment, and the last thing Moss hears is two Fenwicks singing. That's the bittersweet version.)

## Encounters

### FIGHT 36 · Omeruth, the Stillness · Ch20 · **FINALE (15 rounds, changing every 3)** · Party level 20

**Target numbers (L20):** boss HP (all phases) **1,130** (cap **1,450**: four forms, four change types, plus an objective-only final phase) · boss + adds DPR **95 sustained / 143 spike** · AC 18–20 · attack +11 to +13 · DC 18–19 · boss best saves **+8 to +10**.
**HP math (shown):**
- **Omeruth's pools:** Phase 1, the Lake **320** · Phase 2, the Mirror **300** · Phase 3, the Unmade **340** · Phase 4, the Song: **no pool** (its *Last Weight* is an objective, not HP: the protectors hold it off the singers) = **960**.
- **Adds ≈ 3 × DPR across the fight (~480):** Phase 1: 6 Hands of the Stillness × 40 = 240 (the allies fight the Tide-Waves) · Phase 2: Still Selves (one per party member, 40 each = 160) · Phase 3: 2 Hands × 40 = 80 → **~480**.
- **Total ≈ 1,440** (at the 1,450 cap; it changes four times). Party output 161 × 11 rounds (Phases 1–3) ≈ 1,771 + Thief's Reflexes round 1 (~40) ≈ 1,811 × ~80% (allies' help, Ommu in Phase 3, minus the Mirror's lost turns) ≈ 1,450. **Phases 1–3: 11 rounds. Phase 4: 4 rounds (12–15), by design.**
- **Phase timing is fixed, not HP-driven:** Phase 1 rounds 1–3, Phase 2 rounds 4–7, Phase 3 rounds 8–11, Phase 4 rounds 12–15 (`campaign/02`). **If a pool runs out early,** the phase's remaining rounds are a breather (the gathering surges; the party can reposition, heal, prepare the song-circle). **If a pool isn't empty when the phase's rounds end,** the phase changes anyway, and the leftover HP is lost (the Stillness *moves on*; it doesn't care about winning a phase, only about the end). This keeps the finale on its 15-round shape no matter how the dice go.
**DPR math (vs party AC ~19–23):**
- Omeruth: 2 *Still Hand* × 30 (4d10 + 8 = 30; + 2d8 psychic) × 0.55 ≈ **33** · Legendary actions ~1.5 × 14 ≈ **21** · Lair actions ≈ **12**.
- Adds: P1 Hands 6 × (2 × 13 × 0.55) ≈ **86** (the allies engage four: ~**29** reach the party) · P2 Still Selves ≈ 4 × (16 × 0.55) ≈ **35** · P3 the Unmade terrain ≈ **15**.
- **Sustained ≈ 90–100.** **Spike:** *The Mirror* (P2 opening: DC 19 Wis, 55 psychic to everyone ≈ 55 × 4 × 0.55 ≈ 121) + a *Still Hand* ≈ **140–145**.

**Battlefield: the crown of Ommu's shell, and the sea around it** (crop-friendly: **the shell** as the center, a dome 400 ft across with the crown plate (the song-circle) at its top, the shell-ridges in rings around it (half cover), the shell's rim where the sea-giants stand; **the sea** around the shell, with the gathering's ships, barges and diving bells in a ring at 300–600 ft; **the keyhole** a mile south, now right *under* the shell's southern edge, because Ommu has swum over it: a black circle in the water from which Omeruth rises; and **in mirror**, under the water's surface, the Little Sea and the Horned Host).
1. **The crown plate / song-circle.** Where the singers stand (Phase 4). Half cover if the song-circle was laid.
2. **The shell-ridges.** Rings of plates, 3 ft high, every 30 ft: half cover.
3. **The rim.** The sea-giants (woken) hold it in Phases 1–3: Hushed things crossing the rim are fought there.
4. **The sea.** Moving (Ommu is awake) except where Omeruth touches it (still-water terrain, *Sinking*). With Ommu's Blessing (Ch19), the party swims at their walking speed and breathes water.
5. **The gathering.** The allied units (*the Gathering*, below).

**Opening.** S20.4. Every lantern goes out. Every sound stops. Then Ommu breathes, and the sound comes back: the drums from the Host in mirror, Peg shouting from a barge, a heron's cry. Roll initiative. (Ash's Thief's Reflexes: two turns in round 1.)
**Enemy plan.** **Stop everything.** It doesn't hate. It soothes. It wants each of them to *rest*. It targets the **Voice first** (Clara), then the **Hearing** (Ash), then the **child who carries both** (Lark), then whoever is protecting them. It never wastes an attack on the gathering if a Hearkener is in reach. It doesn't flee. It can't be killed in Phases 1–3 (its pools empty, it *moves on* to its next form). Only the Song ends it.

**THE GATHERING (allied troops; narrative units, no full stat blocks)**
Track a **Battle Tide** from −3 (the gathering breaks: the Hushed tide reaches the shell's crown) to +3 (the tide is held far out). Start at **0**, +1 for each of: the alliance fully sworn (all three courts), the Horned Host present, Vessimer present, the song-circle laid, SEAL 2. −1 for SEAL 7+.
- **Each round, at initiative 10,** the gathering acts: the DM narrates one beat (a Crown broadside; the dwarves' shield-wall on the rim; Brannoc's bulls holding the mirror-side; Peg throwing a pot of boiling stew at a Hand). If the Tide is **+1 or higher**, the gathering deals **40 damage** to Omeruth's current pool or destroys one add. At **+3**, 80.
- **Tide changes:** +1 when the party protects a unit from a named threat or completes a phase objective; −1 when a unit breaks, an ally the player cares about is in danger and not saved, or a Hearkener is downed.
- **Allies the player knows by name** can be in danger in Phases 1 and 3 (a Hand grabs Pip off the barge; the Unmade erases the raft with Umbari's stone on it). Each is a moment: save them (one action, one roll at DC 19, or a spell), or the Tide drops. **Nobody from the Lane dies by script** (`campaign/05` §3). They can be *stilled* (Hushed) and woken after.

**PHASE 1 · rounds 1–3 · "The Lake"**
- *What happens:* it rises. The allied armies fight its Hushed tide (*Tide-Waves*, troop-scale, handled by the gathering). The party fights its **hands**: six **Hands of the Stillness** (grey glass figures shaped like reaching arms, each the height of a man) come up over the rim and onto the shell, going for the Hearkeners.
- Omeruth itself stays a mile off at the keyhole, a hundred feet tall, and reaches across the water with *Still Hand* (reach: the whole battlefield; it's a mile of arm). Damaging it in Phase 1 means reaching *it*: ranged attacks at 600+ ft (Zeke's spells; Ash's longbow-equivalent) or going out across the water (Vessimer carries; Moss's *Carry* as the Hart; swimming with Ommu's Blessing).
- **Counter (Bart's Help, and the round-1 nova):** *Telegraph (round 1):* the Lake's surface goes perfectly still around the whole shell, and a grey ripple runs up it like a shiver. *Hush the Small:* at initiative 20 of round 1, every Tiny creature on the battlefield is Stilled until the end of round 2 (Bart sits down on Ash's shoulder and stares at nothing; "*Oh no,*" he manages first). Bart is back by round 3, and very angry.
- **Objective:** keep the Hands off the crown plate. A Hand that reaches the crown plate grabs a Hearkener (the Stillness's first offer).

**PHASE 2 · rounds 4–7 · "The Mirror" (MIND)**
- *Telegraph (round 3):* Omeruth stops reaching. It *looks*. Every still surface on the sea, and the shell's wet plates, shows each of them something.
- ***The Mirror (Phase 2 opening, automatic):*** each creature on the battlefield (party and named allies) makes a **DC 19 Wis save**. *Failure:* 55 (10d10) psychic damage and **Stilled** (Incapacitated, speed 0) until it succeeds on the save at the end of one of its turns. *Success:* half damage. (Vessimer's Blessing: one reroll. Mind Blank stops the damage, not the Stilled.)
- **What each one sees, the last and deepest time** (narrate privately if you like; the player chooses whether to describe Ash's):
  - **Ash:** his parents alive, the twins in their arms, the lamps of Emberford glowing, Willow Lane, forever. Nobody ever leaving. Moss on the step of the Green Jar, never getting older, never having to choose anything. *His mother says, "Asheron. Stay."*
  - **Gideon:** the night before the fire. Everyone asleep. He's fourteen and nothing has happened yet.
  - **Zeke:** the Row at night, every lamp lit, Hollis at a workbench, kind, and nothing ever having been a lie.
  - **Moss:** the ford, eleven hundred years ago. Wynne in the shallows, young. His sister standing beside her. Nobody has had to stand anywhere forever.
  - **Clara:** Hester's kitchen. Hester alive. Hollis reading by the fire. *Wren* never having been lost, or *Clara* never having been found; it doesn't matter, because nothing will ever change.
  - **Lark:** Gran Burdock's burrow, and Lark never grows up, and never has to be brave.
- **Still Selves:** for each Stilled hero, a **Still Self** rises out of the water: a grey copy of *that hero, at peace*, smiling, holding out a hand. It fights to keep its hero still. Struck by its own hero, it's gone at once (and the hero is freed). (It's easier than Ch18's Drowned Loves to strike: it's *yourself*. The player may find that harder, or easier. Watch.)
- **Breaking the Mirror:** Ash's song frees every Stilled ally within 120 ft (Performance DC 21 as an action). Lark can do it once (DC 19). Clara can do it once (DC 21) for *everyone on the battlefield* (the Voice: to many at once).
- **Counter (Zeke's Counterspell / Gideon's aura):** *Telegraph:* the Mirror isn't a spell; Counterspell does nothing. But **Gideon's Aura of Warding** (resistance to psychic) halves the damage for everyone in it, and **Elder Champion**'s presence gives allies in his aura advantage on the save. Make the player feel Gideon's aura matter here; it's his last discovery.

**PHASE 3 · rounds 8–11 · "The Unmade" (TERRAIN)**
- *Telegraph (round 7):* the edge of the shell, on the south side, goes *flat*. Not flooded: flat. The ridges are gone. The grass is gone. There's just grey.
- **TERRAIN:** it begins erasing terrain into flat grey water. **At initiative 20 each round, a 60-ft-deep band of the shell's rim (all the way around) becomes the Unmade:** flat, featureless still-water terrain where nothing has cover, nothing has height, and a creature that starts its turn there takes 4d10 psychic and has disadvantage on all checks. The shell **shrinks** toward the crown plate: 400 ft across in round 8, 280 in round 9, 160 in round 10, 40 (just the crown plate) in round 11.
- **Allies fall back.** The sea-giants on the rim retreat to the crown. The gathering's ships back off (the Tide's damage still happens, but at long range).
- **Ommu fights beside them.** In Phase 3, Ommu lifts her head and *bites* the Stillness (see her ally block). Each round, Ommu's turn: one action from her block. **Ommu's scale** (Ch19): held up as a shield, it keeps a 20-ft circle from being Unmade for the whole phase (the singers can stand there in Phase 4).
- **Counter (Moss's Hart and the long rests):** *Telegraph:* the Unmade can't be healed in (healing spells there restore half). Moss's *Bloom* (as the Hart) is the only thing that *undoes* it: 30 ft, once per phase.
- **Objective:** keep a place to sing. When Phase 4 begins, wherever the singers stand must not be Unmade (the crown plate; Ommu's scale's circle; the song-circle, if laid, resists for one round per Hearkener who laid it).

**PHASE 4 · rounds 12–15 · "The Song" (OBJECTIVE; SC 20-A)**
- *Telegraph (round 11):* the Stillness stops erasing. It *turns toward the crown plate* and comes. For the first time, it moves its whole body. It's crossing the mile from the keyhole to the shell. The sea goes flat in front of it like ice forming.
- **OBJECTIVE:** the Hearkeners sing (SC 20-A). Everyone else protects them. Omeruth has **no HP pool** in Phase 4. It has its **Last Weight**: each round, at initiative 20, it presses down on the singers.
  - ***Last Weight:*** each singer makes a **DC 19 Con save** (advantage if a protector is within 5 ft of them). *Failure:* 22 (4d10) psychic damage, and their song check that round has disadvantage. *Success:* no damage.
  - A protector can **take the Weight** for a singer within 5 ft (a reaction): the protector makes the save instead, at disadvantage, and takes double on a failure. (Gideon will. Moss will. Let the player decide who else.)
  - Damage dealt to Omeruth in Phase 4 *does* matter: **every 60 damage** dealt to it in a round means its Last Weight that round is a DC 17 save instead of 19. (It's the protectors' job: hit it to keep it off the singers.)
- **Moss (upgrade #5):** the Hart for **the whole of Phase 4**. *Stand* every round (enemies can't attack anyone but him). For Ending 2, on round 15 (or when the 10th success lands), he walks out into the keyhole and roots. (No roll. It's his choice, made in S20.1.)
- **Vessimer (if redeemed):** holds the Gap from round 12. She doesn't fight. She stands in the keyhole with her wings spread and *holds the water still enough to sing over*, the way she did at the first Sundering. (She's the Warden Ending 2 needs.)
- **The Horned Host (if turned, and if Vessimer isn't there):** holds it from below: a hundred bulls in the Little Sea, shoulders to the dark. (Ending 2's "great host holding the gap.")

**FINAL BEAT.** The song finishes (S20.6: Ending 1 or 2), or Moss steps in (Ending 3). **EVT-37.**
**Fail-forward.** There is no losing the finale. If the whole party is down in Phase 1–3, the gathering pulls them back to the crown (the sea-giants, Brynja's shield-wall, Peg), and Phase 4 starts with them standing up (at 1 HP and whatever healing the allies can give: Mother Brine, Sister Rosalind and Moss are all here). If the song can't be sung, Moss sings the end of it alone, as the Hart, and stands at the gate (Ending 3).
**DM notes.** **15 rounds, by design.** Don't let a phase drag past its rounds; don't let a nova end one early (spare rounds are breathers). Give the gathering a beat every round. Give each named ally a moment somewhere in the fight. If the player is tired by Phase 3, shorten it to 3 rounds (8–10) and start the Song at round 11. The Song should always get **at least 4 rounds**. That's the part the player will remember.

*Gargantuan Aberration (primordial; older than any god), Unaligned*
**AC** 20 (the Lake) / 18 (the Mirror) / 20 (the Unmade) · **Initiative** +10 (20) · **HP** 960 across three pools (the Lake 320 · the Mirror 300 · the Unmade 340); Phase 4 has no pool (Last Weight)
**Speed** 0 (Phases 1–3: it stands at the keyhole and reaches) · swim 120 ft. (Phase 4)

|   | Score | Mod | Save |   | Score | Mod | Save |   | Score | Mod | Save |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| STR | 28 | +9 | +10 | DEX | 10 | +0 | +5 | CON | 26 | +8 | +10 |
| INT | 24 | +7 | +8 | WIS | 26 | +8 | +10 | CHA | 26 | +8 | +10 |

**Immunities** psychic, poison, cold · **Resistances** bludgeoning, piercing, slashing, necrotic · **Vulnerabilities** thunder (sound and song break stillness) · **Condition Immunities** Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Stunned · **Senses** truesight (everywhere there is still water) · **Languages** none; it offers · **CR** 30 (XP 155,000; PB +9)
*Tuned for party level 20: finale*
*Best saves Wis +10, Cha +10, Con +10, Str +10. **Weak save: Dex +5** (Ash's Cunning Strike DC 19 lands ~70%: Daze matters most; a Dazed Stillness loses its legendary actions for a round; Trip does nothing, it can't be prone).*

**Traits**
- ***Legendary Resistance (4/Day; resets to 2 at the start of each phase).*** If it fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead. *(Say: "It doesn't move. Of course it doesn't." In Phase 4 it has none: the Song is beyond saves.)*
- ***Moves On.*** When a pool reaches 0, or when its phase's rounds end, it changes form at once. It can't be killed in Phases 1–3. Only the Song ends it.
- ***A Mile of Arm (Phases 1–3).*** Its *Still Hand* reaches anywhere on the battlefield.
- ***Thunder Breaks Stillness.*** When it takes thunder damage, it can't use a lair action until the start of its next turn.
- ***The Song Hurts.*** Every Hearkener song success (waking, breaking the Mirror, SC 20-A) deals 20 thunder damage to it, and it loses its next legendary action.
- ***It Offers.*** At the start of each phase, every creature on the battlefield hears it: *Rest. You can stop now.* (No mechanic in Phases 1 and 3; the Mirror is Phase 2's version; the Last Weight is Phase 4's.)

**Actions**
- ***Multiattack.*** Two *Still Hand* attacks (Phases 1–3), or *Hush the Voice* and one *Still Hand*.
- ***Still Hand.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +13, reach anywhere on the battlefield (Phases 1–3) or 30 ft. (Phase 4). *Hit:* 30 (4d10 + 8) bludgeoning damage plus 9 (2d8) psychic damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 20) and pulled 30 ft toward the keyhole (Phases 1–3).
- ***Hush the Voice (Recharge 5–6).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 19, one creature it can see (**Clara first, always, then Ash, then Lark**). *Failure:* 45 (10d8) psychic damage and it can't speak, sing or cast verbal spells until the end of its next turn. *Success:* half damage.
- ***The Mirror (Phase 2 opening; then Recharge 6).*** See Phase 2.
- ***The Unmade (Phase 3, each round; no action).*** See Phase 3.
- ***Last Weight (Phase 4, each round; no action).*** See Phase 4.

**Legendary Actions**
*Uses: 3. Right after another creature's turn, it spends a use. It regains all uses at the start of its turn.*
- ***Reach.*** One *Still Hand* attack.
- ***Still the Water.*** A 30-ft-radius area of sea or shell becomes still-water terrain (*Sinking*) until its next turn.
- ***Show Them (costs 2; Phase 2+).*** One creature that isn't Stilled makes the Mirror's save (no damage; Stilled on a failure).
- ***Raise a Hand (costs 2; Phases 1 and 3).*** A Hand of the Stillness rises from the nearest still water.

**Lair Actions** *(the Drowned Gate is its lair; initiative count 20, losing ties; no repeat two rounds running; +1 use per round at SEAL 7+)*
- ***The Glass Spreads (terrain).*** The still sea around the shell widens: every ally ship within 600 ft is becalmed (the gathering's damage this round is halved).
- ***The Keyhole Pulls (objective pressure).*** Every creature in the water within 300 ft of the keyhole is pulled 30 ft toward it (DC 19 Str negates).
- ***Old Offer (counter).*** It speaks, inside one creature's head, in the voice of someone that creature lost (Ash: his mother; Clara: Hester; Zeke: Hollis, if dead; Gideon: their father; Moss: Wynne; Lark: nobody yet, so it uses Gran Burdock's voice, and Lark says, *"That's not how she talks,"* and is immune). The creature makes a DC 19 Wis save or uses its reaction to move 30 ft toward the keyhole.

**Phases:** P1 the Lake (rounds 1–3): reaching; Hands; Hush the Small. P2 the Mirror (rounds 4–7): the Mirror; Still Selves. P3 the Unmade (rounds 8–11): erasing; the shell shrinks; Ommu fights. P4 the Song (rounds 12–15): it comes; Last Weight; SC 20-A.
**Tactics:** the Voice first. It doesn't hate. It never gloats, never threatens. Everything it does is an offer. It's the most patient thing in the world. It's in no hurry. It's never once been told no by a song it couldn't silence. Until now.
