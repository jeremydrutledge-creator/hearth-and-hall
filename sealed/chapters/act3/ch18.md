> **Act III, chapter 5 of 7.** Level **18** during, **19** on exit. 2 sessions. Early Midsummer, YF 1105. Highmarch's walls, the Greymarsh, and Old Varrow, the drowned old capital: its streets, its bell tower, its chapel, its first glimmer mine, and its palace under the water.

## ★ Must-happen beats

### ★1 · The Greymarsh rises (EVT-34; SEAL +1)
The marsh goes still. Highmarch sees the Hushed dead walking out of Old Varrow toward the chalk ridge. The Heron has opened the Greymarsh gate.
- **A. News reaches the party in Emberford by message-moth from Chancellor Crane:** *"The marsh has stopped moving. The dead are walking out of it. The King says to tell you, 'the grey lake is real.' Come."*
- **B. The party is in Highmarch (late Ch17) and sees it from the walls:** the Greymarsh turning glass-grey from the south edge inward, mile by mile, and in the mist, lines of figures in old harness walking north in perfect silence.
- **C. Lark feels it, and the Heron's voice speaks through every still pool in the realm** at once: in the Lane Well, the Willow's rain-barrel, Bess Carter's washtub, the Hall's pool. ***"Little hearers. Come and stop me. I'd like to see you try. I'd like to see you understand."*** (She means it. She wants them to come.)
- **D. (If the party is in Mirrowen.)** The Little Sea's edge goes flat. Russet's otter-folk scouts bring word: "The bird's gone through to the Tall Folk side. South. Where the old city drowned."

### ★2 · Old Varrow
A drowned-city dungeon: bells under water, Hushed ghosts of the Gate War, the place where glimmer was first mined.
- **A. By boat through the marsh** (SC 18-A; the natural route).
- **B. Through the Highmarch castle well** (a minor gate; Ash opens it, Performance DC 21 or the locket), surfacing in Old Varrow's drowned chapel well (S18.4 first, then the streets).
- **C. By Driftmarket barges, if Granny Tull is allied:** the Seven Mothers know the marsh's old channels. The approach is quiet (SC 18-A links 1 and 3 automatic) but slow (the Hushed dead reach Highmarch's walls first: S18.2 happens with Edric on the walls).

### ★3 · Vessimer's lair
The final fight with the Grey Heron.
- **A. In the drowned palace** (natural: the palace under Old Varrow's central lake, where the Wellgate breached in the Gate War).
- **B. At the old glimmer mine where the Gate War began** (if the party went into the mine first: S18.5). She comes up through the mine's flooded shaft.
- **C. She comes to them:** if the party lingers (takes more than three days, or a long rest in the marsh), she attacks Highmarch, and the fight is on the castle walls (use the castle walls as the battlefield; the bell-tower phase becomes the Royal Cathedral's tower; the drain phase becomes the castle moat draining down into the castle well).

### ★4 · Redeem or destroy (EVT-35)
If Ash chooses mercy (Mercy track high, Moss with them, a Hearkener song), Vessimer can be woken from the Stillness's grip. She becomes the Heron again: grieving, ancient and on their side. She knows the Drowned Gate's inside. Otherwise she dies, and the Stillness loses its voice.
- **A. Ash sings to her at the fight's end** (SC 18-B, phase 4).
- **B. Moss refuses to strike the killing blow and asks Ash to try.** (If the party is about to kill her and Moss is there, he stands in front of her. R-012. He doesn't fight them. He just says, "Lad. Please. Try.")
- **C. Lark, through the bond, sings what the Heron used to be** (if Ash is down, silenced, or far away: Lark can carry SC 18-B, at disadvantage unless Ash is within 120 ft).
- **D. Destroy.** Always open. No roll. If Ash chooses it, it happens, and it's a victory, and the player should feel it was a *real* choice, not a wrong one. (If redemption was on the table and Moss asked, Mercy −1. Don't mention the score.)

## Scenes

### S18.1 · The message (★1)
Wherever it lands. Give the player one beat at home first if they're in Emberford: the Lane's lamps a little dimmer than last month, Peg's pots stirring a little slower. Moss is standing in the Willow's yard with the Heron's feather in his hand, and he says, "She's calling us. She wants us to see." Then: "I'm going to tell her what Umbari said. Whatever happens."

### S18.2 · Highmarch's walls (★1 B; optional)
If the party comes to Highmarch first (by the castle well or the Tamber): the Hushed dead are on the chalk ridge below the walls, standing perfectly still in ranks, waiting. They don't attack. They're *keeping* the city, the way they keep Old Varrow. Crane's Crown Guard is on the walls in silence. **Edric** stands with them, small in his armor, and says to Ash: "They're not attacking. They're just... waiting for us to stop. Why is that worse?"
- **Gideon** can hold them at the walls with his aura (they won't step into it). That's a scene, not a fight: the Crown Guard sees the Bear-mark, and the knight who thought Gideon was an upstart kneels on the wall-walk.
- **The way to Old Varrow** from here: down the ridge, through the ranks of the still dead (they part for the party; they're waiting for something else), and into the marsh.

### S18.3 · The Greymarsh (★2 A; SC 18-A)
A day and a night through the marsh by flat-boat. Reeds gone grey and glassy, chiming when the boat brushes them. Water like a mirror, so still that the boat seems to float in sky. Herons, a hundred of them, standing on one leg in the shallows, all facing south, none of them moving. Bells under the water, a long way off, ringing with nothing ringing them. SC 18-A is the crossing.

### S18.4 · Old Varrow: the drowned chapel (the Fenn-wyck tomb; SEC-23 crest seed)
Wherever they come in (route B surfaces here; route A passes it), the **drowned chapel** of Old Varrow stands half out of the water on what was its hill. Inside, among the drowned pews: a **tomb**, waist-deep, its lid carved with a knight in Gate War harness, and on the knight's shield, **a ford with a stag's antlers above it**: the crest on the split locket (SEC-23). The inscription (Zeke, or anyone, History DC 15): *"Edwin Fenn-wyck, who held the chapel gate. The line of the ford."*
- If the locket is whole, its two halves fit a shallow niche in the tomb's side. It opens: a Gate War knight's **ring** and a letter, too water-damaged to read except one line, in a woman's hand: *"...the Heron came to the ford and stood with us. Don't forget that she stood with us."* (SC 18-B lever: the letter.)
- **The chapel's well** is the cracked Wellgate under Old Varrow (the one that breached in the Gate War). It's open now; the grey pours out of it into the marsh. Clara (or the twins) can close it after the fight (S18.7).

### S18.5 · Old Varrow: the streets and the bells (Fight 31)
Drowned streets, waist-deep and deeper; houses with their doors open under the water; the Hushed dead of the Gate War going about their business in perfect silence (a baker kneading grey dough, a watchman walking a beat, a mother rocking an empty cradle), *keeping* the city as it was the day it drowned. They don't attack unless the party breaks the stillness: a shout, a splash, a spell. Somebody always breaks it. Fight 31.
- **The old glimmer mine** (optional, ★3 B): a shaft in the hill under the chapel, where the first glimmer was cut, four hundred years ago. The walls still glitter faintly. Zeke can read the miners' marks: the date, and a note scratched by a miner: *"The stones sing when we cut them. Foreman says it's nothing."* (Zeke goes quiet.)

### S18.6 · The palace under the lake (★3 A; Fight 32)
Old Varrow's palace stood on an island in the city's central lake. When the gate breached, the lake rose over it. Now the lake's surface is perfectly still, and at the center, the palace's **bell tower** stands up out of it, sixty feet of grey stone with a great bronze bell, and on the bell tower's top, a figure thirty feet tall, grey as rain, standing on one leg. **Vessimer.** She speaks before the party is close. ***"You came. Good. Let me show you what I'm giving everyone."*** Fight 32.

### S18.7 · After (EVT-35; exit)
**If redeemed:** the lake *moves*. A ripple, then wind, then rain: the first rain on the Greymarsh in a year. Vessimer stands in it, smaller, her feathers going from grey to the blue-grey of a real heron. She looks at Moss and says, ***"Brother. It's raining."*** He says, "I know." She asks Ash what his name is (she never asked). She tells them what's inside the Drowned Gate (the Gate's shape; the Stillness's body; that Ommu sleeps "with one eye half-open, waiting for someone to sing to her"). She'll stand with them at the gate. **The Hushed dead of Old Varrow lie down** in the water, one after another, and are at rest. The recent Hushed (the Calm who fled into the Highmarch well) wake, shivering, and ask where they are.
**If destroyed:** she falls into the lake, and the lake *stays still*. Then, slowly, over an hour, it starts to move. The dead lie down. It's quiet, and then it isn't. Moss sits on the bell tower's steps. Nobody should make him get up. Later he says, "She sang it with us, you know. At the start. She was the one who knew how to make water hold still enough to sing over." And that's all he says about it, ever.
**Either way:** the chapel's well can be closed now (Clara's Voice, or the twins: Performance DC 21, or no roll with the duet). That stops the grey pouring into the marsh. (It's part of EVT-35's SEAL move, not an extra one.)

## Encounters

### FIGHT 31 · The Drowned Streets · Ch18 · Significant · Party level 18

**Target numbers (L18):** total HP **685** · enemy DPR **103** (12% of effective HP 858) · AC 18–20 · attack +11 to +13 · DC 18–19.
**HP math (shown):** 4 Hushed Varrow Knights × 110 = 440 · Drowned Bell-Ringer 135 · The Bell Tower (hazard) 110 → **685**. Party output 152 × 5.5 ≈ 836 × ~80% (water, the tower, laying-to-rest choices) ≈ 669. **Expected 5–6 rounds.**
**DPR math (vs party AC ~19–22):** Knights 4 × (2 × 18 × 0.55) ≈ **79** (3 engaged most rounds: ~**60**) · Bell-Ringer (the Toll, recharge, averaged) ≈ **25** · the tower's collapse (once, round 5) ≈ spike **+30** → **~85–103**, spike ~115.

**Battlefield: Bell Street, Old Varrow** (crop-friendly: a drowned street 40 ft wide and 200 ft long, between grey stone houses with their upper floors above water; a stone bridge across it at the middle; a small square at the far end with the **Lesser Bell Tower**, 50 ft, leaning; the water is waist-deep on a human (4 ft) in the street, 10 ft deep in the square).
1. **Waist-deep water.** Difficult terrain for Medium creatures. Small creatures (Bart as a rat; Lark) swim. Prone in it means under it.
2. **The houses.** Upper floors above water: windows, balconies. Second-Story Work: Ash moves across them at full speed. Half cover.
3. **The bridge.** A high arch, 15 ft above the water. The knights try to hold it.
4. **The Lesser Bell Tower.** The Bell-Ringer rings from the top. The tower leans. In phase 3, it falls (*The Bell Tower*, below).
5. **The baker, the watchman, the mother.** Hushed dead townsfolk going about their business. They don't fight. Area spells hit them. They're laid to rest if they drop (no XP; a moment of quiet). Lark asks the party not to.

**Opening.** Someone breaks the stillness (it's always someone: a splash, Bart, a cantrip spark, the Bell-Ringer seeing them). Every Hushed dead townsperson in the street stops and turns. Then the knights come out of the houses' ground floors, under the water, rising.
**Enemy plan.** *Keep the city.* The knights push intruders out of the street, back the way they came. The Bell-Ringer tolls to stun. They don't chase past the city's edge. **They target whoever made the most noise** (casters, Ash humming, Gideon's shouted orders).

**PHASE 1 · rounds 1–2 · "Keep the street"**
- The four Knights come up out of the water in a line and advance. The Bell-Ringer tolls from the tower.
- **Counter (Zeke's area spells):** *Telegraph (round 1):* the townsfolk drift into the middle of the street between the party and the Knights. A Fireball there lays a baker and a mother to rest. The Knights don't care. Zeke will. (It isn't a mechanical penalty. It's a choice.)

**PHASE 2 · trigger: two Knights down, or round 3 · "The bridge" (OBJECTIVE + TERRAIN)**
- *Telegraph (round 2):* the water level drops a foot, then another. Something's draining it.
- **TERRAIN:** the street drains to knee-deep (normal terrain again), but the square at the end becomes a **whirlpool** (10-ft-deep water spinning down toward a cracked culvert: DC 19 Str save each turn in the square or pulled under and 4d10 bludgeoning).
- **OBJECTIVE:** the Knights fall back to the bridge and hold it in a line (Shield-Wall-style: half cover against ranged while two are adjacent). The way to the palace is over that bridge.

**PHASE 3 · trigger: the Bell-Ringer drops, or round 5 · "The tower falls" (TERRAIN)**
- *Telegraph (round 4):* the Lesser Bell Tower groans; its bell swings by itself; bricks fall into the whirlpool.
- **The Bell Tower collapses** at initiative 20 of round 5 (or when the Bell-Ringer drops). See its hazard block. It falls across the street toward the bridge. After: rubble, a ramp of broken stone up to the bridge's height, and the bell lying in the water, still ringing faintly.

**FINAL BEAT.** The Knights are laid to rest, or the party crosses the bridge and the Knights stop at the city's inner edge (they're keeping a line that doesn't exist any more). The **Bell-Warden** (the senior Knight, NPC-194), laid to rest, says, "Is it over? Did the gate hold?" Whatever the party says is what he believes as he goes.
**Fail-forward.** The party is pushed back to the city's edge. They can come in again by the chapel (S18.4) or the mine (S18.5): a longer way. The Heron watches all of it, and Fight 32 starts with her pool's phase 1 at full and one fewer Varrow Ghost (she's curious, not worried).
**Rewards.** The Bell-Warden's sword (a **+3 Longsword**, Very Rare: "Warden's Edge"; if no one uses a longsword, a +3 weapon of another type found in the Knights' armory); the bell's clapper (story: in Ch20, ringing it once in the Phase 1 tide stuns Hushed in 60 ft).
