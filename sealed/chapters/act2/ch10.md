## ★ Must-happen beats

### ★1 · Why north
The party needs proof that glimmer-mining thins the seal: proof the Concord will believe. The dwarves stopped mining it 400 years ago.
- **A. Moss:** "The dwarves know. They swore not to say. They might break it for the Bear's chosen." He looks at Gideon.
- **B. Dagny Coalbeard** (NPC-055, Quickhand Tools) admits her grandmother told her "never use glimmer," and points them to Dun Morrach. (She's ashamed: she's the only dwarf on the Row who uses it.)
- **C. The marks.** The Kessel warning stone (Ch5) and the Varga-vault inscription (Ch9) carry the same dwarven mark: "Oath of the Quiet Stone."
- **D. Hakon Stonebrow** (NPC-049, the Iron Kettle) refuses: "There's an oath. I'll not break it for a Fringe boy." Then, to Gideon only, after a long look: "Go to the King. Show him your hands." (He's seen the oath-light in Gideon's palm.)

### ★2 · Copperhill
Hilde Strand, the Champion (NPC-064). The Haymonth bout is past; she offers an exhibition, a challenge bout, or a re-match. The party needs a pass-guide or a warrant for the Pass Road (the Champion issues both).
- **A. Ash or Gideon challenges for the Champion's Seat** (Fight 13).
- **B. A job:** Hilde hires the party to clear the Pass Road of "foreign raiders" (it's the Grey Wolves; she doesn't know).
- **C. Hilde finds them:** "You're the ones the Wolves are paid to stop." (Her militia heard it in Wolfsgate's taverns.)

### ★3 · The Grey Wolf Company holds Bearback Pass
A Calm cutout has hired Captain-General Ulric Fane's mercenaries (NPC-065) to hold the pass against "foreign raiders." They're professionals, and they're not evil.
- **A. Out-fight them** (Fight 14, the switchbacks).
- **B. Out-bid them** (Tollhaven money), **or** show the Merrow ledger proves their contract was paid with stolen funds, which voids it under company law.
- **C. Out-lawyer them:** the contract says "foreign raiders," and the party aren't foreign. Ulric honors the letter, steps aside, and warns them the money came from Emberford.
- **D. Mixed.** Start the fight, then parley at half strength (Ulric's rule), then out-lawyer on the snow with blood on everyone's boots. Often the most satisfying.

### ★4 · Ursk's bones (Gideon's second oath)
At the shrine on Bearback's ridge, where the Mountain Bear died holding the mountain gate, Gideon is **marked**: Ursk's echo, a vast shape of snow and stone, recognizes the oath taken at the Ford Stone and gives it a name.
- **A. Gideon goes to the shrine on purpose.**
- **B. The fight at the pass is fought *on* the shrine,** and Ursk's echo answers when Gideon stands over a fallen ally.
- **C. A Hushed thing has crossed through the cracked Deep Spring** and is desecrating the bones. Gideon defends them (Fight 15). *(This route runs by default if the party reaches the shrine at all; A and B layer onto it.)*

### ★5 · The dwarves break the oath
King Torvald Deepanvil (NPC-066), seeing the Bear's mark on Gideon, breaks the Oath of the Quiet Stone: glimmer is seal-song, mined glimmer thins the seal, and the Gate War drowned Old Varrow because of it. **SEC-11 revealed; SEC-05 proven** with dwarven records, usable in Emberford.
- **A. At the Anvil Throne,** after SC 10-B (Torvald sees the mark).
- **B. At the shrine.** Princess Brynja (NPC-067) comes up the ridge with a Hearth Guard patrol during Fight 15 or just after, sees the echo, and brings her grandfather the next morning (he climbs the ridge himself, at a hundred and ninety, and kneels).
- **C. If Gideon isn't marked** (the party never reached the shrine, or left before the echo): Brynja breaks the oath herself. "My grandfather is a stone. I'm not. You defended the Bear's bones. That's enough for me." She gives them the records (SEC-11, SEC-05) on her own authority. Torvald is furious, then proud. The proof is the same. Gideon's mark waits (see off-route).

## Scenes

### S10.1 · North
Upriver from Emberford: 4 days by barge to Copperhill, portaging at the Kettle Steps rapids. Or the King's Road: 5 days. The air goes cold and thin. Copper dust turns the river banks green-orange.
- **Simon's reply** arrives if his debt was cleared in Ch9 ("Slate's clean. Come for supper."). One line. Let it sit.

### S10.2 · Copperhill (★2)
The Square, the bout ring, smelter smoke, miners who love a fight.
- **Hilde Strand** (NPC-064): broad, grey-braided, fifties, four-time champion, fair and tired. Older-sister and mentor type; no romance. She's been looking for a worthy successor for two years. "I'm tired of being the best fighter in a town of drunks. Show me something."
- **Gert Volker** (NPC-148), a swaggering mercenary who wants the Seat, is in town too. He's been telling everyone the Champion's gone soft. He'll challenge whoever wins a bout against Hilde, the next day (side window).
- **The warrant.** Hilde issues Pass Road warrants. She'll give one to anyone who earns it (wins a bout, does the job, or tells her honestly what's up the mountain).
- **Fight 13** (route A), or **the job** (route B: she pays 150 gp to clear the road), or **route C** (she finds them in the Barge Head tavern).
- **If Ash or Gideon wins the Seat:** they *rule Copperhill for a year*. It's real. Hilde is delighted ("Finally I can fish"). The winner must judge one dispute before leaving (a funny scene: two miners and a goat). Ruling from afar is fine; Hilde serves as their deputy. **A big funny possibility; let it happen.** It also gives the party the Champion's Hundred (militia) for Ch13 or Act III, if they ever need fifty angry miners.

### S10.3 · Wolfsgate and the Wolf Hall (★3)
A high valley. A town that sells swords. **Nobody may draw steel inside the Wolf Hall.**
- **Captain-General Ulric Fane** (NPC-065): hard, practical, wolf-pelt cloak, keeps his word to the letter, never past it. He receives them in the Wolf Hall: long tables, wolf banners, a ledger on a lectern.
- **The contract.** Ulric will show it if asked politely (he's proud of his paperwork). Key lines (read them aloud):
  > *"…to hold Bearback Pass and the Pass Road above Wolfsgate against foreign raiders until the first of Frostcome… Payment: 2,000 gold, half advanced… Signed for the client: L. Hask, Warden-Captain."*
  - **SEC-04 seed 5 / SEC-17 seed 3:** Lucan Hask's signature. Zeke may know the name (the lamp-warden captain). Gideon remembers a lamp-warden with a burned hand from Ch2, if that happened.
  - **Out-lawyer (route C):** "foreign raiders." The party is from Emberford. Investigation or Insight DC 16 to spot it, or just read the clause. Ulric, after a long pause: "...It does say that." He honors the letter. He also says: "The money came from Emberford. From the Guild's lamp-wardens. If I were you, I'd ask who in your city wants you kept out of these mountains."
  - **Out-bid (route B):** 2,500 gp beats it (more than the party probably has). **Or** the Merrow ledger shows the advance was paid in Merrow bearer notes reported *stolen*: company law voids a contract paid with stolen coin (Ulric checks the note numbers against the ledger: he's thorough). He's angry at Hask, not at the party.
  - **If the party has neither:** they can try both arguments at once in Fight 14's parley (★3 route D).
- **Sergeant-Mage Ilse Brandt** (NPC-149): the company's hedge-war-mage, blunt and amused. She knows Zeke is a caster the moment she sees his hands. "Abjurer? Oh, good. I hate evokers."

### S10.6 · Ursk's bones (★4; Fight 15; Moss's upgrade #3)
The shrine is a ring of standing stones on the ridge's highest hump, where the ridge looks like a sleeping bear's spine. Inside the ring, half-buried in snow and moss, the ribs of a bear the size of a house. Dwarven offerings (iron rings, carved tokens) everywhere. Below, cut into the ridge, a cracked stone well: the old mountain gate's high shaft, sealed with dwarven iron, now split.
- **Fight 15:** the Hushed giant climbing out of the cracked well (see encounters).
- **Ursk's echo arrives** (phase 2). When it does, describe it big: the snow on the ridge rises into the shape of a bear the size of a hill, made of snow and stone and the wind's voice, and it **looks at Gideon**.
  > *(Not words. A feeling in the chest like a hand on the back. Then a name, in a voice like a landslide far away:)* ***"Hearth-Warden."***
  - The oath sworn at the Ford Stone is named: **Gideon is the Bear's chosen, the first in four hundred years.**
- **Gideon's mark (EVT-17).** His Aura of Protection is visibly bear-shaped from now on: a faint warm shimmer like a great paw over his allies. Story: greywilt won't enter it (already true since Ch8, now visible to all). Dwarves kneel to it.
  - **Mechanical boon (chapter-writer addition, logged as CHG-A2-04):** ***Bear's Ward.*** Once per long rest, when a creature in Gideon's aura would drop to 0 HP, Gideon can use his reaction to have it drop to 1 HP instead, and the bear-shape flares. Small, flavorful, and survivable-campaign-friendly.
- **Moss's story upgrade #3 (the Bearback shrine piece).** After the fight, Moss walks into the ring of stones and puts his hand on the bear's skull. Something green and old answers from the stone: a piece of himself left here when the Wardens were four. He grows a little taller (an inch, not more; Aggie will notice). In firelight, his shadow has antlers. From now on he can cast ***Commune with Nature*** and ***Speak with Plants*** at will, without a slot. (If his upgrade #2 was delayed by a Ch6 failure, it returns now too.) Moss, quietly, to the bones: "Hello, old friend. Sorry I was late."
- **Relationship beat:** Gideon and Moss alone at the shrine after. R-003 has changed since Ch7. Gideon: "You carried him. He carried this." (the mountain). "I think I'm meant to carry them." Moss doesn't say anything. He just stands next to him. That's the scene.

### S10.7 · Dun Morrach (★5; SC 10-B)
Down the far side of the pass into the hold under the mountain. Warm stone, forge-glow, hot-spring steam, echoing song. Outsiders need a guest-token past the Front Gate halls.
- **Princess Brynja** (NPC-067), Torvald's granddaughter and heir: a warrior-engineer in her late thirties with grease on her knuckles and a laugh like a hammer on an anvil. Older-sister to Gideon at once (R-087: "a good man with an old oath who needs teaching"); no romance. She'll be the one who gets the party in, through the Front Gate's guest-hall, by vouching for "the Bear's chosen" (if she saw the echo) or because Hilde Strand sent a letter (if Hilde is a friend).
- **King Torvald Deepanvil** (NPC-066), on the Anvil Throne: old, stubborn, honorable. He'll see them. He won't speak of glimmer. SC 10-B.
- **When he sees the mark (★5 route A):** he stands. Then, slowly, he kneels, and every dwarf in the hall goes down with a sound like a rockfall. R-086 flips: "the Bear's chosen, the first in 400 years."
- **The oath broken.** Torvald, in the King's Hall, with the Archivist Sigrun Deeplore (NPC-158) bringing the oldest records in iron-bound slates:
  1. Glimmer is not a mineral. It's **seal-song**: the song that holds the world in two, crystallized where the realms touch.
  2. Mined glimmer **thins the seal**. The dwarves mined it for a century. They cracked the Deep Spring under this mountain.
  3. **The Gate War.** The Stillness came through. **Ursk died holding the mountain gate shut.** And at Old Varrow, where the human kings mined glimmer too, the gate breached and the city drowned.
  4. The dwarves swore the **Oath of the Quiet Stone**: never mine it, never speak of why, so no one would go looking.
  - **SEC-11 revealed. SEC-05 proven.** The slates are proof the Concord will have to hear. Torvald gives the party **certified copies** under the Anvil seal (and Brynja carries the originals south later if needed).
- **Seeds:**
  - **SEC-03 (seed 7):** Torvald, looking at Ash: ***"The old songs say the Ford-watchers came in pairs, sometimes."*** He means nothing by it. Hindsight.
  - **SEC-12:** the archive has a slate of a sea-chart: "the Island that Sleeps," in the Glass, marked with the same keyhole glyph Zeke saw on the astrolabe. Zeke will lose his mind.
  - **SEC-15 (seed 2):** Torvald: ***"The Queen of the Leaf knows what we know. She chose differently."*** He won't say more ("not mine to tell").
- **Brynja joins (EVT-53).** She asks to come south: "Somebody has to make sure the Bear's chosen doesn't trip over his own oath." She's an ally (not a party member): she'll appear in Ch12–13 and Act III. Relationship beat available any time: Brynja teaching Gideon dwarven oath-forms. Gideon calls her "Brynja" and she calls him "Fenwick" and then, by the end of the chapter, "little brother."

## Encounters

### FIGHT 13 · The Copperhill Bout · Ch10 · Contest (non-lethal) · Party level 10
**Not a tuned combat; a show.** Three rounds in the Square's ring, one challenger against Hilde Strand. No killing, no magic, no cutting weapons (blunted blades or fists; Ash's daggers are swapped for wooden practice blades, same stats, bludgeoning). The crowd scores it.
- **Scoring:** each hit = 1 point; a hit for 15+ damage in one blow = 2 points; knocking the other prone = 2 points; forcing them out of the 20-ft ring = 3 points. A fighter "knocked out" (dropped to 0 HP: they're unconscious, not dying, by bout rules) loses at once. Most points after 3 rounds wins.
- **Hilde's math:** she's a CR 9-weight champion. Against Ash (AC ~18 at L10), her 3 attacks at +9 hit ~60%: about 3 × 11 × 0.6 ≈ 20 damage a round. Ash has ~73 HP: he won't be knocked out in 3 rounds unless he's careless. Points are the real fight.
- **Panache** works on her (she's hostile in the ring, Insight +5): she grins. "Oh, I *like* you."
- **Gideon vs Hilde:** two walls hitting each other. The crowd goes insane.
- **Moss in beast form:** illegal ("no magic"). Hilde laughs. "Next year, bear."
- **Win:** the Champion's Seat (S10.2), 100 gp, the Champion's Belt (a gaudy copper belt: worth 50 gp, worth more as a dare), and the warrant. **Lose:** the warrant anyway if the bout was good ("Lose like that every day and you'll be fine").

### FIGHT 14 · The Switchbacks · Ch10 · Significant · Party level 10

**Target numbers (L10):** total HP **410** · enemy DPR **58** (12% of 482) · AC 15–17 · attack +6–+8 · DC 14–15.
**HP math:** Ulric Fane 130 + 3 Grey Wolf Veterans × 55 = 165 + 2 Grey Wolf Crossbows × 40 = 80 + Sergeant-Mage Ilse Brandt 45 → **420**. Ulric parleys at half strength (by his rule), which typically leaves **~80–100 HP unspent**: effective ≈ 330. Party output 91 × 5.5 ≈ 500 × ~70% (switchbacks, rockslide, collapse) ≈ 350. **Expected 5–6 rounds, or 4 plus a parley.**
**DPR math (vs AC ~18):** Ulric 3 × 10.5 × 0.55 = 17.3; Veterans 3 × (2 × 7.5 × 0.55 = 8.25) = 24.8; Crossbows 2 × (8.5 × 0.6) = 10.2; Ilse ~6 (cantrip/spell average) → **~58**.

**Battlefield: the switchbacks below the ridge.** The Pass Road zig-zags up a slope of scree and snow in four legs, each 40 ft long, 10 ft wide, each leg 15 ft above the last.
1. **The legs.** Moving up a leg directly (climbing the slope between legs) is a climb (Athletics DC 13; Ash: free). Running the road is slow (it doubles back).
2. **The Wolves' barricade** at the top of leg 3: sharpened stakes and a wagon. Half cover.
3. **The scree slope.** Loose. Anyone who falls prone on it slides 10 ft down to the next leg (1d6 bludgeoning).
4. **The snow-bridge.** Leg 4 crosses a gully on an old snow-bridge, 30 ft long, over a 60-ft drop. Phase 3's terrain.
5. **The rock-shelf** above leg 4, where Ilse and the crossbows stand. It's loose (phase 2).

**Opening.** The Wolves know the party is coming (Hask's description). Ulric stands on leg 3 with his veterans; crossbows and Ilse on the rock-shelf. A Wolf herald calls down the contract's terms: "Turn back, by order of contract. No offense meant." (A real chance to talk: ★3 routes B/C here, if not already done.)

**Enemy plan.** Professionals. They hold high ground and make the party climb into crossbow fire. Veterans hold the barricade in pairs. Ilse counters the biggest spell. Ulric fights at the front and calls the parley himself at half strength (company rule: "never spend men on a contract that's gone bad").

**PHASE 1 · rounds 1–3 · "Up the road"**
- The party climbs; the Wolves shoot and hold.
- **Counter (Zeke's Shield spell):** the crossbows and Ilse use **volleys and saves**. *Telegraph:* Ilse shouts "Wizard! Make him spend it!" and both crossbows shoot Zeke in the same round, one after the other (to burn his reaction on the first). Ilse uses save-based spells against him, never attack rolls.

**PHASE 2 · trigger: round 3–4 or when the party reaches leg 3 · "The rockslide"**
- *Telegraph (end of round 3):* Ilse plants a hand on the rock-shelf and the stones hum (she's prepared a rockslide charge: the Wolves do this on every pass job).
- **TERRAIN change:** the rockslide comes down legs 1–3. Each creature on legs 1–3 makes a DC 15 Dex save: 4d10 bludgeoning and prone and slides to the leg below on a failure; half on a success. Legs 1–2 become difficult terrain (rubble). The barricade on leg 3 is buried, and the Wolves fall back to leg 4.
- **Counter (Ash's Cunning Action Hide):** the Wolves' **war-dog**, Gristle, comes out (a Mastiff: AC 12, 12 HP, keen smell). *Telegraph:* a bark from the ridge, and Gristle bounds down the scree, nose to the snow. While Gristle lives, Ash can't stay hidden within 30 ft of him. (Gristle is a good dog. Killing him makes Ulric *very* angry; knocking him out, befriending him with jerky (Animal Handling DC 14), or Moss speaking to him (story upgrade #1: automatic) is better.)

**PHASE 3 · trigger: round 5–6 or Ulric at half · "The snow-bridge"**
- **Parley first.** At half HP, Ulric raises an open hand: "Hold! Company rule." Talk. ★3 routes B, C, D all work here. If the party refuses to talk, the fight continues onto the snow-bridge.
- **TERRAIN / OBJECTIVE change:** the snow-bridge is collapsing under the rockslide's weight. Anyone on it at the start of a round makes a DC 13 Dex save or falls through to a ledge 20 ft down (2d6, and must climb back: Athletics DC 15). From round 7 the bridge falls entirely. Wolves on it are in as much danger as the party: **saving a Wolf from the fall** (an action, Athletics DC 14 to grab) makes Ulric stand the company down at once, contract or no contract: "You don't let a man fall who's trying to kill you. Fine. *Fine.*"

**FINAL BEAT.** Parley (most likely), rout, or the Wolves fall back to Wolfsgate. Ulric's last word, either way: ***"The coin came from your city. From Emberford's Guild. A warden-captain with a burned hand. If I were you, I'd watch who's paying to keep you out of these mountains."***
**Fail-forward.** If the party loses: the Wolves don't kill disabled enemies (company rule: ransom pays better). The party wakes in Wolfsgate's Wolf Hall cells, bandaged. Ulric offers terms: "You go home. Or you pay." Hilde Strand (if she's a friend) rides up and pays (150 gp; she'll want it back). Or Brynja does. The pass is then open by the contract's expiry (Frostcome)... which is too late; so Brynja takes them through a dwarven tunnel instead (★4 still reached, by the tunnel's shrine stair).
**Rewards.** See treasure.
**DM notes.** 5–6 rounds, or 4 and a parley. If the party is cruising, Ilse's rockslide comes early. If the party is hurting, Ulric calls the parley a round early.

### FIGHT 15 · The Gate-Breaker at Ursk's Bones · Ch10 · Significant · Party level 10

**Target numbers (L10):** total HP **410** · enemy DPR **58** · AC 15–17 · attack +6–+8 · DC 14–15.
**HP math:** Thurvig the Gate-Breaker 260 + 2 Gate War Shieldbearers × 75 = 150 → **410**. In phase 2 Ursk's echo arrives and **lays one Shieldbearer to rest** at once (−75), but the giant's *Gate-Breath* phase gives it **+40 temporary HP** (the cracked spring feeding it). Effective ≈ **375**. Party output 91 × 6 ≈ 546 × ~70% (snow, the bones to protect, the echo scene) ≈ 380. **Expected 6 rounds.**
**DPR math (vs AC ~18):** Thurvig 2 × 25.5 × 0.55 = 28; Shieldbearers 2 × (2 × 8 × 0.55 = 8.8) = 17.6; Cold of the Stillness aura ~8 per round across the party → **~54–58**. Spike: *Gate-Breath* cone, 8d8 cold (36) × ~2.5 targets × 0.6 ≈ 54 extra, once.

**Battlefield: the shrine on the ridge.**
1. **The ring of standing stones.** Ten stones, each a 5-ft pillar: full cover. They hum when struck.
2. **Ursk's bones.** The rib-cage arch in the ring's center: half cover inside it. The giant is trying to smash them (objective).
3. **The cracked well.** A 10-ft-wide dwarven-iron well-cap, split. Still, freezing water underneath. It's the old mountain gate's high shaft. Things come up out of it.
4. **The ridge edges.** Steep. A creature shoved off the ridge slides 30 ft down the snow (3d6 bludgeoning; climbing back is Athletics DC 15 and two rounds).
5. **Snow.** The whole ridge is difficult terrain except the ring's center (blown clear).

**Opening.** The party reaches the ridge (after Fight 14 or the parley). The well-cap is split. Thurvig, a frost giant of the Gate War, grey and glassy and enormous, is climbing out, with two Hushed dwarven dead in rusted Gate War mail, and he's prying at the bear's skull with a dwarven iron spar. Gideon feels it in his teeth before he sees it.

**Enemy plan.** Thurvig wants the bones broken (the Stillness hates what's left of the Bear: the bones still help hold the shaft shut). The Shieldbearers guard him. They don't care about the party unless the party gets between them and the bones.

**PHASE 1 · rounds 1–3 · "The desecration"**
- Thurvig smashes at the bones. Every round he isn't engaged, he deals 1 "bone damage" (track 3: at 3, the skull cracks, and phase 3's objective gets harder).
- **OBJECTIVE:** get Thurvig away from the bones (shove, Panache-style taunt, Gideon's Abjure Foes, damage that makes him turn).
- **Counter (Moss's wild shape as a front-line beast):** Thurvig's big single hits. *Telegraph:* the giant shifts its grip on the spar and *aims* at the antlered beast in front of it. Its blows burn temporary HP; its *Grip of the Old Cold* restrains (see block).

**PHASE 2 · trigger: round 3–4, or the moment an ally of Gideon's drops, or Gideon stands over the bones · "The Bear answers"**
- *Telegraph (end of round 3):* the standing stones start to hum without being struck. The snow around Gideon's boots lifts, very slightly, and hangs there.
- **TERRAIN / FORM change (in the party's favor):** **Ursk's echo arrives.** See the ally block below. The ring's snow lifts into the shape of the bear; one Shieldbearer is laid to rest at once (it kneels, and its rust falls away to an old dwarf's face, and it's gone). Gideon is marked (★4, EVT-17 begins).
- Thurvig answers with **Gate-Breath** (the cracked spring feeds him): he gains 40 temporary HP and his breath recharges.

**PHASE 3 · trigger: round 5–6, or Thurvig below one-third · "Seal the shaft"**
- **OBJECTIVE change:** the cracked well is opening wider (the Stillness doesn't want to lose this). Something else is climbing (show a grey hand, then another). Close it or hold it:
  - Ash can **hold it steady** (as in Ch8: Performance DC 17, or Perception DC 15 to find the key note and hold it; he can't close it for good: Hearing only).
  - Gideon can **slam the cap shut** with the echo's help (Athletics DC 15 with advantage while the echo stands; it takes his action).
  - Zeke can **re-ward the dwarven iron** (Arcana DC 16; Spell Breaker makes it automatic if he spends a 3rd-level slot on *dispel magic* "backwards," which is a lovely Zeke moment).
  - Any **two** of these succeeding seals the shaft for this age. Thurvig loses his temporary HP and *Gate-Breath*.
- If the skull reached 3 bone damage in phase 1, the cap needs **three** of these instead of two.

**FINAL BEAT.** Thurvig dropped to 0 HP is **laid to rest**, not killed: the grey runs out of him like meltwater and for one second he's a frost giant with braided hair and a face full of grief, who looks at the bear's bones and says, in Giant, "I'm sorry, old one." Then he's snow. (Zeke or anyone with Giant understands. Otherwise, Moss translates, softly.)
**Fail-forward.** If the party is losing: the echo takes the giant itself (it walks into him, and both are snow), but the shaft isn't sealed: it cracks wider (a "dark" result: the mountain gate leaks until Act III; SEAL is unaffected now, but Ch16's Ursk's Den gate is weaker). Gideon is still marked. The dwarves still believe.
**Rewards.** EVT-17 (with ★5). Moss's upgrade #3. The giant's spar is dwarven iron (see treasure).
**DM notes.** Expected 6 rounds. The echo is a turning point, not a win button: it lays one Shieldbearer to rest and buffs Gideon, and that's all. Don't let it fight the giant for the party.
