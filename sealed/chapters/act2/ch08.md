## ★ Must-happen beats

### ★1 · The river is shown
Barge life: poling, river ports, the Glasswater's size. One or two travel encounters.
- **A. Working passage on a barge** (free; the *Dun Heifer*, Skipper Ottilie Bask, NPC-157, a big-armed river-clan woman who sings to her poles). 1.5 days to the Glasswater shore, then a raft out.
- **B. A fast packet boat** (10 gp per day each; half the time; it's the boat the toll-cutter stops first).
- **C. Overland on the King's Road** (slower; 3 days) to the Glasswater shore at Netyard Landing, then hire a raft. Swap Fight 8 for a road version (the same Purse men, on horseback at a toll-bridge).
- **D. Old Bram or the Tolleys keep going.** If Ch7's boat was Bram's ferry-barge, Bram takes them all the way ("Haven't been to the lake in thirty years. Do me good.").

### ★2 · Granny Wenna Tull's song
The Eldest Mother sings the barge-clan song about "the ford-watchers and the Stag who never grows old." She looks right at Moss (R-089). She agrees to shelter Lark. A **lashing** vote of the Seven Mothers is needed: win at least 4 of 7 (SC 8-A).
- **A. Ash makes the case to the Mothers.** SC 8-A as written.
- **B. Lark wins them over herself** by singing to the otter-folk refugees' babies. (This counts as 2 successes in SC 8-A and can't fail, but it costs Lark: her greying doesn't heal this chapter, because she sang too long.)
- **C. If the vote fails, Granny Tull breaks tradition.** "I'm old. Let them cut my thread." She shelters Lark on *The Patient Goose* anyway. (The Drift's goodwill drops one step, and one Mother, Dace Corran, sulks through the raid. Nothing worse.)

### ★3 · Refugees
Otter-folk, hare-folk and badger-folk families are hiding on the Drift. More beastfolk means something is wrong. The scale should land.
- **A.** Seen on arrival at the Drift (the refugee barge, the *Kindly Turn*, forty souls).
- **B.** Met on the river before arrival: a raft of otter-folk families drifting, lost, out of food. (Merrilee, NPC-145, is their elder.)
- **C.** Met in Tollhaven's harbor jail, held by the Purse men as "unlicensed cargo" (if the party skipped the Drift and went straight to Tollhaven). A quick jailbreak or a fine of 5 gp a head.

### ★4 · The drift-gate raid (Fight 9)
Brannoc's raiders come up through a minor Wellgate under the lake at night. **SEAL +1 (EVT-14)** whether or not the raid is beaten: the point is that the gates are being used.
- **A.** At night on the Drift (as written).
- **B.** On the river before arrival: the raiders hit the party's barge after tracking Lark (through a still back-water at Reedsend).
- **C.** At the Glasswater shore camp, if the party is camped on land.

### ★5 · The storm (SC#3)
A greywilt storm: the Glasswater goes still in the middle of a storm, which is impossible. The Drift's lashings start to break.
- **A.** On the Drift (as written).
- **B.** On open water on the party's barge, with the Drift visible and struggling (save the barge, then help the Drift: the stations shift one link).
- **C.** In Tollhaven's harbor, where the Drift has sheltered: the challenge becomes "hold the harbor chain" (same structure; stations re-skinned to harbor chains, the Toll Guard, and the refugee barge against the mole).

## Scenes

### S8.1 · The first camp (level up)
Level 8 here. A riverbank camp below Hobb's Ferry. Fireflies; Lark has never seen them ("Your stars came down"). **Relationship beat:** Zeke teaching Lark to read from the Ferry inn's bill of fare; Lark teaching Zeke a warren song he gets wrong on purpose so she'll laugh.
- **Hobb's Ferry** (if they stop): Old Nan Pike (NPC-075) knows everything that crosses. If SC 7-A ended at 2+ Pressure, she says: "Two Guild men asked after a family with a 'sick child in a hood.' I told them I'm deaf. I'm not." Tobin Pike (NPC-076) offers to pole them as far as the lake for 5 gp.

### S8.2 · Barge life (★1)
Show the Tamber widening, mud-brown to green-silver. Poling (Athletics checks for fun, not stakes), the *Dun Heifer*'s cook frying eels, river ports with lamp-sellers (glimmer prices chalked up: higher every port; Simon Shaw would sigh). Skipper Bask's songs.
- **The Hushed heron flock** (hazard, not a fight). Thirty grey herons stand in the shallows, not fishing, all turned toward the barge. When Lark hums, every head turns at once. If SC 7-A ended at 4 Pressure, they take off together and follow for an hour (the Heron now knows the barge). Otherwise they only watch. Moss: "Hers. Don't look them in the eye." A Wisdom save DC 14 for anyone who does, or 1d10 psychic and a minute of wanting to sit down in the water.
- **Fight 8 · the toll-boat** (if triggered). See encounters.

### S8.3 · The Glasswater goes still at the turn of light
At sunset on the lake, the whole Glasswater goes glass-flat for one minute. The *Heifer*'s crew don't even notice; they say it's always like that. Lark grips Ash's sleeve.
- **Zeke's discovery moment (mapping silence).** Zeke can hear the "silence" as a shape. With an Arcana check (DC 16; or automatic if he spends the minute with his eyes shut), he sketches it: the stillness isn't random, it's *patterned*, a ring around a point under the Drift's usual anchorage. That point is the drift-gate. From now on, in SC#3 he can predict where still patches will spread (see the storm's "still patch" station: he grants advantage to whoever works it).

### S8.4 · Arrival at the Drift; refugees (★3)
Driftmarket from the water: a quarter-mile of lashed barges, cooking smoke, children diving, music. The **Long Plank** boardwalk. The **Galley Ring**. The **Chapel Barge**. **Tull's Raft**, where the Seven Mothers' barges sit in a ring.
- **The refugee barge, the *Kindly Turn*.** Forty beastfolk: otter-folk (most), a hare-folk family from Hollowmere who know Lark by sight and weep, a badger-folk sett-elder (Brock Underhill) who growls at the humans and gives them his cider anyway.
- **Merrilee (NPC-145),** otter-folk elder, bubbly even in grief. She says what refugees know (SEC-05 seed): ***"It's the Tall Folk's singing stones. The Regent says it, and he's a brute, but the stones are true. Our river-songs don't work any more. The water doesn't answer."***
- If Plish is aboard, he finds Merrilee (his aunt). Give the scene a breath.
- **The scale.** Merrilee: "There were six rafts when we came through. There are two now." Where did the others go? Some to Tollhaven (jailed as cargo, ★3 route C), some to Bramblecog's millpond (EVT-14 territory), some "went still."

### S8.5 · The Patient Goose (★2)
Granny Wenna Tull (NPC-062) never leaves *The Patient Goose*. She's nearly ninety, small, sharp as a hook, in a shawl full of fish-bone needles. She has a pot of tea and a lap-cat that's mostly whiskers.
- **The song.** She asks Moss to sit. Then she sings the old one, in a cracked strong voice:
  > *Two at the ford with the lamp held high,*
  > *one to hear and one to cry,*
  > *and the Stag who never grows old stands by,*
  > *till the river runs dry, till the river runs dry.*
  ("One to hear and one to cry" is a **SEC-03 seed**: the gift splits in pairs, sometimes. Nobody should catch it now. Hindsight.)
- She looks straight at Moss through every line. He looks at his tea. Then she says, "Well. You took your time." (R-089 plays as written: she believes he's the Stag of the barge-songs and will vouch for him and his charge.)
- **SEC-12 seed:** later, over tea, unprompted: ***"My granddad's granddad sailed past an island in the Glass that breathed. Up and down, like a sleeping dog. He never sailed south again."***
- She agrees to shelter Lark, but says the Drift decides together. Hence SC 8-A.

### S8.8 · The drift-gate raid (★4; Fight 9)

The refugee children crowd behind him. From now on, greywilt terrain effects (still water, Hushed auras) don't affect creatures inside his aura while he's conscious (story upgrade before the formal Ch10 mark; it's what makes Torvald believe him).

### S8.10 · After: Lark stays (EVT-13)
The Drift holds. Lark will stay with Granny Tull on *The Patient Goose*, hidden in the heart of the town, where still water is broken every few minutes by children diving. ("Nobody's ever still on the Drift, love. Not for a minute.")
- **Goodbye.** Lark, blunt: "You're going to go fight the people who hurt you, aren't you. Come back. Promise like Gran promises: with your ears." (She makes him hold his ears. Pay it off later.)
- **Umbari seed (00 §7 setup, Ch8):** at bedtime, Lark asks Ash for a story, and then tells him one instead: "Gran Umbari says when the world was one, there was a song so long it took a whole elephant to remember it. She's still remembering. She says she's remembering it for somebody." Lark yawns. "Maybe it's you."
- **EVT-13** fires. **Level 9.**
- **Route C to Ch9:** Granny Tull's grandson **Coble Tull** (NPC-144) mentions a Merrow agent in Tollhaven buying "every chip of glimmer on the lake."

### FIGHT 8 · The Toll-Cutter · Ch8 · Ordinary · Party level 8

**Target numbers (L8):** total HP **205** · enemy DPR **39** · AC 15–17 · attack +6–+8 · DC 14–15.
**When:** SC 7-A Pressure 2+ (or the player wants a river fight anyway; then they're plain lake pirates, "Red Tilly Hake's lot," same blocks).
**HP math:** Purser Lusk 60 + 4 Toll Bravos × 36 = 144 → **204**. Party output 74 × 3.5 rounds ≈ 260 at full; ~80% efficiency on a moving deck ≈ 205. **Expected 3–4 rounds.**
**DPR math:** Bravos 2 × 7.5 × 0.55 = 8.25 each → 33; Lusk crossbow 6.6 + net (no damage) → **~39–40**.

**Battlefield.** The *Tithe-Cutter*, a sleek twin-masted toll-boat with Tollhaven's purse-and-scales painted on the sail, comes alongside. Features: (1) the boarding plank between hulls (5 ft wide; shove a creature off with a successful Shove); (2) the cutter's boom (Athletics DC 14 to swing it: a 15-ft line, DC 14 Dex save or 2d6 bludgeoning and knocked prone or overboard); (3) the gap of water (it's not still water; it's just cold: swimming is difficult terrain, and armor makes Athletics DC 15 to stay up); (4) cargo nets (a creature can cut one to drop it: Restrained, escape DC 13).

**Opening.** Lusk hails them politely: "Toll inspection, by weight. And a passenger manifest. We're looking for a family with a sick child." Talk is a real option:
- **Pay the toll** by weight (12 gp) and lie about the manifest (Deception DC 16; Lusk has a description). Success: they wave the barge on, and Lusk writes the barge's name in his book anyway.
- **Bribe him** (50 gp and Persuasion DC 14). He takes it and *still* writes the name.
- **Ash picks his pocket** during the inspection (Sleight of Hand vs his Perception +3; Ash wins). He gets the book (**Ch9 route C seed**: "M.— buying all chips, lake-wide. Report hare-child to House M. at once.").
- **Fight.**

**Enemy plan.** Not a death-match. They want the "child" and they want to get paid. Two Bravos board; two stay and shoot. Lusk nets whoever looks like the leader.
- **Counter (Zeke's area spells):** the Bravos fight **spread out** across both decks and the plank, never within 10 ft of one another. *Telegraph:* as soon as Zeke raises his hands, Lusk shouts "Spread! He's a caster!" and they do. (Only one counter: this is an ordinary fight.)
- **One twist (round 3):** if the fight's still going, the cutter's crew *rams*: both decks lurch (DC 13 Dex save or prone), and the cutter starts to pull away with whoever is aboard it. Chasing it is a jump (Athletics or Acrobatics DC 15, or Ash's 18-ft long jump).

**Surrender.** Bravos surrender at half strength. Lusk surrenders when alone. He's a clerk with a crossbow. He'll trade the book for his life.

**Rewards.** Toll strongbox: 140 gp. Lusk's book (seed). Bravos' purses: 30 gp.

### FIGHT 9 · The Drift-Gate Raid · Ch8 · Significant · Party level 8

**Target numbers (L8):** total HP **335** · enemy DPR **47** (12% of 388) · AC 15–17 · attack +6–+8 · DC 14–15.
**HP math:** Raid-Captain Dughal 110 + 3 Horned Host Raiders × 45 = 135 + Hushed Rennet Pike 90 → **335**. Party output 74 × 5.5 rounds ≈ 407; ~80% efficiency (split barges, jumps, the hostage) ≈ 325. **Expected 5–6 rounds.**
**DPR math (vs AC ~17):** Dughal 2 × 14 × 0.6 = 16.8; Raiders 3 × (10.5 × 0.6) = 18.9 (plus gores on charges, ~2); Pike bite 15 × 0.6 = 9 → **~47**. Spike in phase 2 when the lashings part (falls and the pike's swallow) to ~55.

**Battlefield (the Drift at night).**
1. **The Blind Eye.** The drift-gate: a round, perfectly still pool of water 15 ft across, in a gap between three barges where the Drift always anchors. Lanterns on poles around it. It never reflects the lanterns right.
2. **The Long Plank.** A swaying quarter-mile boardwalk. Difficult terrain at a run; Acrobatics DC 12 to fight on it without disadvantage on attacks.
3. **The *Kindly Turn*.** The refugee barge, 30 ft from the Blind Eye. Forty noncombatants.
4. **The lashings.** Thick ropes binding barge to barge. In phase 2 the raiders cut them.
5. **The Chapel Barge.** A bell. Ringing it wakes the whole Drift (bonus action, adjacent): from the next round, river-clan fighters keep the refugees safe so the party doesn't have to.
6. **Water between barges.** Ordinary lake water, cold and black. The pike lives in it.

**Opening.** Turn of light at midnight? No: the raiders don't need one. They come up through the Blind Eye on a Hushed tide (the Heron opened it from the far side; the Drift's anchorage sat on a minor gate all along). First warning: the lanterns around the Eye go out one by one. Moss's sparrows don't fly over water, so no early warning unless someone was on watch (Perception DC 14, or Ash on watch: automatic).

**Enemy plan.** Two raiders go for the *Kindly Turn* to take hostages (they were told "beastfolk who fled are deserters"). One raider and Dughal search for Lark on Tull's Raft (they have her scent from a dropped hood, if SC 7-A Pressure was 4; otherwise they're searching blind). The pike hunts anyone in the water. Retreat: when Dughal falls or surrenders, the raiders try to drag one hostage back through the Eye.

**PHASE 1 · rounds 1–3 · "Up from the Eye"**
- Raiders rush the refugee barge and Tull's Raft. Dughal bellows challenges at the biggest human he sees (Gideon).
- The pike circles under the barges. Anyone on a plank or edge is a target.

**PHASE 2 · trigger: round 3–4, or when Dughal is bloodied · "The lashings part"**
- *Telegraph (end of round 3):* Dughal roars an order, and two raiders draw short axes and go for the ropes. The Drift creaks.
- **TERRAIN change:** the barges around the Blind Eye come apart. Gaps of 5–15 ft open between the party's positions. Crossing a gap is a jump (Athletics or Acrobatics DC 13 for 10 ft, DC 16 for 15 ft; Ash clears 18 ft). The *Kindly Turn* starts drifting toward the open lake.
- **Counter (Gideon's Protection style):** the current and the parting barges carry protected allies away from him. *Telegraph:* the plank Gideon stands on lurches as the first rope parts. From round 4, at the start of each round, the barge each creature stands on drifts 10 ft in a direction the DM rolls; Gideon can only protect what stays within 5 ft. (Clever answer: lash two barges back together, Athletics DC 15 as an action.)

**PHASE 3 · trigger: round 5–6, or when the pike has swallowed someone · "The last boat"**
- **OBJECTIVE change:** the remaining raiders grab a hostage (a refugee kit, or Coble Tull if he's near) and run for the Blind Eye. If they go under with the hostage, the hostage is in Mirrowen (and shows up in Ch14 as a prisoner the party can free). Stop them: block the Eye, drop a raider, or **Ash hums the gate steady** (once per round, an action: Performance DC 15 or Perception DC 15 to find the key note and hold it; while he holds it, the Eye is "shut" for creatures without a Hearkener's leave, and a raider who reaches it can't go under). This is a foreshadow of Ch9; reward it.
- **Counter (Moss's wild shape):** the Hushed pike grapples and **swallows** a Large-or-smaller beast form (or anyone) and dives. *Telegraph:* its huge grey shape rises under Moss's barge in round 5 and the barge tips. Swallowed: see the block.

**FINAL BEAT.** Raiders down, captured, or gone through the Eye; the hostage saved or not; Dughal captured, dead or gone. **EVT-12** fires. **EVT-14 (SEAL +1)** fires regardless: the gate was used.
**Fail-forward.** If the party is losing, the Drift wakes (the Chapel bell rings itself in the wind, or Granny Tull rings it from the *Goose*), and twenty river-clan polers arrive with boathooks. The raiders take one hostage and go. Lark is never taken from the Drift: she's on the *Goose*, which is the last place they reach.
**Rewards.** Raider iron (see treasure). The CHG-03 line (S8.8).
**DM notes.** Expected 5–6 rounds. If the pike drags things out underwater, end it: it surfaces with whoever it swallowed and spits them out when it takes 20 damage in one turn or when the Eye starts to close.

### FIGHT 10 · The Storm's Combat Beats · Ch8 · (inside SC#3) · Party level 8
Three short combat beats, one per wave of the storm. Each is about **ordinary ÷ 3**: ~70 HP of enemies, ~13 enemy DPR, 1–2 rounds. They're interruptions, not set pieces. Party output 74/round shreds each beat in 1–2 rounds; the danger is what's *not* being done at the stations while they fight.
- **Wave 1:** 2 Stillwracks climb onto the *Kindly Turn* (2 × 35 = 70).
- **Wave 2:** 1 Stillwrack plus the Hushed Rennet Pike, if it survived Fight 9 at under half HP (or a second pike at 35 HP). (~70)
- **Wave 3 · "the raid's last boat":** if any raider escaped Fight 9 with a hostage, they're back on a raft trying to reach the Blind Eye through the storm (one raider, 45 HP, plus 1 Stillwrack). If no raider escaped, 2 Stillwracks instead.
- **Counter for the storm (the healing yo-yo):** Stillwracks drag downed creatures toward the still patch rather than finishing them (an objective change, not an execution). *Telegraph:* the first downed refugee is dragged, not stabbed. A creature dragged into the still patch at 0 HP is lost to the water at the end of the next round unless someone pulls it out (Athletics DC 14 as an action). For a party member at 0 HP that means death saves are made at disadvantage while in the still patch.

### SC#3 · "Hold the Drift" (★5, campaign skill challenge #3) · L8
**Structure (from `campaign/02`):** **10 successes before 4 failures.** The storm comes in **3 waves**. Each wave has a combat beat (Fight 10) and stations. Run each wave as a **chain**: the player assigns each character (and any river-clan ally) to a station for that wave, then resolves each station as one quick check, *in the order the storm hits them*, with the wave's combat beat happening at the same time for whoever isn't on a station.
**Clock:** the still patch at the storm's heart is spreading. Each failure lets it spread one barge-ring inward. At 4 failures it reaches the *Kindly Turn*.
**DCs at L8:** easy 13, standard 15, hard 17. Ash's expertise skills (Stealth, Perception, Sleight of Hand, and Deception or Persuasion) count as near-certain; that's fine for one station per wave, which is why each wave needs four stations covered at once.

**Stations (each wave uses 4–5 of these; re-skin as the storm moves):**

| Station | Checks | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Cut and re-lash** | Athletics DC 15 to haul; Sleight of Hand DC 13 for the knots; Vehicles (water) DC 15 to steer a loose barge back | Two characters can split it: one hauls, one ties (each must succeed; both succeeding = 2 successes) |
| **The sinking refugee barge** | Athletics DC 15 (bail, shore up) · Medicine DC 13 (the drowning) · Persuasion DC 15 (keep forty people from panicking into the water) | If this station fails twice across the waves, refugees scatter: alive, but they turn up in Mirrowen in Ch14 instead of staying on the Drift |
| **The Mothers' lantern-signal** | Performance DC 15 (the signal-song) · Arcana DC 15 (Zeke rigs a glimmer-lamp to flash through the still air) · Ash's Perception DC 13 to read the answering lights | Success lets the far barges know where to pole; next station in this wave gets advantage |
| **The still patch** | Nature or Survival DC 17 to find where it'll spread (Zeke's silence-map: advantage) · **Moss and Lark can wake the water with song** (one success, automatic, once per wave; Lark can do it once per chapter without tiring, after that it advances her greying) · Ash hums the patch awake (Performance DC 17; locket token not available yet) | Gideon standing in the patch makes it stop advancing for this wave (his aura moment) but takes him off any other station |
| **The raid's last boat** | Combat (Fight 10 wave 3) | Defeating the wave's beat in 2 rounds or less counts as 1 success. Taking 3+ rounds counts as a failure. |

**Wave shape:**
- **Wave 1 · "the wind dies":** the storm goes silent in the middle of the rain. Stations: re-lash, refugee barge, lantern-signal, still patch. Beat: 2 Stillwracks.
- **Wave 2 · "the deck goes grey":** fog of greywilt crosses the decks. Gideon's aura moment (S8.9). Stations: refugee barge, still patch, re-lash, lantern-signal. Beat: Stillwrack + pike.
- **Wave 3 · "the eye":** the storm's heart is over the Blind Eye. Stations: the raid's last boat (combat), still patch, refugee barge, re-lash. If the challenge has 8+ successes when wave 3 begins, Ash can try to hum the Blind Eye *steady* as a final station (Performance DC 17 or Perception DC 15): success counts double.

**Fail-forward (from `campaign/02`):** barges lost, refugees scattered (they show up again in Ch14 in Mirrowen, alive), Driftmarket's goodwill cut down.
- **10 successes first:** the Drift holds. The storm breaks into ordinary, glorious rain. Everyone on the Drift dances in it ("the Lashing Dance," right there on the decks). The Drift's goodwill is at its highest: free passage on any river-clan barge for the rest of the campaign, and the Mothers will come to the gathering (Ch20).
- **4 failures first:** the still patch reaches the *Kindly Turn*. The barge goes under slowly; the party can save the people (no roll; they're heroes) but not the barge or the Drift's outer ring. Three barges are lost. The refugees who scatter reappear in Ch14. Lark is still safe on the *Goose* (it's at the center). Goodwill drops one step.

## If the party goes off-route
- **They skip the Drift and go straight to Tollhaven.** Use ★3 route C (refugees in the harbor jail), ★4 route C or B (the raid hits them at a shore camp or on the river), and ★5 route C (the storm hits Tollhaven's harbor, with the Drift sheltering there). Granny Tull's song happens in the harbor, on the *Goose*, moored at the Netyards. The lashing vote is still needed (it's the Drift's law, not its location).
- **They want Lark to come with them to Tollhaven.** Lark wants to come. Granny Tull says no, then sighs and says, "She'll go where she likes. Hares do." Allow it, with costs: the Heron can find her more easily away from the Drift (the Varga vault fight in Ch9 gets +1 Hushed thing), and Lark's greying doesn't heal. She can go back to the Drift any time by barge. Make sure the player knows the trade.
- **They try to close the Blind Eye.** Ash can't close a gate for good (Hearing, not Voice; CHG-01). He can hold it steady for a while (as in Fight 9 phase 3). If he tries a big closing, he gets a minute's grip and then a nosebleed and the knowledge, in his bones, that this needs a second voice. That's a fine **SEC-03 seed** in hindsight.
- **They kill every raider.** Fine; they're soldiers. Nobody's moralizing. Note that Dughal's surrender option was there; Mercy only drops if he surrendered first.
- **They decide to cross into Mirrowen through the Blind Eye to hit back.** Too early. Moss stops it ("Not with her here. Not tonight."). If Ash insists, the Eye won't open for him without a turn of light yet (he hasn't found a gate's key note on his own; that's Ch9). Let him try, feel the note almost come, and lose it.
