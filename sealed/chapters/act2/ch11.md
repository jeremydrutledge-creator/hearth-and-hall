## ★ Must-happen beats

### ★1 · Hollis's offer (EVT-51)
Hollis invites Zeke to a paid fellowship at the Guild (Glimmer Resonance, 60 gp a month, a workshop on the Row, a key to the Guild library) and Gideon to captain a new "Lamp Guard" (the Lane's own night watch, Guild-funded, with Gideon choosing the men). It's generous and plausible. It's his last attempt to keep them close before removing Ash.
- **A. Formal letters with the Guild seal.**
- **B. Hollis visits the Fenwick house in person** (with Hester) and brings supper (a whole roast goose and a crock of Lamp House pickles).
- **C. At the festival's eve, in public,** on the Guildhall steps, with the Provost watching.
- **Accepted or refused?** Either is a story. **Refusal** (by both) leads Hollis to order Ash "removed quietly" (Fight 16 goes ahead). **Acceptance** (by either brother) gives that brother access (Zeke: the Guild library's restricted shelf, with Reserve tuning notes Clara's been given; Gideon: the Lamp Guard's patrol schedules, which put his own men nowhere near Guildhall Isle on Lantern Night), and Fight 16 *still* goes ahead, because Hollis only wanted the brothers, not Ash. Make sure that lands later.

### ★2 · The clues come together: Clara is Wren (EVT-18 begins)
- **A. Ash puts the seeds together himself** (birthday, eye fleck, the lamps, the lullaby) and confronts the evidence: **Lamp House's admission ledger** (SC 11-A): *"Leaffall YF 1085. Infant girl, about two. Brought by Master Grane."*
- **B. Moss hears Clara humming the lullaby, both verses.** She knows the first verse's words; the second verse she hums without words, the tune only, the "little Wren" verse. Moss realizes. It shatters him: ***"She didn't die. I left her. I left her in the fire."*** (This also fires EVT-47.)
- **C. Hester, at the barge-fire memorial,** says to Gideon without thinking: ***"Hollis brought Clara to me that same autumn, poor mite, she had nobody."*** Gideon goes white (R-017 begins to turn).
- **D. The lamps.** Clara and Ash touch hands (passing a cup, steadying her on the ice, a handshake at the Lamplit) and every lamp on the Row bursts. Glass everywhere. Silence. Then every shopkeeper on the Row looking at them.
- **E. The locket (SEC-23).** When Ash and Clara stand close, both pewter halves pull together on their chains and **click shut**, whole for the first time in nineteen years. Inside, under the two glasses: **"A"** and **"W."** The cleanest proof in the campaign. It can stand alone.

### ★3 · Telling Clara (or not) (SC 11-C)
How, when and whether to tell her. She loves Hollis as a father. She **vouches for him sincerely** (vouch #3, R-050): *"He took me in when no one would. He sat up with me every night I had fever."* She doesn't believe it at first.
- **A. Ash tells her himself.**
- **B. Ash waits** (protecting her), and Clara works it out from a lamp flare and the lullaby and comes to him.
- **C. Hollis tells her a version first** ("those Fenwick boys are troubled; they think every orphan is their lost sister") and Ash has to undo it.
- **Outcome tracked (Twin Bond):** at the end of the chapter Clara (a) **believes and is shaken**, (b) **half-believes and hides it**, or (c) **refuses and goes back to Hollis**. All three lead to Ch12. Only (a) and (b) give the first duet there for free.

## Encounters

### FIGHT 16 · The Lamp-Climb Attack · Ch11 · Significant · Party level 11

**Target numbers (L11):** total HP **490** · enemy DPR **63** (12% of 529) · AC 17–18 · attack +9 to +11 · DC 16–17.
**HP math:** Sgt. Mabry 120 + Bastian Harrow 90 + 2 Quiet Knives × 70 = 140 + 3 Hushed Watchmen × 50 = 150 → **500**. (If Rook is on the fall branch and in the fight, he's extra: 48, not counted; he should be stirred, not killed.) Party output 109 × 6 ≈ 654 × ~75% (the crowd, the collapse, the rescue objective) ≈ 490. **Expected 6 rounds.**
**DPR math (vs AC ~18):** Knives (incl. Bastian) 3 × ~13 = 39 (2 × 8.5 × 0.6 = 10.2, plus poison on one hit ≈ +3); Hushed Watchmen 3 × (10.5 × 0.55 = 5.8) = 17.3; Mabry 2 × 9.5 × 0.55 = 10.5 → **~67**; the crowd crush in phase 1 replaces some attacks with shoves, so it runs **~60–63**.

**Battlefield: Guildhall Isle, festival night.**
1. **The Great Lamp pole.** Sixty feet, greased. Climbers on it are the target. At the top, the Great Lamp (a glimmer globe the size of a barrel).
2. **The crowd.** Two thousand people packed in the square. Moving through the crowd is difficult terrain, and a creature can hide in it (the Knives do). Area spells that catch the crowd hurt innocents: the DM says so *before* the spell resolves, so the player can change it.
3. **The Seven Bridges** (two are close: the Lamplighters' Bridge and the Old Ford Bridge). Narrow, lamp-lit, over cold fast water.
4. **The Guildhall roofs** and the Exchange's rooftop terrace (20–40 ft up). Ash's terrain.
5. **The river.** The Fordwater, fast and dark, around the island.

**Opening.** The climb is under way (SC 11-B). At the moment the climber is 40 ft up, someone in the crowd shouts "The pole! It's going!" and the crowd surges. That's the Knives' signal. If nobody in the party is climbing, the attack comes at Ash in the crowd as the pageant passes.

**Enemy plan.** Make it look like a crowd crush. The Hushed Watchmen push the crowd *toward* Ash (and toward the river). The Knives move in the press and strike from it. Mabry stands on the Guildhall steps "directing crowd control" and calling his men by name. If it goes wrong, Mabry runs for the Old Ford Bridge and the Quiet's chapel.
- **They want Ash dead**, quietly. They don't want Clara hurt (she's in the crowd with Hester: if either is threatened, a Knife will pull back, and the player should see that hesitation).

**PHASE 1 · rounds 1–3 · "The crush"**
- The crowd surges. Each round, each party member on the ground makes a DC 16 Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check or is moved 10 ft in the crowd's direction (toward the river) and knocked prone on a failure by 5 or more.
- The Knives strike from the crowd (Hidden in it: see their *Crowd-Cloak*).
- **Counter (Bart's Help):** the Knives have been told about the owl. *Telegraph (round 1):* a Knife on the Exchange terrace points a hand-crossbow *up*, not at Ash, and says "Bird first." From round 2, one Knife shoots Bart whenever he's helping Ash. (He comes back tomorrow. "A crossbow. For *me*. I'm honored, honestly.")

**PHASE 2 · trigger: round 3–4 · "The pole comes down"**
- *Telegraph (end of round 3):* a crack like a mast breaking, and the Great Lamp at the top sways and *flares*. One of Mabry's Watchmen has sawn the pole's base.
- **TERRAIN change:** the pole falls across the square toward the Lamplighters' Bridge. DC 16 Dex save for anyone in its 60-ft line (5 ft wide): 4d10 bludgeoning, half on a success. A climber on it rides it down (Acrobatics DC 16 to jump clear to a roof; Ash's Second-Story Work gives advantage; failure is 6d6 falling). The Great Lamp shatters: bright light everywhere in a 60-ft radius for 1 round (no one can hide), then the square is dark except for festival lanterns.
- The fight spills onto the rooftops (the Knives go up after Ash) and onto the Lamplighters' Bridge.
- **Counter (Gideon's Protection style):** the Knives **ignore Gideon**. *Telegraph:* when the pole comes down, Bastian Harrow shouts "Not the big one!" They fight at range or on the roofs, where Gideon isn't. (Gideon's Abjure Foes still works; his aura still works; he just can't be the wall they run into.)

**PHASE 3 · trigger: round 5–6, or when Mabry is bloodied · "Into the river"**
- **OBJECTIVE change:** the Hushed Watchmen turn the crowd toward the river. People are going into the Fordwater off the Lamplighters' Bridge: children, an old man, **Clara and Hester** (they were on the bridge). Each round, 1d4 civilians go in. Pulling someone out is an action (Athletics DC 14 from the bridge, or a rope, or Moss as anything that swims). **Saving people beats finishing Mabry.** If Clara goes in and Ash pulls her out, that's a moment: give it room.
- **Mabry runs** for the Old Ford Bridge. Catching him: he has 30 ft speed and a head start; Ash catches him by roof (no roll needed if Ash chooses to chase), but then he's not saving people. That's the choice. Captain Hale's men arrive at the end of round 7 and cut Mabry off if nobody else did (so he isn't lost by choosing the river).

**FINAL BEAT.** The Knives dead, captured or fled; the Watchmen down (stirred or laid out); the crowd out of the river; Mabry captured (by the party or by Hale's men). **EVT-48** fires.
**Fail-forward.** If the party is losing: the crowd itself turns. Fringe folk who know Ash (the Lane is here for the festival: Peg Mallory with her pan, Brynja with her pickaxe, Ma Garrow's crew if she likes him, the Tolleys) pile into the Knives. The fight ends, ugly. Mabry escapes into Old Ford and is caught by Hale two days later, half-drowned at the Quiet's still bowl. EVT-48 still fires.
**Rewards.** See treasure. Mabry's papers (the Calm's order to "remove the second Fenwick quietly, festival night, by the Keeper's wish").
**DM notes.** Expected 6 rounds. If the player gets clever early (Panache on Bastian Harrow; a Silence spell on the Watchmen, who are thunder-vulnerable but deaf to orders in silence), reward it and shorten the fight.
