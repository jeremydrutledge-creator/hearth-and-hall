## ★ Must-happen beats

### ★1 · Before the night
The party knows what's coming. They try to stop it legally. Nobody believes them in time, or someone believes them too late. Gift-giving among friends (Enchanters' Row gifts land here).
- **A. Take the ledger to the Concord.** Hollis talks them round, warmly ("These poor boys. Their parents. Of course they want someone to blame. That's not my hand, Margery: look at it, it's a copy of a copy."). **Osric Vane** (R-090) believes them, for selfish reasons, and says so loudly, which makes everyone else less likely to. **Provost Teague** (R-091) half-believes: she doesn't trust "lamp people," but she won't move against the Concord's most respected voice on Lantern Night without more. She gives them one thing: "If you're right, I'll be at the Ford Stone at midnight to relight it. Come and tell me you were right."
- **B. Go to Captain Hale.** After EVT-48 (Mabry), Hale believes them. He has 300 men for a city of 65,000 on its biggest night. He'll give them the Watch warrant (if not already), Ostley's Fringe watch-house, and himself on the Islands.
- **C. Go to Hester.** She doesn't believe it. She's furious, and then, alone, she goes to look (SEC-10: this is the night she finally looks). She takes the Lamp House children with her as far as Old Ford, because they're going to the procession anyway.
- **D. (If they skip it all.)** It doesn't matter: the night comes. Hester goes to look anyway, because Clara told her something (if Clara knows), or because she found the listening-lamp's still bowl in her own house and understood what it was.

### ★2 · The Reserve is lit (SC#4)
At midnight, every lamp in Emberford starts pulling light down into the ground. The party (plus whoever they've won over) must break the chain of connected lamps district by district.
- **A.** Run as written from the party's position.
- **B.** If the party goes straight for the Reserve, **allies run the districts**, and each district's result is one roll by the ally the party trusted most there.
- **C.** If the party is elsewhere (Lamp House), the challenge is run from Old Ford outward, and the districts they can't reach count as **automatic failures** (which makes the 5-failure outcome likely, as the costly route intends).

### ★3 · The Grand Reserve under the Guildhall (Fight 21)
The party fights down into the Guildhall's deep cellars to the Reserve: a cavern of tuned glimmer humming one note. Zeke unweaves the tuning. The twins (if together) sing it apart. Hollis is there.
- **A. Fight down through the Guildhall** (SC 13-A, the front way).
- **B. Through the Hall of Two Rivers from below,** via the cracked floor in Old Ford (after Fight 20).
- **C. Ash opens a still-water route from the Lane Well** (SEC-22: the Lane Well draws from the Hall's springs; Ash hears it; Moss finally says so). They come up through the Hall into the Reserve's floor.

### ★4 · Hester dies (Loss #1) (EVT-21)
In Old Ford, the Hushed dead rise from the cracking Hall. Hester gets the Lamp House children out and holds the chapel door. The party learns of it, sees it, or arrives too late.
- **A. Ash hears it through the city's song:** a single held note, then silence.
- **B. A Lamp House child runs to find the party:** **Tansy** (NPC-078), eight, the one who hums Ash's tune.
- **C. The costly save route.** If the party (Ash personally) goes to Lamp House / Old Ford **instead of the Guildhall cellars**, they can save her. **The Reserve isn't dispersed** (no EVT-23), **Hollis escapes un-reached**, and **EVT-22 is SEAL +3** (not +1). (`campaign/00` §7.)

### ★5 · Hollis's end (EVT-24)
With Hester's death, Hollis breaks. The choice is Ash's: **kill him, capture him, or reach him.** Reaching him (SC 13-C) turns him into a broken witness who will testify (huge for Ch17 and the endings). Capture gives a hostile witness. Death removes the witness and gives Clara a wound that must be healed.
- **A. At the console in the Reserve chamber**, when Hester's lamp goes out (he feels it: R-044; he's kept her "in the light" all his life, and his own listening-lamp is in her house if Ash gave it back).
- **B. Over Hester's body.** He leaves the console and goes down through the broken floor into the Hall and out into Old Ford, to the chapel. The party finds him there, on his knees in the doorway, holding her apron.
- **C. At the still water.** He walks to the Reserve's still pool (or the Bend, or the Ford Pool, wherever is nearest) to give himself to the Stillness: "It promised. It promised no more grief." Stop him, or don't.
- **D. (If the costly route was taken.)** Hollis escapes un-reached. He flees the city through the Ford Pool or a cellar well, toward the "horned ones" his letters mentioned. (Ch14: he can be found, captive or adviser, in Mirrowen. That's Act III's business. Note it.)

## Scenes

### S13.1 · The last days of Lantern (★1)
Snow on the Lane. Every window has a lamp. The Row is mobbed. The procession is being rehearsed on Guildhall Isle. It's the most beautiful the city has ever looked. Say so. It should hurt.
- **The Concord** (★1 A). A side chamber in the Guildhall, the Provost, Osric, Hollis, Big Tam Hollin (NPC-035: he votes for the truth in Ch17; tonight he says "I'd believe a dwarf over a lamp-man any day" and is ignored), Dorcas Fell (NPC-034, Lamplighters: frightened; she's the one who can be turned: show her the Kessel manifests and the Reserve's tuning schedule, Persuasion DC 18, and she'll order her lamplighters to cut the chains in their districts at midnight: +advantage on two districts of the player's choice in SC#4).
- **Hollis, in person.** He is gracious. He touches Ash's shoulder. "My dear boy. I know what you think. I know what you've lost. Come to the Guildhall tonight. Stand with me at midnight. Then you'll understand." He believes it. He'd give Ash the peace he's giving everyone.
- **Captain Hale** (★1 B). He gives what he has (300 men spread over a city; himself and twenty on the Islands; Ostley and the Fringe watch-house on the Lane and Old Ford).
- **Hester** (★1 C, D). Play R-040 to its last breath. "My brother has spent his life on this city. How *dare* you." Then, after the door has shut, she stands in Lamp House's kitchen with the still bowl from the listening-lamp (or a Quiet chapel tea-bowl Hollis gave her) in her hands, and she looks into it, and for the first time she lets herself see. (The party doesn't see this. Tell it later, through Tansy.)
- **Gift-giving (Lantern Night custom).** Small enchanted trinkets are the customary gift. `campaign/05` §4: **Moss** gets a compass that points to one person, with a hair of Ash's (Keepsake & Locket, Uncommon 400 gp; Widow Marsh will make it overnight for Ash). **Clara** might give Ash something (if Twin Bond 3+: a lamp she made for him, that glows the grey-green of their eye-fleck). **The Lane** gives the Fenwicks a new door-knocker shaped like a stag (Pip's idea; nobody tells him why it's funny). Record every gift.
- **Brannoc's scouts (footprint).** Moss comes back from the Fordwood at dusk and says, quietly, to Ash alone: "There are horned ones at the Ford Pool. On the other side. Waiting. They want to see if tonight cracks it." (If SC#4 fails badly, see exit.)
- **Allies.** Before midnight, the party can visit allies to set up the districts (see SC#4's ally table). Each visit is a short scene.

### S13.2 · The lamp-lighting procession (side window)
At dusk, the Provost walks from Old Ford's Ford Stone to Guildhall Isle with a single lamp, lighting each district's master lamp on the way. Every lamp in the city comes on behind her in a wave. Ash hears it.
- **Ash's discovery moment: he hears every lamp in the city as one song.** It's beautiful, and it's wrong: every lamp is humming the *same* note, the Reserve's note, and the note is pulling *down*. He can feel the lamp-chain like a net with the Guildhall at its center. (This is how he knows, at midnight, which way the light goes.)

### S13.3 · Midnight (★2; SC#4)
The bells ring twelve. On the twelfth, every lamp in Emberford dims to blue, and then the light *runs*: down the lamp-posts, along the chains, into the gutters, into the ground, toward Guildhall Isle. The snow glows blue from underneath. People cheer: they think it's the display. Then the first lamp goes grey, and the man under it sits down in the snow and stops moving.
- Run **SC#4**. The party splits (each character takes a district; allies take others). That's how solo play makes sense here: narrate each character in turn.
- **Zeke's discovery moment (the Islands district): he unweaves the city's lamp-chain.** At the Great Lamp on Guildhall Isle (rebuilt since the Lamp-Climb), he can see the whole chain, every thread, and pull one. Spell Breaker and Counterspell are his tools; describe it as unpicking a knitted net.

### S13.4 · Old Ford (Fight 20; ★4)
The combat district. The Hall of Two Rivers lies under Old Ford, and the drain is cracking its ceiling. The Gate War dead who drowned with the old Hall's keepers rise through the cobbles, Hushed. See Fight 20.
- **Hester.** She got the Lamp House children as far as the old chapel (the same mossy chapel the Quiet rents: the Calm's own still bowl is inside). When the dead came up, she put the children through the chapel and out the back into the West Gate road, and **held the chapel door**. She sang while she did it: the Bright Lady's lamp-hymn, badly, loudly.
- **The held note.** Ash hears it (★4 A) through the city's song: Hester's hymn, one long held note, then **silence**.
- **Tansy** (★4 B) finds the party wherever they are: barefoot in the snow, shaking. "Aunt Hester said run and find the boy who hums. She said you'd know what to do. She's at the door. She said don't look back. I looked back."
- **The costly route (★4 C) and the choice.** See S13.6.

### S13.6 · THE CHOICE (Hester or the Reserve)
**When:** the first time Ash hears the held note, or Tansy reaches him, *before* he's committed to the Reserve chamber. Usually just after SC#4's midnight wave, or partway down SC 13-A.
**Say it plainly, as DM, out of character if needed:**
> "Here's where you are. If you go to Old Ford now, yourself, you can reach Hester in time. If you do, the Reserve won't be stopped: the city's seal cracks badly tonight (worse than it would otherwise), and Hollis gets away, un-reached. If you go down to the Reserve, you can stop it, and you can probably face Hollis, but you will very likely not reach Hester in time. Your friends can go to Old Ford without you, but they won't get there in time either. It's the song: it's *you* she can hear. What do you do?"
- **Don't make it a trick.** Don't hide the costs. Don't change them after he chooses. **Don't punish either choice.** Both are heroic. Both cost.
- **If Ash goes to Hester (costly save route):** he gets there as the chapel door is splintering. Fight 20's last phase is at the door (if not already fought). He holds the door with her, or carries her out. **Hester lives** (EVT-21 doesn't happen as a death: log "Hester saved, costly route"). **EVT-23 doesn't happen** (the Reserve isn't dispersed). **EVT-22 is SEAL +3.** **Hollis escapes un-reached** (★5 route D). Hester, alive, has seen the Reserve's truth on the chapel's still bowl (the Calm's own bowl): her blind spot is gone. R-040 breaks the way it would have in death, but she has to *live* with it. She sits on the chapel step in the snow and says, "He's my brother. He's my little brother." She's a witness against Hollis in Ch17, and a very angry one. (Loss #1 doesn't happen: `campaign/00` §7 allows it, and the endings are unchanged. Note for the Act III writer: loss level B still caps at two; don't replace her.)
- **If Ash goes down:** Hester dies at the chapel door, with the last child through. Fight 20's dead go still at the moment she does: they stop, and turn toward the chapel, and lower their weapons. The note she held is still in the air, somehow, for one more breath. **EVT-21.**

### S13.7 · The Reserve chamber (Fight 21; SC 13-B)
A cavern under the Guildhall that shouldn't fit under the Guildhall: two hundred feet across, the walls entirely tuned glimmer, faceted like the inside of a geode, all humming one note. In the center, a still pool. Around it, the **tuning frame**: a ring of glimmer forks taller than a man. At the console (a lectern of brass and crystal), **Hollis**, coat off, sleeves rolled, playing the frame like an organ. Above him, standing up out of the glimmer floor, the **Reserve's Warden**: a figure of tuned crystal the size of a house, waking as the party comes in.
- **Clara** is here if she's on outcome (c) or chose to stay "to understand": on the tuning platform, singing, eyes open, crying. If she's with the party, she's beside Ash.
- **The cracked floor (SEC-06 seed):** through cracks in the chamber's floor, far below, the party can see the **doors of the Hall of Two Rivers**: vast, stone, carved with **twin figures** at a ford, a stag between them. (Ash sees the twins. Clara sees them. They look at each other.)
- **SEC-07 seed (Hollis's papers):** on the console, his notebook, open: *"The horned ones across the water serve the same peace. The Regent will cross when the seal breaks. It will be a kindness to them too."* (Take it: it's Ch14's lever.)

## Skill challenges

### SC#4 · "Lantern Night" (★2, campaign skill challenge #4) · L13
**Structure (from `campaign/02`):** **12 successes before 5 failures, across 6 districts (2 successes each).** Each district is a short scene. The party splits: each character takes a district; allies take others. Narrate each in turn.
**Run each district as a 2-link chain** (two quick checks under that district's pressure). A district is **saved** at 2 successes. A failure in a district is a failure for the whole challenge, and that district **keeps draining** (SEAL creep, Hushed citizens, damage): narrate it. A district can take more than two attempts, but each attempt past the second costs a failure *if it fails*.
**DCs at L13:** standard 17, hard 19. Reliable Talent: Ash's expertise checks succeed. Put him where *his* skills fit, and everyone else where theirs do.
**Ally rolls (route B/C):** an ally running a district without a party member rolls **d20 + ally bonus** vs the district DC, once per link. Ally bonus: **+6** for an ally the party has *earned* (helped, saved, trusted), **+3** for a neutral ally, **+0** if no one is there.
