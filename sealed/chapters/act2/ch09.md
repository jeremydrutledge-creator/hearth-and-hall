## ★ Must-happen beats

### ★1 · Why Tollhaven
The party needs to know who funds the Calm and how big the Grand Reserve is.
- **A. The Kessel manifests** (Ch5) list Tollhaven buyers: "House M." on every crate.
- **B. Osric Vane hires the party** to prove the Enchanters are cornering the glimmer market. He wants the Merrow ledger (R-090: he believes Hollis is hiding a market corner behind charity; he's right for the wrong reasons). Pay: 500 gp now, 1,000 on delivery.
- **C. Driftmarket is a day's sail away.** Coble Tull (NPC-144) brings news that a Merrow agent is buying "every chip of glimmer on the lake" (and Lusk's book says the same).
- **D. Simon Shaw's debt** (always present, S9.0). Even if none of the others land, Simon's letter makes Tollhaven personal.

### ★2 · Society and the heist (SC 9-A)
A Purse Council masquerade, Dame Abernathy's clock collection, the House of Merrow's counting-house. **Panache** gets a showcase.
- **A. Infiltration through the masquerade.**
- **B. A cat-burglar run across the counting-house roofs.**
- **C. A legal angle:** buy into the Merrow futures market and audit it from inside (a long con).

### ★3 · The Merrow ledger
Proof the Calm has spent a fortune buying and moving glimmer to "the Guildhall Reserve," signed with Hollis's lamp-in-a-circle sigil, not his name.
- **A. Taken in the heist.**
- **B. Handed over by Dame Abernathy,** who wants the House of Merrow broken (a deal: the party does a job for her first, S9.3).
- **C. Copied by Zeke** from the Purse Council's archive using the astrolabe's cipher (the slow, clever route; S9.2).

### ★4 · Ash opens a gate (SC 9-B)
In the House of Varga's flooded vault, Ash hears its key note, hums it, and the gate opens on his own (no turn of light). They go through into Saffra for a few hours, then come back, or use it as the way out of a sealed vault.
- **A. As an escape route from the heist** (the vaults share a wall: the Merrow counting-house backs onto the Varga vault).
- **B. Ash chooses to try it,** after Moss tells him a Hearkener can. ("You held the Eye at the Drift. This is the same, only you open your hands instead of closing them.")
- **C. Coins are going missing** from the Varga vault ("they cross"). Master Otto Varga (NPC-143) hires the party to find the "thief," and the vault is where it happens.

## Scenes

### S9.0 · Opener: Simon's letter (personal hook)
A message-moth finds Ash on the Drift (or the road): a scrap of brown chandler's paper in Simon Shaw's tiny careful hand.
> *Ash. Don't come home on my account. The House of Merrow bought my debt off the Commons moneylender. They want it all by Leaffall or they take the shop. 212 gold. I'm doing sums. Put it on the slate, as they say. — S.S.*
- Play R-030: Simon won't ask. He never asks. That's the point.
- This gives Ash a reason to hit the House of Merrow that has nothing to do with gates. The debt deed is in the Merrow counting-house (SC 9-A, link "the strongroom": a bonus objective). Taking or burning it clears Simon's debt (**EVT-15** touches R-030: "if Simon's Merrow debt is cleared").

### S9.1 · Arrival: the City of Purses
Bright-painted houses showing off. Everyone haggling. Contracts are holy; a handshake is worth nothing; a wax seal is worth everything.
- **The Weighing Docks:** every barge pays toll by weight. The party's barge is weighed. (If they have Dughal's horn-caps, they owe 4 gp for them.)
- **The Toll Guard:** polite, well paid. They'll arrest you for a broken contract faster than a broken nose.
- **The rival:** **Percival Hume** (NPC-142), a smooth young broker in a plum coat, keeps outbidding the party for odd jobs and turns up everywhere. Harmless, smug, eventually useful (if Ash beats him at something, he becomes a source: he knows every Purse house's gossip and will sell it).
- **The Grand Wager** is on in Lamplight Row this week (side window).

### S9.2 · Dame Constance Abernathy and the astrolabe
The First Purse (NPC-063) is sharp, cheerful and ruthless. She collects clocks: two hundred of them, all ticking in her Gull Hill villa, all set three minutes apart "so I'm never surprised."
- **How the party gets in:** a courier job (she's always hiring), Osric Vane's introduction, or Ash's Panache at the masquerade.
- **The astrolabe (Zeke's discovery moment).** In a glass case: a Sundering-era astrolabe of black bronze, its rings marked with tiny still-water symbols. Zeke can read it (Arcana DC 16 or History DC 16; Sundering-script reading makes it automatic after a few minutes' study). He learns:
  1. It marks the turns of light at **every** Wellgate in Eyrland. Its rings show the gates **line up on Midsummer's Eve**. ("Every gate, the same minute. Once a year. Ash, that's your *birthday*.")
  2. One ring points into the Sundersea at a spot marked with a keyhole glyph: **"the keyhole"** (SEC-12 seed).
- **The cipher (★3 route C).** The astrolabe's outer ring is a cipher wheel, and the Purse Council's archive uses an old version of it for sealed filings. With a day in the archive (access through Abernathy or a forged reader's pass, Forgery Kit or Deception DC 17), Zeke can decipher the Merrow filings and copy the ledger's key pages (Investigation DC 17 ×2 over two days). Slower, legal-ish, and it doesn't alert the Merrows.
- **Abernathy's deal (★3 route B).** She wants the House of Merrow broken ("They're buying something they can't sell, which means they're buying for someone. I don't like people I can't see."). She'll hand over a copy of the ledger she already has (her spies took it a month ago) if the party does one job first: steal the House of Merrow's *sealed bids* for next year's Weighing Dock contract from the counting-house (the same heist, different prize). Ruthless and practical. If they deliver, she pays 800 gp too.
- **She's an older woman ally type** (sharp, mentorly, never romantic). She likes Ash if he's honest about being a thief. "Finally. Someone in this city who admits it."

### S9.3 · The House of Merrow
A narrow, tall counting-house of green marble in the Counting Quarter, backing onto the House of Varga's older, damper building.
- **Lady Honoria Merrow** (NPC-140), head of the house: silver-haired, still-voiced, faintly grey around the eyes. Calm (she attends a "tea circle" by still water). She never raises her voice. She says "the Keeper" once, by accident, and corrects it to "our keeper of accounts."
- **Glimmer futures:** she's been buying every chip on the lake for two years at any price. She ships it upriver in sealed crates to "the Guildhall Reserve."
- **The Merrow letter (SEC-03 seed 6)** sits in her correspondence: *"…the Keeper's daughter, whose voice tunes the Reserve, will need the last lake shipment by Frostcome…"* Ash or Zeke can find it in the heist (any link that reaches the study) or in the archive (route C). Nobody should understand it yet.

### S9.4 · The Purse Council masquerade (★2 route A; Panache showcase)
At the Purse Hall: masks, card tables, a string quartet, a bronze coin-statue with a fountain in its mouth.
- **Panache in society.** Ash's Panache (L9 house rule) charms a *non-hostile* creature for a minute on a won contest (Persuasion vs Insight). At the masquerade it's the key that opens doors: a guard at the stair (Insight +2), a Merrow clerk who knows the strongroom hours (Insight +3), Percival Hume (Insight +4), even Lady Merrow herself (Insight +6). Each success is a link in SC 9-A.
- **The card tables** (side window): Kings and Ferrymen with Purse heirs. Good place to learn which Merrow clerk drinks.
- **Valentine Crewe** (NPC-141), the Calm-hired duelist, is here in a white fox mask. He's charming, bored and dangerous. He's been told to watch "a Fringe boy with a grey fleck in his eye." If Ash talks to him, Crewe is delightful. "I'm a duelist. It's a very boring job until it isn't." (He becomes the leader of Fight 11.)
- **Hostile Panache:** in Fight 11, Panache on Crewe (hostile) makes him duel Ash alone. That's the showcase. Let it shine.

### S9.6 · The vault (★4; Fight 12; SC 9-B)
Routes A (escape from the heist through the shared wall), B (on purpose) or C (the Varga job). See Fight 12.

When it opens, describe it big: the coin-silt on the vault floor lifts in a slow spiral, the water stands up into a ring, and through it Ash sees a striped awning and hears bells.

### S9.7 · A few hours in Saffra
They come up through a fountain basin in a tabaxi story-hall courtyard in **Saffra**, Mirrowen's neutral trade city: bright awnings, bells on every doorway, the smell of spice and river.
- **Saffi-of-the-Lamplight** (NPC-061), the Tale-Keeper, is there within ten minutes (she hears everything). She's thrilled. "The boy who hears! The one from the stories! Sit. Talk. I collect you." She trades: a story from Ash (any true one about the rescue) for news.
  - **What she tells:** Brannoc's raids are hitting Eyrland's minor gates; the Regent's own brother, Fergan, "asks too many questions in the war-tent"; Tuskhold still holds; refugees pass through Saffra every night and the city pretends not to see. **Saffra trades with the Regent to survive** and hates itself a little for it.
  - **Seed for Ch14:** she can arrange truces. "If you ever need to talk to a horned one without being gored, come to me." (She'll remember Ash's story word for word at the Great Telling.)
- **Getting home:** Ash can open the gate again from this side (SC 9-B's last link only, at advantage; he knows the note now), or wait for the turn of light. If the Toll Guard are waiting at the Varga vault when they return, see "off-route."
- **Lark (if present)** sees beastfolk markets for the first time in months and cries in the good way.

They don't name Hollis. They do prove Mabry is Calm (SEC-21) and that the Calm ordered the siege. On the fall branch, nothing arrives, and Netta's next gossip moth says "Rook's eyes have gone funny."

## Encounters

### FIGHT 11 · Chandeliers and Balconies · Ch9 · Ordinary (upper band) · Party level 9

**Target numbers (L9):** total HP **255** · enemy DPR **44** · AC 15–17 · attack +6–+8 · DC 14–15.
**HP math:** Valentine Crewe 95 + 4 Merrow House Guards × 40 = 160 → **255**. Party output 91 × 3.5 ≈ 318; ~80% efficiency (balconies, chandeliers, Panache taking Crewe out of the general fight) ≈ 255. **Expected 3–4 rounds.**
**DPR math (vs AC ~17):** Crewe 3 × 9.5 × 0.6 = 17.1; Guards 4 × (9.5 × 0.6 = 5.7) = 22.8 → ~40, plus chandelier/hazard use ~4 → **~44**.

**Battlefield: the Merrow counting-house's great hall.** Green marble, two balconies (15 ft up) on opposite walls, a staircase, three crystal chandeliers on chains, long counting tables, and the iron door to the strongroom.
1. **Chandeliers.** A creature can cut a chandelier's chain (AC 15, 10 HP, or Sleight of Hand DC 14 on the winch) to drop it: DC 15 Dex save for each creature in a 10-ft square below, 4d6 bludgeoning, prone on a failure. Or swing on one from balcony to balcony (Acrobatics DC 14; Ash with Second-Story Work: DC 10).
2. **Balconies.** Half cover from the floor. Guards with crossbows like them.
3. **The counting tables.** Overturn one for half cover (action); run across them (difficult terrain).
4. **The strongroom door.** Iron, warded. Thieves' Tools DC 18 (Fast Hands lets Ash try as a bonus action). Inside: the ledger, Simon's deed, the sealed bids, 1,200 gp of bearer notes.
5. **The shared wall.** The back of the strongroom is the Varga vault's old wall. A Perception DC 15 hears water behind it. (★4 route A.)

**Opening.** Depends on SC 9-A: at 0 Pressure there's no Fight 11 inside; Crewe meets the party *outside* as they leave and it's only a duel (Crewe alone; see "if they're clean," below). At 1–2 Pressure, the guards are alert; at 3, the guards and Crewe are waiting in the hall.

**Enemy plan.** The guards hold the strongroom door and the stairs. Crewe goes for Ash, because he was told to watch "the boy with the fleck." He wants a *duel*, and he'll take one if offered.
- **Counter (Ash's Sneak Attack from flanking):** Crewe is **Watchful** and the guards fight back-to-back. *Telegraph:* as soon as Ash moves to flank, Crewe smiles and steps so his back is to a marble pillar: "I know what you are, little thief. Rogues love company." Once per round, an ally within 5 ft of Crewe doesn't count for Sneak Attack against him. Steady Aim and Bart's advantage still work. Panache works best of all (it pulls him into a one-on-one).
- **Panache showcase.** If Ash uses Panache on Crewe (hostile) and wins, Crewe accepts: "Oh, *finally*." For the minute, he fights only Ash (disadvantage against anyone else) and the guards hang back, watching the duel, unless someone else attacks Crewe (which ends Panache). Describe the duel like a stage fight: across the tables, up the stairs, onto the chandelier.
- **Twist (round 3):** the strongroom's alarm-chime rings (a glimmer bell: Lady Merrow is warned) and the guards start lowering the iron grate on the hall doors. Getting out after round 5 means lifting it (Athletics DC 18, or two people at DC 15), or going **through the back wall** into the Varga vault (★4 route A: the wall's mortar is rotten from damp; three successful attacks or a Shatter or Thunderwave opens it).

**If they're clean (0 Pressure).** Crewe alone in the moonlit street, rapier out: "You stole something. I'm paid to mind. Let's be civilized." A duel. If Ash wins without killing him, Crewe bows and leaves: "Tell your grey-eyed friends I said you were lovely." He's done with the Calm (he didn't like them). He can turn up again in Act III as a sword for hire on the party's side, if the player likes him.

**Surrender.** Guards surrender at half strength. Crewe yields when below 30 HP ("I'm expensive, not stupid").

### FIGHT 12 · The Varga Vault · Ch9 · Significant · Party level 9

**Target numbers (L9):** total HP **410** · enemy DPR **52** · AC 15–17 · attack +6–+8 · DC 14–15.
**HP math:** The Varga Crab 190 + Lamp-Warden Spellward 75 + 3 Lamp-Wardens × 48 = 144 → **409**. (If Lark came along, add one Stillwrack, 35 HP: the Heron finds her more easily away from the Drift.) Party output 91 × 6 ≈ 546; ~75% efficiency (the flood, swimming, the gate objective in phase 3) ≈ 410. **Expected 6 rounds.**
**DPR math (vs AC ~17):** Crab 2 × 14 × 0.6 = 16.8 + grapple crush ~3; Spellward 15 × 0.6 = 9 + flare ~3; Wardens 3 × (2 × 8.5 × 0.6 = 10.2) = 30.6 → **~52–60** in phase 1 when all are fresh; settles near 50.

**Why lamp-wardens are here.** The Merrow strongroom alarm rang to the Guild's lamp-warden post in Tollhaven (they guard the Merrow glimmer shipments). They come in through the Varga building's front, chasing the party into the vault. On route C (the Varga job) they arrive because the Merrows tipped them that "Fringe thieves" are in the Counting Quarter.

**Battlefield: the House of Varga's flooded vault.** An old stone vault, 60 × 40 ft, two levels: a raised walkway around the walls (10 ft up) and the sunken floor, already knee-deep in black still water. Coins glint under the water everywhere: the coins that "go missing" and come back.
1. **The vault water.** The Wellgate. It's still: greywilt rules apply from phase 2 (see below).
2. **The walkway.** Iron rails, slick with damp. Ladders at each corner.
3. **The coin-mounds.** Heaps of silted coin under the water: difficult terrain, and anyone can scoop a fistful (it's gold; it's also Varga's).
4. **The old dwarven mark** (SEC-11 seed for Ch10 ★1 route C): carved over the vault's original arch, half under water: a dwarven rune-band reading "Oath of the Quiet Stone. Do not dig." (Zeke reads it: Dwarvish or Arcana DC 12.) The Varga's ancestors built over a dwarven warning.
5. **The sluice gate.** An iron gate in the floor-drain that the Vargas use to pump the vault. Opening it (Athletics DC 16, action) drains 1 ft of water a round. In phase 2 it's jammed by the rising gate-water (DC 20).

**Opening.** The party comes in (through the wall, the stairs, or the Varga's front door). The water is calm. Then the coin-mound in the middle stands up. It's a giant crab, Hushed, its shell crusted with three centuries of coin.

**Enemy plan.** The crab guards the gate (it's the gate's keeper now; the Stillness put it there). It grabs whoever goes near the water's center. The lamp-wardens come down the stairs in a line and fight from the walkway, the Spellward at the back with the ward-lantern.

**PHASE 1 · rounds 1–3 · "The keeper of the coin"**
- The crab grabs and crushes. The wardens hold the walkway.
- **Counter (Zeke's spells):** the Spellward's **ward-lantern** absorbs one spell. *Telegraph (round 1):* the Spellward unhoods a lantern whose light bends toward Zeke like a plant to the sun. The first spell of 5th level or lower that Zeke casts targeting a creature or area within 30 ft of the lantern is drawn into it and fails (the slot is spent). After that the lantern is spent (cracked, dark). Smart answer: target the lantern (AC 15, 15 HP), or bait it with a cantrip-level... no, it only takes a leveled spell; bait it with a 1st-level one. Wall of Force or Animate Objects cast *outside* the 30 ft still work.

**PHASE 2 · trigger: start of round 4, or when the crab is below half · "The vault floods"**
- *Telegraph (end of round 3):* the coins under the water all turn face-up at once. The water stops making any sound. Then it rises.
- **TERRAIN change:** the water rises 5 ft at the start of each round (round 4: waist-deep on the floor; round 5: the floor is fully underwater; round 6: the walkway is ankle-deep). Underwater: melee attacks with non-piercing weapons have disadvantage, ranged attacks beyond normal range miss, creatures have resistance to fire, and **Fireball and similar fire areas are snuffed underwater** (not a counter; it's physics, and it was telegraphed by the water). The water is **still water**: a creature that starts its turn submerged makes a DC 15 Wis save or takes 2d8 psychic (Gideon's Aura of Warding resists, and his story moment from Ch8 means creatures in his aura ignore this).
- The crab is happiest underwater (it gains the *Submerged* trait below).

**PHASE 3 · trigger: round 6, or when Ash goes near the water's center · "The key note"**
- **OBJECTIVE change:** the vault is filling. The way out is the stairs (crowded by wardens) or **the gate**. Ash hears the key note under the silence (Perception DC 15; Ash auto). Run **SC 9-B** (below). While Ash works, the others hold the crab and wardens off him.
- **Counter (Gideon's Initiative Swap):** two more lamp-wardens arrive on **initiative count 10** from the stairs (reinforcements). *Telegraph (round 5):* boots and lantern-light on the stair above, and a shout: "Second squad!" (Each has 48 HP; if the fight is already long, skip them.)

**FINAL BEAT.** The gate opens (EVT-16): the water stands up into a ring and the party can go through to Saffra. The crab, if still standing, sinks back into the coin and goes still (the gate is open; it's no longer "keeping" anything). The wardens won't follow into another world; they've never heard of one, and they're terrified. Or the party beats everyone and opens the gate at leisure.
