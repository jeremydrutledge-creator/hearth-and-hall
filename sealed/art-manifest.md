# Art Manifest (SEALED — DM ONLY)

> One consolidated, priority-ordered list of every image the campaign needs. **Apply the style line from `/art/style-bible.md` to every prompt.** Generate a reference portrait first for anyone drawn more than once, then build later images from that reference.
>
> **[P]** = player-safe now (its file name and picture spoil nothing on day one); it lives under `/art/` or `/player/maps/`. Everything else lives under `/sealed/` until the party meets the person or reaches the place, then the DM moves it (or shows it). Player-version maps of places not yet reached wait in `/sealed/maps/player-versions/`.
>
> **Tiers:** **T1** needed before session one · **T2** Act I (Ch2–6) · **T3** later (make as play approaches; don't pay up front).
>
> **Maps:** paint with no text labels; keys live in the text files. Keep features spaced for cropping. Player and DM versions share one composition so they line up.
>
> **Counts:** 270 images · T1 45 · T2 55 · T3 170 · player-safe [P] 63.

### [P] Eyrland and the Tamber River (player)
`/player/maps/eyrland-region.jpg`

A painted bird's-eye map of Eyrland, a great river basin like a tilted bowl: grey mountains with steaming springs on the north rim, a dark old forest in the west, rolling hills and sheep country to the east, and the sea along the bottom. The Tamber River runs north to south through the middle, widening into a huge mirror-smooth lake two-thirds of the way up (the Glasswater), with a reed marsh south of the lake, then down past a walled capital to a port city at the river's mouth. Emberford sits on islands where the river splits. Small cold lake in the foothills; a tributary out of the western forest; another out of the eastern hills. A dead-calm patch of sea far to the south. Only what a local would know. Paint as a classic illustrated map with no text labels at all (labels live in the text files). Keep key features well spaced so the DM can crop sections.
