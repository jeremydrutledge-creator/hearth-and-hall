# 03 · Secrets and Seeds (SEALED — DM ONLY)

> Every secret has **at least two seeds before its reveal**, in fair-in-hindsight places. "Dig early" says what happens if the party goes looking before the planned reveal. The rule is **never stonewall a good roll**: give a true partial answer that opens a new question.
>
> SEC-01 to SEC-16 come from world file 00 §9. SEC-17 to SEC-22 are new here. The Truth File step must carry these IDs.

## SEC-01 · Moss is Mossoran, the Moss-Crowned Hart
- **Who knows:** Moss. Mother Burdock and Granny Wenna Tull suspect from old songs. Queen Amarys knows. Aggie Thorne knows he doesn't age (SEC-20) but not what he is. The oldest Willow Lane neighbors (Walt and Dora Finch) remember "an herbalist called Moss" from their own childhood.
- **Seeds (before Ch6):**
  1. Ch1: Moss knows Thistle's name and home ("Thistle of the Mere").
  2. Ch1/2: Dora Finch: "He looked just the same when I was a girl. Some folk keep." She laughs it off.
  3. Ch2: Moss never says "I don't know" about Mirrowen. He says "I'd rather not say."
  4. Ch3: moss blooms with tiny white flowers where Moss stands at the Ford Pool. He speaks to the wolves' bodies in a language like wind in leaves.
  5. Ch3: the stone reads "the Ford-Watcher and the Hart sang the world in two." Moss goes white.
  6. Any wild shape: his beast forms are always antlered or mossy (elk, stag, moss-backed bear, horned owl).
  7. Ch6: Mother Burdock kneels: "Old Mossback."
- **Reveal:** Ch6, ★6 (true form at the Glass Mere shore). Full story told in Ch7 ★2.
- **Dig early:** if Ash corners Moss before Ch6 with strong evidence (two or more seeds plus a good Insight or Persuasion), Moss admits he's "older than he looks" and "made a promise to your family a long time ago," and asks for trust until "the other side." He does not lie, but he won't say "Hart." If Ash pushes past that, Moss walks out to the Fordwood and doesn't come back until dawn (a real rift scene).

## SEC-02 · Ash is the Eyrland Hearkener; the call is Lark
- **Who knows:** Moss. Thistle guesses at once (Ch1). Mother Burdock. The Heron (from Ch6).
- **Seeds:** the tune he's always hummed (opener, Ch1); the Ford Stone hums for him at the turn of light (Ch2); "your mother could have heard it" (Ch2); Thistle's "You're him" (Ch1); the pool listens to his hum (Ch3).
- **Reveal:** in layers. Ch2 (it's a call from Mirrowen), Ch3 (Ash affects gates), Ch6 (Lark is real and the bond is mutual), Ch7 (Moss explains the Hearkener line).
- **Dig early:** Zeke can find "Fenn-wyck, ford-watchers" in the Lyceum's old charters (DC 20 Investigation), which explains the family name but not the gift.

- **Seeds (before Ch11):**
  1. Ch2: every lamp on the Row brightens when they stand close. Shopkeepers joke.
  2. Ch2: Clara has the same grey-green fleck in her left eye.
  3. Ch2/7: Clara hums the lullaby's first line.
  4. Ch5 or 7: Clara's birthday is Midsummer's Eve ("Same as yours? How funny!").
  5. Ch7: Hollis's gift lamp was made by Clara. It flares in the Fenwick house.
  6. Ch9: the Merrow letter: "the Keeper's daughter, whose voice tunes the Reserve."
  7. Ch10: Torvald: "The Ford-watchers came in pairs, sometimes."
  8. Any time: Hollis says she came to him "as a little one, the autumn of the bad fires."
- **Reveal:** Ch11 ★2 (four routes).
- **Dig early:** Lamp House admission ledger (Ch5+; Investigation DC 18 or charm Hester's clerk) shows "Leaffall YF 1085, infant girl, about two, brought by Master Grane." That's the whole secret. If Ash finds it before Ch11, **move the reveal up** and let Ch11 become "telling Clara." Don't block it.

## SEC-04 · Hollis Grane ordered the Fenwick killing and leads the Calm
- **Who knows:** Hollis; Lucan Hask (SEC-17); the Calm's inner circle (Orla Venn, Giles Mabry, Professor Cyril Doone). Nobody else.
- **Seeds:**
  1. Ch2: a lamp-warden notices the lamps flaring around Ash and reports it.
  2. Ch5: Kessel crates bear Hollis's private sigil, a lamp in a circle. The same sigil hangs in the Quiet's chapel in Old Ford.
  3. Ch7: Hollis's gift-lamp card, in his hand, phrased like the Calm's teachings.
  4. Ch9: the Merrow ledger is signed with the sigil, for "the Keeper."
  5. Ch10: Ulric Fane's contract was signed by Lucan Hask, lamp-warden captain.
  6. Ch11: Hollis's offer letter: "every hand a light, every grief an end."
- **Reveal:** Ch12 ★3 (the fire-night ledger).
- **Dig early:** the Quiet's chapel (Ch5+) can be infiltrated. Professor Doone or Giles Mabry can be caught and interrogated. Either gives "the Keeper of the Still Lamp leads us" and "the Keeper is a great man of the Guild," but not the name. Hollis is careful. If the party *names* him before Ch12 with evidence, he goes on the offensive early: Ch11's assassination attempt moves up and Hester's vouch becomes a confrontation ("How dare you, after everything he's done for your family?"). The fire ledger is still needed for proof.

## SEC-10 · Hester Grane's blind spot
- **Who knows:** Hester, deep down.
- **What it is:** she knows Hollis has secret evening meetings, that some of the Lamp House children he "placed" were never heard from again (they went to the Calm's quiet houses: alive, Hushed), and that he weeps at still water. She chooses not to look, because looking would end the only family she has.
- **Seeds:** Ch5 (vouch #1, plus she mentions "Hollis's evening circle, for the grieving, he's so good to them"); Ch7 (vouch #2); Ch11 (she notices the matching eye flecks and changes the subject); Ch11 ("He's tired. He meets with his circle most nights. I don't ask.").
- **Reveal:** Ch13: she looks, and dies.
- **Dig early:** if the party confronts Hester with proof before Ch13, she's devastated, refuses to believe it, and goes to ask Hollis. He lies beautifully. She comes back to the party, believing him *more*. (This is the vouch twist's teeth. Only the Lantern Night reality breaks it.)

## The vouch twists (built to the player's favorite model)

> *A trusted ally vouches sincerely for someone because she has a real blind spot.*

| Vouch | Who vouches | For whom | Why sincere | Blind spot | Chapter | Ledger edge needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **#1** | Hester Grane | Hollis | She's loved him all her life; he's the kindest man she knows | SEC-10 | Ch5 | Hester → Hollis |
| **#2** | Hester Grane | Hollis (the gift lamp) | She thinks the lamp is pure kindness | SEC-10 | Ch7 | Hester → Hollis |

**Fairness check (hindsight):** after Ch12–13, the player should be able to look back and see that every vouch for Hollis was sincere, every seed pointed at him, and he was never written as a cartoon. Hester's death is the cost of her blind spot, not a punishment for it.
