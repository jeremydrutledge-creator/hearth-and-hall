# 04 · Endings (SEALED — DM ONLY)

> Three endings. Ash's choices across the whole campaign decide which are possible, and in the finale he chooses among the ones that are. Track three hidden values in the Sealed GM Notes and log every change with the chapter and the choice.

---

## The three tracks

### 1. Twin Bond (0–6) · Ash and Clara
- Starts at **0**. Ending 1 needs **3+**. Ending 2 needs **5+**. At **0–1** by Ch17, Ending 3 is forced.
- **+1** each:
  - Ch2/7: Ash is kind, curious or generous with Clara before he knows (buys from her, defends her from a Row bully, is honest with her).
  - Ch11: Ash tells her the truth himself, gently (or lets her come to it, and is there when she does).
  - Ch11: Ash does *not* try to force her to leave Hollis.
  - Ch12: the duet (if she's there).
  - Ch13: Ash spares Hollis (captured or reached), knowing what he means to her. Or, if Hollis dies, Ash grieves *with* her, not at her.
  - Ch13–17: Ash includes Clara in family (Restday supper, the brothers, the Lane, a gift from the Row).
- **−1** each:
  - Ash uses her (manipulates her into helping without telling her).
  - Ash kills Hollis in front of her when another way was open.
  - Ash makes her choose between him and Hollis before she's ready (Ch11–12).
  - Ash keeps her out of decisions about her own gift.
- **Clara lost:** if Twin Bond is 0 at the end of Ch13, Clara leaves Emberford (or, if the Heron is still active, Vessimer takes her in Ch15–18). She can return in Ch20 only if Ash goes after her. That's a big optional side chapter.

### 2. Mercy (0–6) · how Ash treats enemies who can be saved
- Starts at **0**. Ending 2 needs **4+**, plus at least one redemption (Brannoc *or* Vessimer).
- **+1** each:
  - Ch3: wake Borra (the Hushed bear-folk conscript) instead of killing him.
  - Ch5/12: Hushed miners spared and woken.
  - Ch7/11: Rook given a real chance (see 05).
  - Ch13: Hollis captured or reached rather than killed.
  - Ch14–15: Brannoc parleyed with and turned (a redemption).
  - Ch15: the Pens woken.
  - Ch18: Vessimer redeemed (a redemption).
- **−1** each:
  - Killing a surrendering enemy.
  - Killing Brannoc when the parley was available.
  - Destroying the Heron when redemption was on the table and Moss asked.
- *Never moralize at the table.* Players see consequences, not a score.

### 3. The Glimmer Choice (yes/no) · will Ash push Emberford to give up its lamps?
- **Yes** if: the Concord votes to end glimmer mining (Ch17) **and** Ash personally argued for it, knowing it ends Emberford's lamp-age (the city goes dark-ish; the Row's trinkets die).
- This is a choice the Lane will feel. Peg Mallory's self-stirring pots, the heat-stones in Bess Carter's kitchen, Zeke's beloved Row. Make it cost.
- **Required for Ending 2.** It's preferred for Ending 1 (without it, Ending 1's seal starts thinning again within a lifetime; say so in the epilogue).

## Ending 1 · Re-sing the Seal ("The Long Quiet")
**Requires:** Twin Bond 3+. (Possible with lower Mercy.)

**What happens:** Ash and Clara (with Lark) sing the Sundering whole again at the Drowned Gate. The Stillness sinks back into its prison and sleeps another thousand years. Every glimmer crystal in Eyrland goes dark at the same moment. Mirrowen and Eyrland stay apart, but the twins can keep a few Wellgates open as friendly doors (the Ford Pool, always).

**Costs:**
- Emberford's lamp-age ends. The city of lamps learns to burn oil again. The Row's trinkets die (the Lane's gifts with them, except true magic items).
- Brannoc (if alive and turned) goes home a hero. If not, Mirrowen rebuilds under the Free Clans.
- Moss survives, restored to most of his strength, and keeps watching the line. "Another thousand years, lad. I've done it before."

**Epilogue beats:** Restday supper, now with Clara at the table. The Lane in candlelight. Lark visiting through the Ford Pool. A lamp floated on the river on Midsummer's Eve: four now, "Mum, Dad, and one each for luck."

## Ending 2 · End the Sundering ("The Rain")
**Requires:** Twin Bond 5+, Mercy 4+, at least one redemption (Brannoc or Vessimer), the Glimmer Choice made, Thalassa-Ommu woken, Umbari's song received.

**What happens:** Ash, Clara and Lark sing the *other* song, the one the first Hearkener couldn't sing alone: not a cage, an unmaking. Ommu rises. The redeemed Heron (or the turned Regent's host holding the Gap) stands with them. The Stillness doesn't sleep. It *moves*: it rains, and is gone. Over a generation, the realms grow back together. Wellgates become rivers. Beastfolk and humans meet again as the old songs said.

**Costs:**
- **Moss.** Mossoran spends himself to anchor the joining. He becomes the land itself at the old Ford: a great tree with antler-shaped branches growing out of the Ford Stone in Old Ford. He can still be heard in the leaves. (This is heartbreaking and earned. Give the scene full weight, and let Moss choose it before the song, in a quiet talk with Ash.)
- The lamp-age ends too, but nobody misses it as much as they thought, because the world is waking up.

**Epilogue beats:** the Ford Stone tree. Lark and Ash on the Fenwick roof looking at a sky that has two moons now. Gideon swearing the first joint oath of the Wardens. Zeke teaching Sundering-script at the Lyceum. Clara singing at the Ford Festival.

**Note:** this is the hardest ending and the best. The player should be able to see the door to it without being told it exists. Umbari, the Hall carvings and the Heron's redemption all point to it.

## Ending 3 · The Warden's Seal ("The Stag at the Gate")
**Triggers:** Twin Bond 0–2 by Ch20 (Clara lost, turned, or estranged), **or** the finale goes badly (SEAL 10 early, or the Hearkeners are downed in Phase 4), **or** Ash chooses it to spare someone else.

**What happens:** Mossoran becomes the seal himself. A living stag-shaped standing stone at the Drowned Gate holds the Stillness in its prison forever. The realms stay split. Glimmer dims but doesn't die (the seal is now Moss, not the song).

**Costs:**
- Ash loses his oldest friend, and the herbalist who carried him out of the fire.
- If Clara was lost, she's not at the table. Ash can still find her afterward (a bittersweet epilogue option).

**Epilogue beats:** the Green Jar, now run by Aggie Thorne. Ash leaving a sack of flour on the step every Restday. The stone stag at the edge of the Glass, which sailors say turns its head to watch the ships go by.

## If the player tries for an ending they can't reach
Never block the attempt without a reason in the world. If Ash tries the unmaking song without a redemption, the song fails partway: the Stillness is wounded but not unmade, and the Hearkeners can switch to re-sealing (Ending 1) or Moss steps in (Ending 3). Narrate what was missing ("You needed one of the old Wardens, or the Regent's host, holding the Gap"), so the player understands, in hindsight, why.
