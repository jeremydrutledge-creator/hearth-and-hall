## Folk memories (seeds hidden in plain sight)

- **The Ford Festival pageant** shows "the Lady of the Ford" crossing the river with a lamp, "her stag" beside her, "chasing away the Grey." Kids play the Grey with sheets.
- **A Fringe nursery rhyme:** *"Grey man, grey man, still as stone, / Leave the ford-watch child alone. / Mossy horns and lamp of gold, / Keep the river from the cold."* Everyone knows it. Nobody thinks about it.
- **"Don't wish on still water."** A Midriver and sailors' saying.
- **Lamps "jump"** when a child is born at sunset: an old wives' tale.
- **Old Varrow's bells** "ring for the Bear" in fog.
