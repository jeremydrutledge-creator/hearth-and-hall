# 05 · Mirrowen, the Other Realm (SEALED — DM ONLY)

> Mirrowen (MEER-oh-wen; aka Mirowen, Mirrowin, Mirro-when). The smaller realm, a land of talking-beast clans, ancient trees, standing stones that hum and songs that still work. Classic Narnia-like wonder: earthy, honest, bright. **Not fae.** No courts of trickery. Beastfolk are people with clans, farms, laws, grudges and good food.

## Shape and feel

- About the size of one Eyrland province. A **reflected land**: its rivers, lake and mountains echo Eyrland's in miniature. Its great river, **the Rennet** (aka Renet), mirrors the Tamber and flows *north*, from a tiny sea in the south up to a mountain lake. (The reflection runs backward. Seed: a map of Mirrowen held up to a mirror looks like a map of Eyrland.)
- Colors are a touch richer, nights darker, stars bigger. Sound carries. Old trees occasionally speak, slowly, and only to beastfolk elders. (Not fae, just old.)
- Magic in Mirrowen runs on **song** rather than glimmer. Beastfolk singers ("chanters") can mend wood, calm storms and wake standing stones. As greywilt spreads, songs are fading.

## Mirrowen's politics at campaign start

- **Brannoc Ironhorn, the Horned Regent**, controls the Horngard, Hollowmere and the Glass Mere, and besieges Tuskhold. He calls himself Regent "until the blight is cured."
- **Saffra** is neutral and trading with everyone.
- **The Free Clans** (FAC-08): otter-folk, fox-folk, the hidden Warren Mothers, bear-folk, Tuskhold's defenders. A loose, desperate resistance led in spirit by Grandmother Umbari and in the field by a fox-folk captain, **Russet Vane** → *rename to avoid repeating Vane*: **Russet Tamsworth** (aka Rusty). He's clever, tired, and doesn't trust humans.
- Refugees cross into Eyrland through thinning gates, especially at Emberford, Driftmarket and Bramblecog.

## Greywilt and the Hushed

- **Greywilt:** spreading grey on leaves, still water, songs going quiet. In living things it starts as calm and ends as grey glassy stillness. Hushed creatures are not dead. They're stopped: obedient, painless, unchanging.
- **Hushed** stat-block tag (for encounter step): immune to charm and fear, resistant to psychic, vulnerable to thunder (sound and song break stillness), and slow to react (–2 initiative). They don't flee. A Hushed creature can be **woken** (healed of hushing) by a Hearkener's song or by greater restoration plus a DC 15 Performance check. That's an important hopeful mechanic: many enemies can be *saved*.
- **Hushed dead:** the dead of old greywilt events (the Gate War) are preserved and walking. Undead-adjacent, treated as undead for spells.

## How Mirrowen sees Eyrland
- A land of "the Tall Folk under the water," known from old songs. The Tall Folk once were friends. The stag Mossoran is a beloved legend. Tabaxi traders know a little of Eyrland's real cities.
- Brannoc's propaganda: the Tall Folk are stealing Mirrowen's song to light their lamps. **True,** and that's what makes him dangerous and sympathetic.

## Side-activity menu (Mirrowen)
- *Saffra:* **the Great Telling** (storytelling contest, perfect for Ash's Liars' Night skills), bell-market haggling, a rooftop chase race.
- *Horngard:* **the Drum Challenge**, **the Bull-Leap** (Acrobatics), ritual wrestling.
- *Tuskhold:* memory contests, the long-stone puzzle.
- *Otter-folk rafts:* swim races, otter-slide, fish-tickling.
- *Things worth stealing:* Brannoc's orders, the keys of the Quiet Pens, a stolen Warren Mothers' songbook.
