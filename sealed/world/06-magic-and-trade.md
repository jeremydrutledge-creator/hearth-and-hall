## Glimmer: how everyday magic works

- **Glimmerstone ("glimmer")**: pale blue-white crystal, cool to the touch, faintly humming. It glows when tapped and holds a simple enchantment for weeks.
- **Refined glimmer chips** power lamps (1 cp a chip, a month's light), heat-stones (5 cp), message-moths (1 cp a flight), cold-boxes (2 sp a month), and the charms of the enchanters' row.
- **Only the Enchanters' Guild may mine and refine glimmer** (city charter). Everyone else buys chips.
- **Prices are rising.** The Guild says the "deep springs" are running thin. (DM: they're over-mining for the Grand Reserve, and the seal is thinning so the crystal grows more slowly.)
- **Every enchanted trinket on the Row uses a glimmer core.** True permanent magic items (from adventuring, ancient forges, dwarves, elves) do *not*. That's a useful distinction later, when glimmer dies in Ending 1.

## The Enchanters' Row

**Where:** a narrow, glass-roofed lane in Glimmerway, about two hundred feet long, lined with tiny workshops. Glimmer sparks drift in the air. Tinkling chimes, the smell of hot glass and lavender oil. Commissions are taken at front counters; work is done in back.

**How commissions work:**
1. Pick a workshop and describe what you want.
2. The enchanter quotes a price and a time (usually 3 days to 3 weeks).
3. Pay half up front. Pick up and pay the rest.
4. Custom engraving or a message is free. Personalized gifts are a Row specialty.
5. Every Row item needs a Guild license stamp (a tiny lamp sigil). Unlicensed items are cheaper and illegal.

**Price guide:** per the **2024 DMG**, base prices by rarity are Common 100 gp, Uncommon 400 gp, Rare 4,000 gp, Very Rare 40,000 gp, Legendary 200,000 gp, with consumables at half. (Builder: verify against the 2024 DMG table in the shops step.) Row items are mostly **Common** custom wondrous items: small, delightful, personal. ## Wider trade
- **Exports of Emberford:** glimmer goods, enchanted trinkets, cloth, river trade.
- **From the north:** iron, copper, dwarven steel, gems.
- **From the Hobbland:** cider, wool, clockwork.
- **From Tollhaven:** money, credit, imported luxuries.
- **From Saltreach:** salt, fish, sea trade with the Salt Isles.
- **From Aranhollow:** almost nothing: rare herbs, elven wine once a decade.

## Common knowledge (player-safe)
- Glimmer powers lamps, heat-stones, message-moths and the Row's trinkets. The Enchanters' Guild owns all of it. Prices keep rising.
- Six magic shops worth knowing in Emberford: the Patient Owl (old books and scrolls; be patient), Brightwell & Daughters (rich people), Odd Lot (cheap and weird), Mother Brine's (potions, sailors), the Iron Kettle (dwarven weapons, no glimmer), the Clockwise (gnome gadgets).
- The Enchanters' Row will make you almost any small charm you can imagine, for a price.
