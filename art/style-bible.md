# Art Style Bible (locked)

**Style line — append to EVERY prompt:**

> Classic painted fantasy illustration in the manner of a 1980s adventure book cover: rich oil painting, visible brushwork, dramatic warm-and-cool lighting with deep shadows, painterly detail, period-accurate medieval clothing and materials, grounded and heroic rather than cartoonish, no text, no letters, no watermark, no border.

## Rules
- **No text in images.** Labels, room keys and names live in the text files. (Exception: none.)
- **Portraits:** chest-up or waist-up, 3/4 view, plain painterly background in the character's home colors, face lit clearly. Make a reference portrait first; every later image of that character passes the reference image back in.
- **Maps:** painted as an old illuminated map seen from above (region maps) or a high bird's-eye view (cities, interiors). No labels. Leave clear, distinct landmarks so the DM can describe "you are here." Wide compositions that crop well into quarters.
- **Interiors:** high three-quarter bird's-eye view, warm interior light, readable layout.
- **Creatures/villains:** full figure, in a setting, dramatic light. Never demonic imagery (no horns-and-hellfire look, no pentagrams, no hellscapes).
- **Palette for the home city:** warm stone, slate blue roofs, river green, and the pale blue glow of glimmer lamps.

## Models
- Default for portraits/scenes: Gemini Pro image model (best consistency and composition).
- Maps and anything precise: the Pro model.
- Cheaper Flash image model only for rough drafts.
