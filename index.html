    Hearth & Hall

    A village-building tale of timber, stone & stew.

    Your keep stands at the heart of a wild valley — deep forest west, rocky hills east, a pond south.

    Settlers haul every load back to the keep. It only holds so much — raise storehouses to grow.

    Tap a settler, then a tree, rock or berry — or use ⚑ Assign for a standing job. ⚒ Build opens the menu; craft 🏹 bows there before you can hunt.

    Set a 🚩 Rally on a forest or rocks and idle & new settlers gather there automatically.
